# Top 3 Product Candidates — April 2026

## Product 1: Bee Venom Skin Repair Cream
- **Format:** 60g cream, black/gold jar
- **COGS (all-in):** $7.90
- **Target Retail:** $39.99–$49.99
- **Margin:** $32.09–$42.09
- **Key Ingredients:** Bee venom, glucosamine, calendula, vitamin B2, hyaluronic acid, salicylic acid
- **Problem it solves:** Visible skin blemishes, dark spots, rough skin texture — things people see in the mirror and want gone NOW
- **Why we like it:** Most acute/visible problem of any product we found. "Bee venom" is a unique, curiosity-inducing mechanism that cold traffic hasn't been burned on. Dramatic before/after potential. Native ad storytelling writes itself. Maps perfectly to Invisible Woman avatar (women 40-55 self-conscious about visible skin imperfections). Consumable repeat purchase.
- **Key risk:** Must position as "skin repair/blemish care" — NEVER "mole removal" (medical claim = Meta ban + FTC risk). Need to verify product actually delivers visible results via sample order.

## Product 2: Whipped Beef Tallow & Honey Balm (120g)
- **Format:** 120g whipped balm, amber jar
- **COGS (all-in):** $17.01
- **Target Retail:** $49.99–$69.99
- **Margin:** $32.98–$52.98
- **Key Ingredients:** Grass-fed beef tallow, honey
- **Problem it solves:** Dry, irritated, sensitive skin from chemical-laden skincare. Wanting to simplify to one natural product.
- **Why we like it:** Rides the #1 fastest-growing product on Kalodata (EVIL GOODS, 318% growth). Carnivore/ancestral trend is massive. Zero education needed — "natural ingredients your grandmother used." Premium whipped texture justifies higher price. Consumable.
- **Key risk:** China sourcing vs "natural/ancestral/pure" brand story is a credibility conflict. EVIL GOODS specifically markets USA-sourced grass-fed tallow. Higher COGS limits margin room for Meta ads.

## Product 3: Butter Honey Body Cream (60g)
- **Format:** 60g cream, black/gold jar
- **COGS (all-in):** $7.67
- **Target Retail:** $39.99–$49.99
- **Margin:** $32.32–$42.32
- **Key Ingredients:** Tallow, glycerin, almond oil, beeswax, honey, potassium palmate
- **Problem it solves:** Same as Product 2 — dry/irritated skin, chemical skincare fatigue
- **Why we like it:** Same tallow trend as Product 2 but at LESS THAN HALF the COGS. Better margin math for Meta ad economics. Same brand story potential.
- **Key risk:** Same China sourcing concern. 60g is half the size of Product 2 — harder to justify premium pricing. More ingredients dilute the "simple/ancestral" story slightly.
