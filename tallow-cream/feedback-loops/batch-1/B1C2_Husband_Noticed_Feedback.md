## Step 1: Hypothesis

### What this ad did right

**1. The hook is the proven behavioral winner across two separate runs (HIGH CONFIDENCE)**

"My husband looked at me last week and said 'your skin looks different. What are you doing?' He never notices anything." This hook drove a 5.71% CTR (highest of the batch with statistical sample) AND a 100% LPV-to-ATC rate. Two LPVs both added to cart. That's not noise. That's the audience telling us this story makes them want to investigate the product. The B5C1 rewrite — same hook, densified — produced our first full-price purchase ($49.99). The hook works. Twice.

**2. External-witness frame is the right structure for the avatar (HIGH CONFIDENCE)**

The Invisible Woman's deepest pain isn't physical — it's being un-seen. The husband-notices opening inverts that pain in the first sentence. It promises the reader "someone will see you again." That promise is what drives the click and the ATC. This was the breakthrough insight that powered the entire B5 batch.

**3. CBO did vote for this ad — second-most spend in the batch (HIGH CONFIDENCE)**

$11.65 of campaign spend isn't massive but it's 4-10x more than the starved ads (B1C1, B1C3, B2C1, B2C3). Meta saw the engagement signal and started feeding it. That delivery decision is real signal — Meta's algorithm is willing to scale this creative if we can lower the auction price.

### What killed conversion

**1. $166 CPM means the auction is punishing the page/pixel/category, not the creative (HIGH CONFIDENCE)**

The clicks were strong, but the cost-per-impression was fatal. The same hook at $30 CPM would have delivered 5x the impressions for the same spend. The fact that the densified B5C1 also produced a high CPM ($228) tells us the CPM is upstream — pixel classification, page health, or product category — not the creative itself.

**2. Element density was 5/10 — predates the density rule (HIGH CONFIDENCE)**

Original B1C2 had: hook, inciting incident, low point, single secondary character (husband), product mention. Missing: trust ladder, multiple secondary characters, skeptic convert frame, escalating stakes beyond the husband moment, progressive transformation timeline. The B5C1 rewrite added all of these, which is why it converted at full price.

**3. Sample size on conversion is too thin to draw final conclusion (MEDIUM CONFIDENCE)**

2 LPV → 2 ATC is directionally amazing but it's 2 data points. We need 20+ LPVs at this LPV-to-ATC rate before we can call the funnel solved. The B5C1 rewrite gave us one purchase from one ATC — also too thin to call it a structural win on its own. What we have is consistent directional signal across two runs, not statistical proof.

**4. Compliance was borderline — body copy still references symptom language (MEDIUM CONFIDENCE)**

Original copy uses "my skin became someone else's" / "the dryness came first" / "the way my foundation would settle into lines I didn't have a year ago." These are not the worst offenders, but they sit close to the symptom-word strike list and the "your skin changed" diagnostic frame. The B5C1 rewrite moved most of this to functional descriptions and external observations, which likely contributed to slightly better delivery on B5C1.

## Step 2: Action — What Changes Going Forward

### Keep
- The hook — verbatim. "My husband looked at me / said 'your skin looks different' / He never notices anything." This three-beat opener is the highest-converting native-ad opener we have in the account.
- The external-witness structure as the primary frame for any Invisible Woman ad.
- The "absent-minded husband notices" specificity — it's the believable detail that makes the whole story work.

### Change
- Already done in B5C1: full 10-element density build, compliance pass, 6th-grade reading level, social-proof close instead of "if you're 50+" close.
- Going forward, every Husband Noticed variant must keep the FIRST THREE SENTENCES intact and only vary the body. The hook is locked.

### Test Next
- B5C1 is the active test. Watch its conversion data over the next 5-10 LPVs to see if the 100% LPV-to-ATC rate from B1C2 was real signal or a tiny-sample fluke.
- A future variant should keep the same hook but replace "husband" with a different witness (adult daughter, longtime friend, hairdresser) to test whether the witness identity matters or whether the witness ACT is what carries.

---

## Decision

Killed B1C2 correctly to give B5C1 a clean runway. B1C2's contribution to the project is structural: it identified the highest-leverage hook in our entire library and unlocked the densification thesis for B5. The hook itself lives on through B5C1. CPM problem cannot be fixed at the creative layer — it requires upstream investigation (pixel classification, fresh campaign, product naming).
