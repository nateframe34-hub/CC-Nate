**Ad Name:** B9C3 — The Counter With One Thing (One Jar)
**Batch:** 9 (Invisible Woman — Object-Discovery hook family)
**Angle:** Object-discovery — narrator notices ONE jar on a friend's near-empty bathroom counter at book club, contrasted with her own counter of fourteen products

## Step 1: Hypothesis

### What this ad proved

**1. The object-discovery hook converts on Day 1 (HIGH CONFIDENCE)**

Apr 14 was B9C3's first day of delivery and it converted at $10.82 spend, 4.62x gross ROAS. That's not a slow-burn ad finding its audience — that's an ad that worked the moment Meta showed it to the right people. The "one jar on a counter that used to have seven" image and the matching opening lines are doing fast pattern-recognition work in the feed.

**2. Conversion holds on fresh BM (HIGH CONFIDENCE)**

Apr 21: 5 clicks → 4 LPV → 2 ATC → 1 checkout → 1 purchase at full price. Same ad, completely different account environment, still converted. This rules out "B9C3 only worked because of a specific old-BM audience pocket." The ad itself carries the conversion mechanic.

**3. Highest engagement signal in the project library (HIGH CONFIDENCE)**

13.79% CTR on Apr 16 is the highest CTR logged in the entire tracker. 7.52-10.42% CTR across the other days. The hook is the most attention-getting in the project — the "one jar on an empty counter" image gives the visual recognition that the body then unpacks.

**4. Multi-character chain is a load-bearing structural choice (HIGH CONFIDENCE)**

Five named characters, each doing distinct work: Elaine (26-year friend, discovery + skeptic-convert bridge), Aunt Winnie (76-year-old beekeeper in Vermont, peer-adjacent authority via age and longevity), Sylvia (11-year coworker, workplace witness + social proof close), Lily (grown daughter, witness to the unexpected habit-cessation), husband (final close beat at outdoor dinner). The chain creates a credibility ladder — the narrator → Elaine → Winnie chain means the mechanism arrives via THREE women who tried it before the reader is asked to. No other converter in the project has this density.

**5. The "not-carrying" habit-cessation beat is unique and powerful (HIGH CONFIDENCE)**

Travel-size lotion packets in three places (purse, car, desk drawer), stopped buying, didn't notice she'd stopped. Different from B5C1's "not-checking front camera" and adds a NEW vector of habit extinction (carrying-as-coping, not vanity-as-coping). The Lily-asking-for-one beat makes it a relational discovery moment, not a self-discovery moment. This is the single most original line in the project library: "That was the thing I wanted back. Not the soft skin. The not-carrying."

**6. The husband close at an outdoor restaurant table works (MEDIUM CONFIDENCE)**

Different from the kitchen-table close in B5C1. The candle, the waiter three feet away, hand on her face — public-but-intimate setting. Adds variety to the husband-notice family of closes and proves the close mechanic isn't location-locked.

### What this ad did NOT prove

**1. Sustained day-over-day conversion (HIGH CONFIDENCE)**

Apr 16 had the project's highest CTR (13.79%) and zero ATCs. Conversion is bursty — Apr 14 yes, Apr 16 no, Apr 21 yes. Three data points across 7 days is not a 3-day sustained signal. We need to see if the Apr 14/Apr 21 pattern holds across consecutive days at sustained spend before calling this a winner.

**2. CPM efficiency (MEDIUM CONFIDENCE — confounded by CBO)**

$552 CPM on Apr 21 is ugly on the surface, but it's a CBO concentration effect — Meta poured 69% of the day's spend into the only converting ad. If B9C3 ran in isolation or in an ad set where budget were forced to distribute, its true CPM would likely be in the $200-300 range (consistent with old-BM behavior). Not yet measured cleanly.

**3. Whether the hook is replicable across other object-discovery scenes (UNKNOWN)**

The "one jar on an empty bathroom counter" image is specific. We don't know if the conversion comes from the visual specificity (this image, this counter) or the structural mechanic (any object-discovery beat in any setting). Hasn't been tested with a B9C3 v2.

**4. Whether Aunt Winnie is the load-bearing character (MEDIUM CONFIDENCE)**

Winnie is mentioned by name 7 times. She's the source of the original mechanism. She delivers the line about the fat structure. She's 76 with great skin and bees in Vermont. Without her, the mechanism arrives directly from Elaine — which would make it less credible. But we haven't tested a version without Winnie. Possible that Winnie IS the conversion mechanism here, not the object-discovery hook.

## Step 2: Action — What Changes Going Forward

### Keep

- **The hook image and opening lines** ("There was one thing on the counter. One jar. Amber glass. Simple label."). Locked. Highest CTR in the project.
- **The Aunt Winnie character.** 76-year-old beekeeper in Vermont who's been on tallow since 1987 is the most credible peer-adjacent authority anywhere in the library. Treat as an asset to potentially carry into other ads (different narrator, same Winnie figure as the discovery-source-of-source).
- **The "not-carrying" habit-cessation beat with the Lily reveal.** Single most original line in the project. Don't dilute by reusing in other ads — this stays as B9C3's signature.
- **The husband-at-restaurant-table close.** Adds variety to the husband-notice family. Don't replace.
- **The five-character chain.** Don't compress to 3 — the depth IS the credibility.

### Change

- **Move out of CBO-concentration risk.** Run B9C3 in its own ad set (or with a budget cap) so its CPM and conversion can be measured cleanly. Right now CBO concentration both saves it (gets all the budget) and distorts it (CPM looks worse than it is).
- **Write a feedback-loop entry after every 3rd run.** This ad doesn't have a feedback file before today and we lost analyzable signal as a result. Going forward: every 3 days of B9C3 spend → update this file.
- **Don't let it run in the same ad set as B7C1 long-term.** Both are Invisible Woman + object/photo discovery + 4-week transformation. CBO will always pick the most recent converter and starve the other. Separate ad sets.

### Test Next

- **B9C3 on fresh BM at $25-40/day for 5 consecutive days.** This is the real test. If 3 of 5 days produce a purchase at $20-30 CPA, this becomes a validated winner.
- **B9C3 v2 — different object, same mechanic.** Variant ideas:
  - "Bathroom drawer with one jar in it" (instead of empty counter)
  - "Friend's purse open on a table with one jar" (object-discovery in a non-bathroom setting)
  - "Friend's gym bag with one jar in the side pocket"
  Tests whether the object-discovery STRUCTURE is what converts or the specific empty-counter-with-one-jar image.
- **B9C3 v3 — Aunt Winnie isolation test.** Same hook (Elaine's counter) but the mechanism arrives directly from Elaine without Aunt Winnie. If conversion drops, Winnie is the load-bearing authority. If conversion holds, the chain depth was overkill and lighter ads can win.
- **Carry the "stopped buying X in three places" habit-cessation pattern into a new ad** (B12, B13, B14). Different product (e.g., "stopped buying drugstore sunscreen in three places"), same mechanic. Tests whether the not-carrying beat generalizes.

---

## Decision

**Protect, isolate, and budget-prioritize on fresh BM. This is the closest the project has come to a winner.**

Two full-price sales across two BM environments. Highest CTR in the project. Cleanest fresh-BM conversion shape. The five-character chain and the "not-carrying" beat are unique enough that this ad should be treated as a structural template — its DNA goes into B12/B13/B14 as the format to beat, not just a single ad to keep alive.

The immediate move: get B9C3 into its own ad set on fresh BM with a $25-40/day budget for 5 consecutive days. If sustained ROAS lands above 2x across at least 3 of those days, it crosses Nate's "winner" threshold and becomes the locked control ad against which all future creative is measured.
