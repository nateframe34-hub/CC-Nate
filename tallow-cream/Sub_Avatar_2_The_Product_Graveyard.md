## What Makes This Sub-Avatar Relatable

The "product graveyard" is one of the most universal experiences in skincare. The physical evidence — that drawer full of half-used products — is something almost every woman over 35 can picture in her own bathroom. When you name it in an ad, it triggers instant recognition AND instant shame (she knows she's wasted money).

The power is in the BEHAVIOR. She's still searching. She hasn't given up. That behavior — still watching reviews, still reading ingredient lists, still clicking on skincare ads — means she's reachable on Meta. She's exhausted but not done. That's the gap between the NMN "Burned Supplement Buyer" (who stopped buying) and this avatar (who keeps buying despite being burned).

## The Gap

She wants something that works (desire), she's tried everything mainstream skincare offers (experience), she's exhausted from repeated disappointment (emotion), but she keeps searching and buying (behavior). The gap is: **every product she's tried is a VARIATION of the same approach (synthetic/water-based formulations). She needs a fundamentally DIFFERENT type of product — not a better version of what she already tried.**

Tallow IS that different thing. It's not another serum, cream, or lotion. It's animal fat. It's so different it sounds wrong — which is exactly why it might work when nothing else did.

## Angles & Hooks

**Reason to Buy:** You've tried every serum, cream, and lotion the beauty industry makes. None of them worked because they're all built the same way. This is built completely differently — it's the fat your skin is actually made of.

**Hook 1:** "I have a drawer full of skincare products that didn't work. Then I tried cow fat."
**Hook 2:** "Retinol. Hyaluronic acid. Ceramides. Vitamin C. None of them. Then this."
**Hook 3:** "The ugliest product in my bathroom is the only one that works."

*Why this works:* The "product list → unexpected solution" pattern is a proven native format. The contrast between sophisticated skincare ingredients and "cow fat" IS the pattern interrupt. It's the "I put cow fat on my face" discovery story that our research identified as the #1 tallow hook.

### Angle 2: Your Skin Doesn't Need More Products. It Needs Fewer Ingredients.
**Reason to Buy:** You've been layering 5-10 products because each one does one thing poorly. This does everything because it matches what your skin is actually made of. 4 ingredients. That's it.

**Hook 1:** "10 products, 47 ingredients, $200/month. Or this. 4 ingredients. $50."
**Hook 2:** "I threw away everything under my bathroom sink. Replaced it with one jar."

### Angle 3: You Haven't Tried Everything. You've Tried Every Version Of The Same Thing.
**Reason to Buy:** Every cream, serum, and lotion you've bought is water-based with synthetic ingredients. You haven't actually tried something DIFFERENT — until now.

**Hook 1:** "You've tried 20 products. They were all the same product."
**Hook 2:** "What if every skincare product you've ever bought was built wrong?"
**Hook 3:** "Same base. Same fillers. Different label. That's the skincare industry."

*Why this works:* This is the NEW INFORMATION strategy from Origins. It reframes her entire product experience — she thinks she's tried everything, but she's actually tried the same CATEGORY of product 20 times. This gives her new hope without insulting her intelligence.
