## Section A: Ladder cleanly under v2 (no change needed)

These were arguably already operating on the new Core Desire — the v1 framing mis-labeled what was actually working. The Voice-of-Customer council agent was right that these wins were about agency/relief/competence, not external validation.

### 1. One-Jar Discovery (B9C3) — ✅ CLEANEST FIT
- **v1 surface desire:** "I want what my friend clearly has"
- **v2 surface desire:** "I want one thing on my counter, not fourteen"
- **Why it ladders cleanly:** Identity payoff is "Not the soft skin. The not-carrying." That's pure agency/competence — she's no longer fighting her skin's daily logistics. The discovery character (Elaine's near-empty counter) is the visible proof that someone stopped fighting and it worked.
- **Close treatment:** Husband at outdoor restaurant table appears in current copy but functions as confirmation, not requirement. The ad would still work if the close shifted to "I haven't dug for a lotion packet in eleven weeks." No copy change needed for next iteration; close-swap as optional B16+ test.

### 2. Sebum-Anger (B3C2) — ✅ CLEANEST FIT
- **v1 surface desire:** "I want to understand why nothing has worked"
- **v2 surface desire:** "I want to stop pouring water on an oil problem"
- **Why it ladders cleanly:** Identity payoff is "Stop refilling the bucket. Get the right bucket." That's pure self-orientation. The intellectual-anger register IS anger at the skin's refusal to cooperate, expressed through anger at the system that misled her. Direct-to-reader CTA already.
- **Close treatment:** No change. The narrator's vindication IS the close.

### 3. Daughter Sent It (B12C3) — ✅ CLEANEST FIT
- **v1 surface desire:** "I want to be seen by my daughter"
- **v2 surface desire:** "I want to stop fighting my skin so my daughter doesn't have to watch me fight it"
- **Why it ladders cleanly:** The gift framing means the daughter is the *discovery channel*, not the *validation source*. The ad works because mom's fight with her skin became visible to her daughter, who acted on it. The cooperation arrives via the gift — not because the daughter approves of how mom looks.
- **Close treatment:** Mother-daughter close stays. Possibly stronger under v2 because the relief is shared.

### 4. Retinol Burnout (B14 batch) — ✅ CLEANEST FIT (and possibly strongest under v2)
- **v1 surface desire:** "I want to be seen + stop being failed by treatments"
- **v2 surface desire:** "I want to stop fighting my skin AND stop fighting the products that were supposed to help"
- **Why it ladders cleanly:** Retinol IS the avatar's enemy. The skin AND the product are antagonists. Habit cessation is "stopped flinching before applying anything." Pure adversary-resolution framing. This sub-avatar may be the single best fit for v2.
- **Close treatment:** No change. "Trust yourself" identity payoff already maps to v2.

## Section B: Ladder under v2, but close-swap recommended

These sub-avatars currently close on external validation (husband, professional, sister, friends seeing the change). Under v2, that close becomes optional — and the council suggests it may be a weaker close than self-orientation. The existing ad copy stays as the v1 control. New ads under these sub-avatars test the close-swap.

### 5. Husband-Noticed (B5C1) — 🟡 CLOSE-SWAP TEST PRIORITY
- **v1 surface desire:** "I want my husband to look at me the way he used to"
- **v2 surface desire:** "I want my skin to stop being the third person in the room"
- **Why the swap matters:** Current close is "It's the Tuesday. It's the moment someone who's been looking at your face for twenty-six years looks up and notices something changed." Under v2, the deeper relief is that the SKIN stopped being the wall between her and the people she lives with — the husband's noticing is the *evidence* of that wall coming down, not the *goal*.
- **Recommended close-swap test:** Replace the husband-as-validator close with a self-orientation close ("the morning I caught myself making coffee without checking the mirror") OR the mirror close ("the Saturday I looked in the bathroom mirror and didn't catalogue what was wrong"). Test in B16 against B5C1 as-is.
- **Status:** This is the #1 priority close-swap test (B5C1 is the most validated converter, biggest learning opportunity).

### 6. Mother's Photo (B7C1) — 🟡 CLOSE-SWAP TEST PRIORITY
- **v1 surface desire:** "I want skin like my mother's was at this age"
- **v2 surface desire:** "I want what my mother had — skin that didn't fight her"
- **Why the swap matters:** The current close is mother-on-the-nightstand (heritage validation). Under v2, the stronger close is the narrator REACHING the cooperation her mother had ("I had a normal morning. Like she used to have."). The mother becomes the proof-of-concept that cooperation is possible, not the validator that the narrator has earned it.
- **Recommended close-swap test:** Self-orientation close ("the first morning I didn't think about my face") with the mother-on-the-nightstand kept as the IMAGE but not the validating beat.
- **Status:** Secondary priority. Test in B17 after B16 data.

### 7. Professional Witness / Hairdresser (B12C1) — 🟡 CLOSE-SWAP RECOMMENDED
- **v1 surface desire:** "I want Renee to look at me and SEE something different"
- **v2 surface desire:** "I want to sit in Renee's chair without flinching at the salon lights"
- **Why the swap matters:** The professional-noticing close is structurally identical to husband-noticing. The salon chair / fluorescents motif could close on self-orientation — the moment she stopped dreading the lights — without losing the avatar's specificity.
- **Status:** Tertiary priority. Not active in pipeline.

### 8. Sister Visit (B15C1) — 🟡 CLOSE-SWAP RECOMMENDED
- **v1 surface desire:** "I want my sister not to be alarmed by my face"
- **v2 surface desire:** "I want to stop being on the defensive when my sister visits"
- **Why the swap matters:** The "look like yourself again" close from Lisa is external validation. Under v2, the relief is that the narrator stopped FIGHTING her face long enough that Lisa's visit became unremarkable. Sister visit can stay as the hook (it's a strong urgency anchor) but the close should ground in the narrator's own experience.
- **Status:** Written but not launched. B16 could swap this close before launch.

### 9. Group Text (B15C2) — 🟡 CLOSE-SWAP RECOMMENDED
- **v1 surface desire:** "I want to send the selfie without bracing"
- **v2 surface desire:** "I want to wake up not bracing"
- **Why the swap matters:** Heaviest external-validation close in the roster (three friends seeing her). Under v2, the deeper relief is that the bracing-to-take-a-selfie was a tax her skin extracted from her every Sunday morning, and the tax stopped. The selfie can stay as the close mirror image — but the identity payoff is "not-bracing," not "being seen."
- **Status:** Written but not launched. Close-swap before launch is a low-risk improvement.

## Section C: Significant re-frame needed

### 10. Photo Avoider (B13 batch) — 🟠 STRUCTURAL RE-FRAME
- **v1 surface desire:** "I want to be in the family photos again"
- **v2 surface desire:** "I want to stop calculating which side of my face is up before every photo"
- **Why this is harder than a close-swap:** The whole avatar is built around external-visibility (literally being photographed). It's the most external-validation-dependent sub-avatar on the roster.
- **The v2 re-frame:** The conflict isn't "I'm not in the photos" — it's "every photo has been an EFFORT, and I'm tired of doing the effort." The avatar wakes up tired of the daily, recurring photo-management labor. The close isn't "I'm in the photos again" — it's "I stopped doing the math before every photo."
- **Three B13 ads (Camera Roll, Christmas Card, Behind The Camera) all close on external visibility.** Under v2, they need re-framing toward the labor-of-managing-being-photographed.
- **Status:** Written but not launched. Decision needed: rewrite or hold. Recommendation: hold for now, address in a later batch. B16 should not include Photo Avoider variants.

## Section D: Mother-figure reframe — ladders cleaner under v2

### 11. Old Notebook (B15C3) — 🟢 LADDERS CLEANER UNDER v2 THAN v1
- **v1 surface desire:** "I want to inherit my mother's wisdom about skin"
- **v2 surface desire:** "I want what was on the page in the notebook — skin that didn't fight her"
- **Why v2 fits BETTER than v1:** The 1979 notebook has six lines including "wash face evening, Pond's cold cream, two fingers." The whole artifact is documentary evidence that the narrator's mother had a *cooperative relationship with her own skin*. The whole point of the find IS the v2 Core Desire made concrete. The husband's "you look like your mom" close is fine but the more powerful close is the narrator herself: "I had what was on the page. I had what she had."
- **Status:** Written but not launched. Recommendation: small close-tweak before launch (move emphasis from "look like your mom" to "had what was on the page"). Not a full rewrite.
