## How to Use This Roster

When writing a new ad:

1. Identify which existing Sub-Avatar the ad belongs to. If it doesn't fit any of the 11, the ad is either (a) a new Sub-Avatar that needs its own file before writing copy, or (b) outside the Invisible Woman Core Desire (which means it should not be written at this scale).
2. Read the Sub-Avatar file in full before writing — every Sub-Avatar has specific verbatim language, image triggers, and structural constraints that the ad must respect.
3. If you're writing a hypothesis-test variant within an existing Sub-Avatar (like B6's three variants under Husband-Noticed, or B9's three variants under One-Jar-Discovery), add the new ad to that Sub-Avatar's "Ads Built For This Sub-Avatar" table after writing. Note what variable the new ad isolates.
4. For Sub-Avatars 5-11 (unvalidated), the "Notes" section enumerates what we expect to learn. After launch, update that section with what was actually learned.

## Pattern Across the Converters (Sub-Avatars 1-4)

What the four validated converters share, per `Converter_Pattern_Synthesis.md`:

- Solution-aware avatar (not problem-aware)
- Accidental discovery (never "I went looking")
- Peer-adjacent (not medical) authority
- Water-vs-oil mechanism at the 50-60% body position
- Progressive day-marker transformation (Day 1, Day 4-5, Day 9-10, Day 14, Day 21)
- Habit-cessation beat (3 of 4 — B3C2 missing)
- Husband-notice close (3 of 4 — B3C2 ends on a direct-to-reader CTA instead)
- Skeptic-convert frame ("I made the face you're making")
- Compliance posture (zero menopause, no symptom strike-list, no shame framing)

The unvalidated Sub-Avatars (5-11) all build on this skeleton with one or more deliberately tested variations. Each Sub-Avatar file documents which variations its ads are testing and what we expect to learn.
