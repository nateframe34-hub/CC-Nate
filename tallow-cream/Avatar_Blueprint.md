## Understanding the Product First (Origins Rule: Know what it DOES, not what it IS)

### Physical Product
- 120g whipped balm in amber jar
- 4 ingredients: grass-fed beef tallow, manuka honey, olive oil, calendula flower extract
- Face + body moisturizer
- 3-year shelf life

### Functional Product (What it DOES for people)
- Replaces sebum the body stops producing (especially post-menopause — sebum drops up to 60%)
- Deeply moisturizes dry, cracked, irritated skin in a way water-based lotions can't
- Simplifies skincare from 10+ products down to 1 jar
- Soothes eczema, psoriasis, KP, and damaged skin barriers (strongest testimonial evidence)
- Provides a "clean label" alternative to chemical-laden skincare products
- Creates a ritual of self-care that feels indulgent, not clinical

### Product Mechanism
**HOW it works:** Tallow's fatty acid profile is ~87% similar to human sebum. It doesn't sit on top of skin like water-based lotions — it absorbs because your skin recognizes it as its own. The manuka honey adds antibacterial + healing properties. The calendula soothes inflammation.

**Why this mechanism matters:** In a Stage 3-4 sophisticated skincare market, "deeply moisturizes" is a dead claim. But "your skin recognizes tallow as its own sebum" is a NEW MECHANISM for most cold traffic. They've never heard this before. It gives them NEW HOPE that this will work when everything else didn't.

## Desire Evaluation

Before building the avatar, we evaluate desire clusters from our research using Spencer's formula: **Urgency × Staying Power × Scope**.

| Desire Cluster | Core Desire | Urgency | Staying Power | Scope | Evaluation |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Skin Restoration** ("I want my skin to feel normal again") | Health | HIGH — daily discomfort, visible | PERMANENT — worsens with age/menopause | MASSIVE — every woman 40+ experiences skin changes | **STRONGEST** |
| Chemical-Free Skincare ("I want to stop putting chemicals on my skin") | Control / Health | MODERATE — fear-based, not pain-based | PERMANENT — growing awareness | LARGE — clean beauty is mainstream | Strong but fear, not pain |
| Simplification ("I want ONE product, not ten") | Comfort | MODERATE — frustration, not urgency | PERMANENT — routine fatigue grows | LARGE — anyone with a skincare routine | Strong secondary |
| Skin Condition Relief (eczema, KP, psoriasis) | Health | VERY HIGH — daily suffering | PERMANENT — chronic conditions | MODERATE — ~15-30% of adults | Strong but narrower scope |
| Anti-Aging / Wrinkle Reduction | Status / Health | MODERATE | PERMANENT | MASSIVE | Crowded, every brand claims this |

**Winner: Skin Restoration.**

It scores highest across all three criteria. It's felt DAILY (urgency), gets worse every year and accelerates dramatically at menopause (staying power), and affects virtually every woman over 40 (scope).

The research reveals the desire language is restoration-focused, not improvement-focused:

- "My skin just... changed. Nothing I used to use works anymore."
- "I want my skin to feel like it used to"
- "I tried everything — retinol, hyaluronic acid, ceramides — nothing actually fixes the dryness"
- "My skin barrier is destroyed"
- "I woke up and my skin was someone else's"

This maps to **Health** (#1 on the 6 Mass Instincts hierarchy) — the same instinct level as NMN, but with a VISIBLE, TANGIBLE product that delivers results you can SEE and FEEL.

## CORE AVATAR

### Desire (Category 1)

**"I want my skin to feel like it used to."**

This is the single surface-level desire that anchors the entire avatar system. It captures:
- Moisture restoration ("I want my skin to stop being dry and tight")
- Barrier repair ("I want products to stop irritating my skin")
- Texture return ("I want soft, smooth skin again — not papery/crepey")
- Confidence restoration ("I want to stop thinking about my skin every time I look in the mirror")

**Check:** Does it start with "I want"? → Yes.
**Check:** Can you connect it to a core desire? → Yes — **Health** (#1 on the 6 Mass Instincts).

**Why this works as the Core Avatar desire:**
- It's surface-level (not "I want to be healthy" which is too broad)
- It's specific enough to be relatable ("like it used to" implies something was LOST)
- It's broad enough to encompass multiple sub-groups (menopause, eczema, chemical damage, aging)
- It implies DECLINE (something changed) which creates urgency
- It implies RESTORATION (something can be recovered) which creates hope
- The product can ACTUALLY deliver on this claim — tallow genuinely works for dry/damaged skin

**What it is NOT:**
- It's not "I want younger-looking skin" (that's anti-aging/status — crowded, overpromised)
- It's not "I want clean skincare" (that's control/fear — weaker purchase driver)
- It's not "I want to simplify my routine" (that's comfort — too rational, not emotional enough)
- It's not "I want to cure my eczema" (that's a medical claim — Meta will flag it)
