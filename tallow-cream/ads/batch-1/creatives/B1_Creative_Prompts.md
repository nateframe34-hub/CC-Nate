# Batch 1 Creative Prompts — The Invisible Woman

> **Format:** Native/camouflage static. Must NOT look like an ad. Should look like a photo someone posted on Facebook with their story. No logos, no text overlays, no product branding visible. Just a real-looking photo.

---

## B1C1 — "Nobody Warned Me" (Desert dry in one year)

**Primary Image:**
```
Photorealistic candid photograph of a woman in her late 40s sitting on the edge of her bed in early morning light, wearing a simple gray t-shirt, gently touching her cheek with her fingertips while looking at a small handheld mirror with a slightly concerned, thoughtful expression. Messy morning hair, no makeup, natural skin texture with visible fine lines. Soft warm light from a window to the left. The bedroom is real and lived-in, not staged. Muted warm tones, cream bedding, natural wood nightstand. Shot looks like it was taken by a partner or friend, not a photographer. Slightly imperfect framing. iPhone quality, not studio quality. Candid, intimate, real. No product visible.
```

**Alternate Image:**
```
Photorealistic photograph of a woman's bathroom counter from her perspective, looking down. Multiple skincare products scattered — a few bottles, a tube of cream, a serum dropper. Her hands are visible at the edges of frame resting on the counter. Warm bathroom light. The products look used, some half empty, caps slightly askew. This looks like a photo someone took of their own bathroom to post in a Facebook group. Not styled, not curated. Real life. iPhone photo quality.
```

## General Notes for All B1 Creatives

- **NO text on the images.** The body copy IS the ad. The image is just the scroll-stopper.
- **NO product branding visible.** If the product appears, it should look like something sitting on a real bathroom counter, not a product photo.
- **Imperfect framing is better.** Slightly off-center, slightly tilted. It needs to look like a phone photo, not a studio shoot.
- **Skin must look REAL.** Fine lines, texture, natural color variation. If the AI generates porcelain-smooth skin, regenerate. Our avatar is 45-55. She needs to see someone who looks like her, not a retouched model.
- **Warm tones across all three.** Golden morning light, cream/honey color palette. No cool/blue tones.
- **Generate 3-4 variations of each** and pick the one that looks most like a real Facebook photo.
