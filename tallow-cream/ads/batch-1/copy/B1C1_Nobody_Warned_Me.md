## Body Copy

Hot flashes? Expected.
Night sweats? Sure.
Mood swings? Okay.

========

It started around 47.

My moisturizer just… stopped working. The same one I'd used for 12 years.

I thought it was the formula. Maybe they changed it.

So I bought a new one. Then another. Then another.

CeraVe. Neutrogena. That $80 serum my coworker swore by.

Nothing. My skin would drink it in 30 minutes and feel tight and dry again by lunch.

I started layering. Serum, then cream, then oil on top. Three products just to get through the morning.

By 2pm I'd be in the bathroom reapplying like it was chapstick.

========

But the worst part wasn't the dryness.

It was catching my reflection in the bathroom at work and thinking "who is that?"

My skin looked tired. Not old. Tired.

Like it had given up.

I stopped taking selfies.

Because I don't look like me anymore, baby.

"It's just aging," she said.
