# B22C3 - Trend Graphic: The 76-Year-Old Beekeeper

**Batch:** 22 (format test, new isolated campaign replacing whiteboard's budget)
**Cell:** C3 of 3
**Format:** Same produced badge-graphic format as C1/C2 (format held constant), badge swapped to "VIRAL" instead of "BREAKING NEWS" since this beat is a trend/character story, not a mechanism report.
**Ported element:** The named hyper-specific authority. Per `Converter_Pattern_Synthesis_v2.md`: Winnie, 76, Vermont, keeps bees, "I've been using the same thing since 1987. Four ingredients. I bought my first one by accident at a farmer's market in Brattleboro." Never tried in a cheap format before.

**Why C3 changed (2026-07-02):** original C3 concept (husband-notice witness close) was scrapped by founder: a "BREAKING NEWS" badge on a private romantic moment doesn't make logical sense, nobody reports on someone's dating life. The badge-graphic format only works when the claim is plausibly a trend or something spreading, which the witness beat isn't. Named authority is: a specific, quotable, oddly credible person having her routine "go viral" is a real trend-story shape, same logic as the founder's own dog-supplement reference ad ("dog owners are flooding Facebook"). Witness-close is parked, not deleted, it may belong in a future screenshot/text-message format instead, per the original synthesis doc's own suggestion.

**Compliance note:** Aesthetic only, no claim of actual broadcast/endorsement, no real network branding, no claim of a specific virality metric (no fake "10 million views," just the framing device).
