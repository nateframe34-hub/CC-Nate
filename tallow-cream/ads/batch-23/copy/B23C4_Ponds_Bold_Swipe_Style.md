# B23C4 - She Used Pond's: Bold Swipe-Style Image (Camouflage Deliberately Loosened)

**Batch:** 23, Cell 4. Same copy as B23C2, a second image variant to run alongside it, not a replacement.
**Base copy:** Identical to B23C2 (hook resequenced, CTA bullet block, P.S. close). See that file for the full body copy, unchanged here.
**Test variable:** Image only. Same purpose as B23C3: push past B23C2's subtle candid face-reveal toward the swipe file's brighter, more produced lifestyle-photo register (closer to Ad #5's warm selfie-with-product shot), explicitly accepting camouflage loss in exchange for maximum scroll-stop and the lowest possible CPM. Run alongside B23C2, not instead of it.

## Why this specific moment

Uses the photo-discovery beat that opens the ad ("She was 52 in that photo. I'm 52 now.") combined with the product itself, similar in spirit to Ad #5's smiling-selfie-plus-product-texture composition. Unlike B23C2's understated sister-at-the-door image, this version puts a genuine, warm, camera-facing smile front and center.

## Image Prompt

```
A bright, warm photograph of a woman in her early 50s sitting at a kitchen table in soft morning light, genuinely smiling directly at the camera, holding an old photograph in one hand (a faded 1990s snapshot, visible but not the focus) and a small amber glass jar of balm in the other, both objects held up slightly like she's comparing them. Her expression is warm, a little emotional, a real smile with visible laugh lines, not a stiff posed grin. The kitchen background is soft and lived-in, a mug of coffee, morning light through a window, softly out of focus. Color grade is warm and elevated, closer to a well-shot lifestyle campaign photo than a raw grainy phone snapshot, while the moment itself still reads as genuine rather than stiffly staged. No text overlays, no captions, no logos, no legible label text on the jar. 4:5 aspect ratio, 1080x1350.
```

**Aspect ratio:** 4:5 (1080 x 1350)
**Delivery target:** Meta, US only

## What this deliberately gives up

Same tradeoff as B23C3: a warmer, better-lit, more composed photo than the account's usual candid standard, still plausible as something real but noticeably more produced. Product is visible here (unlike B23C3), so the label must stay unreadable, this is still a native ad, not a produced PDP-style shot, the camouflage loosening is about photographic polish and a warm direct-camera smile, not about becoming an overt product ad.

## Compliance check

No new claims, no health/medical language, zero em dashes, label kept unreadable to avoid reading as a branded product shot.

## Why this cell

Completes the same 3-point curve as B23C3 for the second proven ad (faceless baseline -> B23C2's candid face -> this cell's produced/bold face), so both proven ads get the same structured comparison rather than only one being tested at the bold end.
