## Format-fit lens (why the ranking below is ordered the way it is)

The B22 chyron format is a **single-frame, blunt-declarative, information-promise** format. A news badge primes the reader to expect a fact or reveal stated flatly, not a story that needs paragraphs to land. That means:

- **Best fit:** angles that are already a complete, punchy claim or stat on their own (a comparison, a number, a blunt confrontation, a reveal). These read as a real "headline" without any narrative scaffolding.
- **Worst fit:** angles that are actually CLOSES, identity beats, or moods that require earned context to land. `Brand_Mastery.md` already documents this exact failure mode: SA3 Angle 1 is flagged as "a CLOSE that converts, and per B22C3's failure, a poor single-frame hook," B22C3 died specifically from a news-badge/private-moment mismatch. Any angle with that same shape inherits that risk.

## Ranking rationale, short version

**Top tier (1-4): stat/comparison/blunt-claim angles.** These are already headline-shaped: a number, a contrast, or a flat confrontation. No narrative scaffolding needed, which is exactly what a single-frame badge format can carry. Confrontation ranks #1 specifically because `B25_Concept_Plan.md` already flagged it as the strongest fit, and its blunt-statement psychology is the literal definition of a news chyron.

**Mid tier (5-9): reveal/mechanism/identity angles that still work as a one-line headline but need slightly more setup than a pure stat.** SA1A1 promoted to lead has strong verbatim-validated language and reads as a real reveal, but it's a personal-experience reveal rather than a pure fact, a small step down from pure information angles. The guarantee/last-first-try angle (#9) reads more like an offer callout than a "breaking news" fact, which is why it's mid-lower despite being a strong angle in other formats.

**Bottom tier (10-14): identity, mood, and close-shaped angles.** These need earned context (a story, a relationship, a lived pattern) to land, exactly the shape `Brand_Mastery.md` already flags as risky for this format (SA3's documented "poor single-frame hook" note, and B22C3's actual documented failure). SA6 ranks last for a second reason on top of format mismatch: it's explicitly parked pending primary research, not ready regardless of format.
