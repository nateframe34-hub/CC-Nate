# B25 CONCEPT PLAN (planning stage, not yet a launch brief)

**Date started:** 2026-07-07 (founder request)
**Concept:** News-headline format ads (the B22 chyron/badge family) routed into a long-form presell destination (advertorial or listicle). The ad's only job is the cheap scroll-stop and the click; the destination carries the desire ladder.

---

## 1) Why this batch exists (the strategic logic)

B22's post-mortem logic, stated plainly: the news-headline format is the cheapest delivery the account has ever produced (B22C1 ran $47-52 CPM pre-presell, vs $200+ historical native median), and it even generated ATCs and a checkout-initiated on pocket-change spend. Its diagnosed weakness was never the format, it was that a short chyron ad can't carry the full desire ladder the way native's 1,500-word copy does, so clicks arrived shallow and didn't convert.

That diagnosis implies its own fix: keep the format's cheap click, move the selling job to a page. B22C1's presell pilot (running now) and B24 (launched 2026-07-07 into the same campaign) are both partial tests of this. B25 is the full, deliberate version: news-headline creatives built FOR a presell destination from day one, headlines written as article teases rather than product claims.

## 2) Brand_Mastery fields (mandatory per Part 8, to be finalized per cell)

Market context: Stage 5 market / Stage 3-4 tallow niche. The news format is inherently a **New Information** delivery vehicle (a "news" visual promises information, not a product pitch), which is why it pairs naturally with Problem Aware targets. Every candidate angle below states its fields.

## 3) Candidate angles (draft, founder to react)

**A. Confrontation (parked from B24, this is its natural home): "$80 MOISTURIZER. 70% WATER. STILL TIRED SKIN."**
- Sub-avatar: SA2+SA5 Graveyard/Label-Reader. Awareness: Solution Aware. Response: New Mechanism + blunt New Information.
- Why here: the blunt-statement psychology that made Confrontation risky as a quote-card is exactly what a news-badge format rewards, news graphics ARE blunt declarative statements. This is the strongest format-angle fit of anything parked.

**B. Mechanism re-run with tease headline: the proven water-vs-fat reveal, headline rewritten as an article tease.**
- Sub-avatar: SA1/SA2 shared. Awareness: Problem Aware. Response: New Information.
- B22C1's mechanism cell is the only news-format cell that ever produced funnel movement. Re-running it with a destination-first headline ("The one ingredient every failed cream shares" style, tease not reveal) is the closest thing to a validated cell in this batch.

**C. Tired-Looking news tease: "'YOU LOOK TIRED' HAS A CAUSE. IT ISN'T SLEEP."**
- Sub-avatar: SA1. Awareness: Problem Aware. Response: New Information.
- The validated anchor sub-avatar in the account's proven awareness slot, dressed in the format. If B24's quote-card V1 wins its read, this becomes the same angle tested across a second format (clean format-vs-format comparison, one variable).

## 4) The destination question (advertorial vs listicle): recommendation

**Recommendation: listicle, built new.** Reasoning:

1. **The advertorial destination is already being tested.** B24 (3 creatives) and B22C1 both route to the advertorial right now. A third batch into the same page adds spend, not information. A listicle gives us a genuinely untested destination format.
2. **The listicle suits the news format's psychology.** A news-style ad promises information; a listicle ("5 reasons women over 40 are throwing out their moisturizer" family) delivers information in the exact shape the click expected. Advertorial = story promise, listicle = information promise. News-headline ads make an information promise.
3. **The Javvy-style listicle is a proven presell pattern** from the swipe file (founder-referenced), with inline blue text CTAs, which we've already built the styling and tracking pattern for on the advertorial.
4. **Hedge:** if B24's advertorial read (first purchases or clear ATC signal) comes back strong before B25 finishes production, reconsider, a proven page beats an untested one. This decision can stay open until renders are done; the ads themselves don't change either way, only the link.

## 5) Structure and testing method

- **3:2:2 again** (3 creatives from the angle list above, 2 primary texts, 2 headlines), Marksman logic, images only.
- **SUPERSEDED 2026-07-07 (founder decision):** each cell routes to its OWN fully personalized listicle, not one shared destination. Founder's reasoning: this batch is still testing angles end to end, and maximizing ad-to-page congruence raises the odds a genuine winning angle actually shows through instead of being suppressed by a mismatched page, that outweighs the cleaner isolated-variable read a shared page would give. Per-creative UTMs still apply. See `tallow-cream/store/theme/sections/tallow-listicle-confrontation.liquid`, `tallow-listicle-labelcount.liquid`, `tallow-listicle-readaloud.liquid`.
- Listicle page gets the same UTM-passthrough + per-position CTA tracking script as the advertorial (lift it wholesale from `tallow-advertorial.liquid`).

## 6) Format spec (locked from B22's corrected version)

Sharp, produced product/lifestyle photo (NOT grainy TV screenshot), bold badge tag (generic, no real outlet names ever), bold all-caps headline overlay with a yellow key-phrase highlight, optional inset circle callout for a proof graphic. Faces: B22C1's render had a face and delivered at the account's cheapest CPM ever, while B23's faces died, the difference is plausibly produced-ad context vs quote-card context. Carry the face question as an explicit variable to watch, not a rule either way.

## 7) Compliance (same as B22)

Aesthetic of a news graphic only. No real outlet names, call letters, or logos. Nothing claims actual coverage. Generic/fictional badges only. No banned phrases, no medical claims. Listicle page carries the same advertisement-disclosure footer as the advertorial.

## 8) Sequencing (proposed, founder to confirm)

B24 just launched into the B22 campaign (2026-07-07). Proposed gate: **hold B25 launch until B24 has its first real read** (a few days of delivery, per the standing "decisions on purchases only" rule), because B25's destination decision (advertorial vs listicle) is directly informed by whether B24's advertorial converts. Production (copy, renders, listicle page build) can proceed in parallel now; only the launch waits.
