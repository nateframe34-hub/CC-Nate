**Angle:** SA2 Angle 1 lead + Angle 2 mechanism at 55%
**Awareness:** Solution-Aware · **Sophistication:** Stage 3-4 market, Stage 3 response
**Character type:** Peer + Skeptic Convert
**Hook format:** Message-not-yet-sent
**Door:** *I was too embarrassed to ask for over a year*

---

## Why this cell exists

Same format as C1, deliberately different door. C1 enters on **another woman's
answer** — discovery. C2 enters on **the narrator's own hesitation** — shame.
Per the concept framework these pull different segments: C1 catches the woman
who is curious, C2 catches the woman who has been quietly comparing herself to
someone and has never said so out loud.

Running the same format twice is only justified if the doors genuinely differ.
These do.

**What it isolates:** whether the shame entry outperforms the discovery entry
inside an identical format. If C1 and C2 land close together, the format is
doing the work and the door is not — which is itself worth knowing before we
build B35.

**Structural note:** this hook adds one short beat to the front of the B17C1
architecture (the year of not asking) and changes nothing else. The graveyard,
chain, mechanism, timeline and cessation beats are untouched.

## Selected hook

> I typed this message four times over about a year and deleted it every time.
>
> *"Random question and please don't think I'm weird. What's the one jar in your
> bathroom?"*
>
> I've been in that bathroom a hundred times. Eighteen months ago the counter had
> seven things on it. Now there's one, and I have fourteen, and I could not make
> myself hit send.

**Why it works:** the loop is the reader's to close — she does not know what the
jar is, and neither does the narrator. The self-selection is sharper than C1's
because it runs on an admission rather than an observation: only a woman who has
privately compared her own bathroom to someone else's recognises herself here.
"Over about a year" is the specific that certifies it; nobody inventing this
detail chooses a year. No product, no benefit, no category.
