while the proven copy is held fixed? This is the cleanest version of the

## Selected hook

> I typed it four times before I sent it.
>
> *"Random question. What's the one jar in your bathroom?"*
>
> I'd been in that bathroom a hundred times. Eighteen months ago the counter had
> seven things on it. Last week there was one.
>
> She wrote back with a photo and one line: *"I wondered when you'd ask."*

**Why it works:** no product, no benefit, no category — the reader cannot tell
what is being sold. Self-selection runs entirely on recognition of a crowded
counter. The loop is built by the reader out of her own bathroom, which is the
exact mechanic that makes B17C1's opening work: the sentence has no content
until she supplies it. "Typed it four times" is a confession of odd behaviour,
which is the detail that certifies the narrator as real.
