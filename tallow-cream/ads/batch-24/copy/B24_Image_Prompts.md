# B24 Image Prompts

Per Brand_Mastery.md: V1/V3 target Problem Aware (New Information response), V2 targets Solution Aware (New Mechanism response). Market is Stage 5 sophisticated (moisturizer), our sub-category sits Stage 3-4 (tallow), so images should read as editorial/documentary, never as polished beauty-ad glamour, which a Stage-5-jaded buyer discounts on sight. No faces in any B24 creative (the B23 lesson: faces attracted lookers not buyers at this account's CPMs). Warm, amber, cream, candlelit palettes throughout, matching brand voice (real-woman, dry humor, never spa/clean-beauty).

All three variations needed at: 1080x1350 (4:5), 1080x1080 (1:1), 1080x1920 (9:16, first time producing this size, safe-zone text kept clear of top/bottom 250px for Stories/Reels UI).

Large serif typography, dark charcoal-green ink, centered, set in a classic newspaper-style serif (Georgia/Times family). Bottom-right corner: small, sharp product photo of one amber-glass jar of whipped tallow balm, no lid reflection glare, sitting on the same cream background so it reads as part of the page not a sticker. Small arrow icon and 'read the story' in a thin sans-serif underneath the arrow, bottom-left. No people, no faces, no hands. Composition must leave the top third and bottom 250px clear for platform UI (Stories safe zone) when producing the 9:16 crop."

Ticker text: "Sleeping fine. Skin wasn't."

**Alt treatment, candid photo:**
Prompt: "Documentary-style candid photograph, NOT a posed beauty shot: a bathroom mirror catching soft, slightly cool morning window light, condensation faintly visible at the edges, a single amber jar of balm resting on the counter in the foreground, slightly out of focus, mirror reflection shows only the empty bathroom, no person in frame. Muted, slightly desaturated color grade like an editorial lifestyle photo, not a glossy ad. Headline text overlaid across the lower-middle of the image on a dark semi-transparent gradient band for legibility, white serif type."
Banner text to render: "'YOU LOOK TIRED.' NO. IT'S MY SKIN."
Alt banner option: "SHE STOPPED SAYING 'THIS IS JUST MY FACE.'"

## Advertorial page images (news-article rewrite, 2026-07-07)

The advertorial now opens with an outcome-first hook rather than a scene-setting story, so its hero image should reinforce "resolved/after" rather than "puzzled/before."

**Hero image (top of article):**
Prompt: "Documentary-style photograph of a bathroom counter shot in warm natural window light, slightly overhead angle. The counter is nearly empty: one amber-glass jar of whipped tallow balm sits alone near the sink, lid off, a bit of the whipped balm visible inside. No clutter, no other products, a folded hand towel in soft focus in the background for warmth. No people, no faces, no hands. Color grade: warm, slightly desaturated, editorial rather than glossy ad lighting."
Alt text: "One jar on an empty bathroom counter."

**Mechanism diagram (mid-article, water-vs-fat explainer):**
Prompt: "Clean, minimal editorial infographic, two-panel side-by-side comparison on a cream background. Left panel labeled 'WATER-BASED CREAM': a simple line illustration of a water droplet sitting on top of a skin-surface cross-section, with a small upward arrow and light steam lines indicating evaporation, timestamp label '7am to noon' beneath. Right panel labeled 'SKIN-IDENTICAL FAT': a simple line illustration of a fat molecule absorbing down into the skin cross-section, with a downward arrow indicating absorption, timestamp label '7am to 2pm and beyond' beneath. Flat, editorial infographic style (like a newspaper science-desk graphic), muted amber and forest-green line work, no photorealism, no people."
Alt text: "Water evaporates, fat absorbs diagram."

**Jar photo (mid-article, product close-up):**
Prompt: "Close-up, sharp product photograph of an open amber-glass jar of whipped tallow and honey balm, whipped texture visible and slightly swirled at the surface, shot at a slight angle on a warm wood or linen surface, soft warm side-lighting, shallow depth of field with the background softly blurred. No hands, no people, no applicator tools in frame."
Alt text: "Whipped tallow and honey balm jar."

## Compliance notes (all prompts)
No real outlet names, no fabricated view counters, no doctor coats/stethoscopes/medical settings, no faces per the B23 lesson, no readable competitor branding on any "cluttered shelf" generic products, no before/after skin-texture claims implied by the imagery itself (the copy carries all claims, images stay atmospheric/product-only).
