## Why Marksman and Not Sniper

We are at Origins step 3: Marksman again at plateau. We have one validated angle (B9C3 One-Jar). B17 is Sniper on it (urgency variants). If B17 lifts the One-Jar to clearly profitable, we stay Sniper (chain depth, offer, etc., as B19). If B17 doesn't, the One-Jar angle has plateaued and we Marksman to find new direction. That is what B18 is.

The Marksman rule (ORIGINS_REFERENCE §16.3): hooks call out the SPECIFIC sub-avatar/angle, the body/hold demonstrates the SHARED core desire. We do not test variations of one angle. We test three different angles head-to-head and let Meta pick the winner.

## What Varies (the "hook" per Marksman)

Three things change per cell, calibrated to the specific sub-avatar:

1. **The opening scene** (the scroll-stop): each ad opens on a moment that names the specific experience this sub-avatar lives.
2. **The image**: each cell gets its own image, because in Marksman the image IS the angle's lever for Meta delivery and click.
3. **The habit-cessation beat** (skeleton element 7): the unexpected discovery that proves the change. Varies per angle so the proof matches the experience the hook named.
4. **The identity payoff line** (skeleton element 9): resolves to the shared core desire but in language that closes the specific experience the hook opened.

The body in between (failed-products inventory, the ask to Elaine, the mechanism explainer, the day markers, the husband-or-peer close) stays roughly equivalent across all three.

## Per-Cell Hook + Image Concepts

### B18C1: The One-Jar (proven baseline)
- **Hook scene:** At Elaine's house, guest bathroom, one jar on the counter (B9C3 opening verbatim).
- **Habit-cessation beat:** Stopped buying travel lotion packets in three places (B9C3 verbatim).
- **Identity payoff:** "Not the soft skin. The not-carrying." (B9C3 verbatim).
- **Image:** the proven near-empty-counter / one amber jar shot. Same image B9C3 used.

### B18C2: Cognitive Relief
- **Hook scene:** Narrator walking past a store window on her lunch break, catching her own reflection, and realizing she did not check. She has not done that without checking in eight years. Opens on the absence of the habit, then catalogs the running mental commentary that has played underneath everything else.
- **Habit-cessation beat:** Stopped narrating her own face. Somewhere in week three she noticed the running commentary had gone quiet. Did not catch herself touch-checking at her desk. Did not run the morning calculation of "how long will this hold."
- **Identity payoff:** "It wasn't the soft skin. It was the quiet."
- **Image:** a woman's reflection in a daytime store window or car window, her face slightly out of focus, hands at her side (not raised to check). First-person POV, walking past, not stopping. The image stops the scroll on a moment of NOT looking, which is the entire angle.

### B18C3: Predictability
- **Hook scene:** Narrator at the bathroom mirror on a Wednesday morning, calmly looking at her own face, and noticing it looks exactly like it did Tuesday. And Monday. She has not had that experience since 2019. Opens on the sameness, then catalogs the years of volatility (Tuesday fine, Wednesday burning, Friday flaking, Sunday breaking out under the jaw).
- **Habit-cessation beat:** Stopped trying to predict it. Stopped opening the bathroom drawer to choose between three different routines based on which face she got. Stopped bracing.
- **Identity payoff:** "I knew what I was getting. I have never been so grateful for boring."
- **Image:** first-person POV at her own bathroom mirror, soft morning light, her face visible but the focus is on the simplicity of the counter beneath the mirror (one jar). The image is about the boring sameness of the morning. NOT a glamour shot, NOT a transformation reveal.

## Marksman Decision Rules (different from Sniper rules)

Marksman is fast direction-finding, not slow optimization. So the rules are looser, the read happens earlier, and we are looking at angle-vs-angle signal, not converter-skeleton-vs-variant.

- **Primary read: which angle Meta DELIVERS and clicks** (CTR and CPM by cell). Meta will tell us within ~3-5 days which angle the auction wants. Cheapest cell to deliver = the angle Meta thinks our audience wants. Highest engagement = the angle the audience responds to.
- **Secondary read: cost per ATC.** Which angle gets clicks that try to buy. This separates "people read the story" from "people are moved by the story."
- **Tertiary: LPV-to-purchase.** Slow to read at low budget, but the eventual decider.
- **Pivot checkpoint inherits from B17:** ~$300 spend or ~14 days. If no cell shows a path on indicators, kill the batch. Difference from B17: instead of pivoting to a new test, B18's failure mode is to pivot to a DIFFERENT non-angle lever (offer, advertorial, different avatar). Three angles in head-to-head Marksman exhausts the "is it the angle?" question.
- **Winner declaration: same 10-purchase floor.** No crowning on 2 sales.
- **Per-cell budget at launch:** $25/cell ($75/day total) if budget is at that level when B18 fires. If still at $20/day re-warming budget when B17 pivots, ramp first or run the test at $7/cell which is below readable: better to extend B17 a few days while ramping than launch B18 underfunded.
