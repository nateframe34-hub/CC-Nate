## Density / Fit Notes (whiteboard format — not the 10-element native skeleton)

- **Scroll-stop:** the crowded 14-bottle cluster = instant "that's my counter" recognition (Comp Ad 1's proven hook image, rebuilt as diagram)
- **Learning beat:** the 14 → 1 math + black arrow = the replacement argument made visual
- **Objection handled:** "still tight by noon" on the left kills the assumption that more products = better results
- **Proof language from reviews:** "stopped buying the rest" / "I use nothing else" / "4 ingredients"
- **Close:** identity/relief payoff ("the tight feeling went with them"), not "soft skin"
