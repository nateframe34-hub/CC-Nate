## Density / Fit Notes (whiteboard format)

- **Scroll-stop:** the itemized receipt-style stack triggers "wait, how much DO I spend?" — arithmetic curiosity
- **Learning beat:** the running total ($574) is the "aha" — most women have never added it up
- **Objection handled:** price. Reframes $49.99 from "a cost" to "the end of a $574 drain." This is the cell that turns the category's #1 complaint into the argument.
- **Body/figure note:** this cell uses a cost-stack rather than a face (the "body" of this argument is the money). Acceptable exception — the arithmetic IS the visual. If a face is wanted, a small dull→soft face pair can sit under each total, but the stack is the hero.
- **Proof language from reviews:** "a little goes a long way," "lasts months," "replaces the whole stack" / "I use nothing else"
- **Close:** the bottom line is the comparison resolved — money framing, not "soft skin"

## Compliance Audit

- ✅ "serum / retinol / cream" appear ONLY as line items on the discarded left-side stack, not describing our balm
- ✅ No "anti-aging" / "wrinkles" / "menopause"
- ✅ Low point is functional ("tight by noon") + financial, not shame/medical
- ✅ No medical claims
- ✅ Price + guarantee present
- ⚠️ Cost figures ($574, line items) are illustrative/representative of a typical stack — keep them plausible, not a specific competitor's published prices (avoid disparagement). Generic category items only, no brand names on the board.
