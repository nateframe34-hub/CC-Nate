## Density / Fit Notes (whiteboard format)

- **Scroll-stop:** "flip your face cream over" is an instruction the reader can act on mentally in <1 second — she pictures her own bottle. The two-label comparison is the dwell hook.
- **Learning beat:** WATER as ingredient #1 is the reveal most women have never noticed. The BLUE mechanism note ("evaporates by noon → sits on top" vs "close to what skin makes → absorbs in") teaches the why — this is the B9C3 mechanism delivered as a label comparison.
- **Objection handled:** trust / "what's actually in it." Turns the 4-ingredient simplicity into a verifiable, readable proof rather than a claim.
- **Body/figure note:** uses ingredient-label rectangles as the "physical thing" rather than a face. The label IS the body of this argument (you read a real label). Acceptable per the comparison structure; the WATER-vs-absorbs mechanism note keeps it teaching, not just listing.
- **Proof language from reviews:** "ingredients I can't pronounce," "no water filler," "close to what skin makes" (the bioidentical/"skin recognized it" language reframed compliantly)
- **Close:** the readable-vs-unreadable bottom line — trust framing

## Compliance Audit

- ✅ Ingredient names on the left (water, glycerin, phenoxyethanol, dimethicone, fragrance) are accurate generic category ingredients, NOT a specific brand's published list — keep them generic/representative, no brand names, to avoid disparagement
- ✅ No "anti-aging" / "wrinkles" / "menopause"
- ✅ "close to what skin makes / absorbs in" is the compliant mechanism phrasing (NOT "bioidentical," NOT "your body," NOT "repairs") — matches the locked B9C3 language
- ✅ No medical claims ("cures," "heals," "repairs from within")
- ✅ No "chemical-free / toxin-free / all-natural" influencer-compliance language — the argument is "readable vs unreadable / water-filler vs absorbs," a neutral category observation, not a fear/harm claim. Deliberately stays OUT of the competitor's "RUIN your microbiome / big pharma" fear lane.
- ✅ Price + guarantee present
