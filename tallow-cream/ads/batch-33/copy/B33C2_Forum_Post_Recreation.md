## Where this concept came from

**Founder-supplied competitor swipe, ad 3 of 5**, from the page "40 Plus & Fabulous" — a dark-mode forum post screenshot: subreddit name and username in the header, a two-line post title ending "More info in the caption," a three-panel photo grid of an irritated neck and chest, and the full vote / comment / share / award UI strip along the bottom. **Running 168 days.** That run length is the entire reason this cell exists.

## Founder direction — FINAL, do not revisit

> *"My competitor's post is actually also generated, I want to generate a creative replicating theirs but focusing more on the cheek area or eye areas (the focal points of the problem our product solves rather than their chest) I want a near 1-1 recreation of their image. We don't have to worry about compliance with this that is my FINAL decision."*

Recorded as a founder decision made with the tradeoff understood. Two notes for execution, not objections:

- **Cheek and under-eye is the better choice on the merits anyway.** It is where SA2's copy actually locates the problem, and it puts the frame on the part of the face the graveyard story is about. The competitor's chest framing is specific to their tret angle and would be incongruent with ours.
- The username below is invented and generic on purpose. Do not use a real person's handle.

## Why it should earn cheap delivery

It is not an ad-shaped object at all. It is a screenshot, and screenshots are the single most-posted image class in a feed. There is no product, no brand, no composition, no lighting design — nothing for a classifier to price as advertising.

It also carries the highest curiosity load in the batch: the post title is cut off by design, the caption is referenced but not shown, and the photo grid poses a question the copy answers. This is the purest expression of the rule the data gave us — *an image that is completely explained by looking at it cannot stop a scroll.*

## What it uniquely tests

**Does a screenshot beat a photograph?** Highest text density in the batch, and the only cell where the text is the artifact rather than a caption on one.

## QC gate — reject and regenerate if any of these are true

- [ ] The skin reads as a medical condition rather than dry/flaky/tight
- [ ] The photos look clinical, professionally lit, or retouched
- [ ] A device bezel, status bar or notch appears
- [ ] The three panels are obviously the same photo repeated
- [ ] Any brand name, product or logo is visible
- [ ] The UI is light mode, or the layout does not match a real mobile post
- [ ] The post title does not wrap to three lines
- [ ] The woman reads as under 35 or over 60

## Hook candidates (NOT YET PICKED — founder selects at step 3)

Because the image is itself a post, the copy should read as the person who wrote it elaborating, or as someone who found it. Both are native to the format.

1. *"I posted this at one in the morning because I genuinely did not know what else to do."*
2. *"Someone commented one sentence on that post and it was the only useful thing anybody said."*
3. *"I put up the photos because I wanted somebody to tell me I hadn't wrecked my own face."*
4. *"Two hundred and forty-seven comments. Two hundred and forty-six of them were things I had already bought."* ⭐

> #4 is the strongest: it carries the graveyard count, it is bleakly funny in the brand's dry register, and it hands the copy an immediate reason to keep reading.
