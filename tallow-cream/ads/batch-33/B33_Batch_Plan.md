## The decision this batch encodes

The account's diagnosis as of 2026-07-30 is unambiguous and names one variable:

- **Copy converts.** B17C1 lifetime: 7.34% CTR, ~5% click→purchase, 12 purchases.
- **The funnel converts.** ~5% LPV→purchase, 43% ATC→purchase, 8% LPV→ATC. All normal-to-good.
- **Delivery cost kills it.** Lifetime CPC $3.03-3.24 against a **$1.44 breakeven requirement** (CPA ≈ CPC × 21). A **2.2x gap, entirely traffic cost.**

Both proven converters were killed 7/30 after ~$961 combined spend, 15 purchases, ~$64 CPA, never a profitable week.

**So: hold the angle constant, vary the creative.** When you know which variable is broken, you test that variable — and at ~$50/day the budget can only resolve one. Four cells run SA2 Angle 1 (the account's proven angle, ~15 of ~20 lifetime sales) in four genuinely different media. One cell hedges on Angle 2.

## The creative rule this batch is built on

> **An image that is completely explained by looking at it cannot stop a scroll. The image must create the gap the copy closes.**

Derived from the account's own spread: B5C1 11.98% CTR and B7C1 10.86% / **$1.75 CPC** (both first-person POV with a story-bearing artifact) versus B17C1 6.90% and B28C1 9.20% (jar on a surface) versus B30 0.99% (faces, soft styling, long-form copy underneath — which rules out copy length and isolates the image).

**Supporting finding, 2026-07-30 competitor swipe (5 ads running 168-208 days): 4 of 5 bake text into the image, and it is always *diegetic*** — it belongs to an object inside the frame (a whiteboard, the post itself, a speech bubble, a document). Our existing ban on overlay badges is right but was over-generalised into "no text at all," producing images that stop a scroll and then give the eye nothing to do.

## Reading the results

**Judge on CPC against $1.44**, not CPM, not CTR, not purchases. A cell is readable at **100+ impressions, roughly $10-15** — which is what makes five cells affordable on ~$50/day at all.

**Rolling-window rule still applies:** no verdict under 3 days or 300 cumulative impressions, whichever comes later. At the cheap CPMs these formats have been producing ($24-117), concentrated spend clears 300 impressions per cell inside a day or two.

**Sequencing:** B32 (SA11, whiteboard × long-form) is live and starved. Launching five more cells onto the same ~$50 puts everything back under the impression bar, which is the exact failure that has dogged every batch this month. **Build B33 now, launch when B32 has a verdict.**
