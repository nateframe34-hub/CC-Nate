## Copy edit

One added clause in the Day 1 beat, giving the image a literal moment to depict. No new beats, no new characters.

**Original:**
> Day 1 - I put it on before bed. It melted into my palm like warm honey. I pressed it into my cheeks, my jaw, my forehead. Thirty seconds total.

**Edited:**
> Day 1 - I put it on before bed. Dabbed a little on the back of my hand first, out of habit, the way you test anything new. It melted into my palm like warm honey. I pressed it into my cheeks, my jaw, my forehead. Thirty seconds total.

Reasoning: the story already describes the texture ("melted like warm honey") but never gives a literal hand/arm moment to photograph. This adds one clause, the habitual test-on-your-hand-first beat, which is something almost everyone actually does and gives the image a real, congruent action.
