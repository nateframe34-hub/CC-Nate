## Copy edit

One sentence expanded to give the image a literal scene to depict, no new beats added.

**Original:**
> When I got home that night I went into my own bathroom and counted what was on my counter.
> Fourteen things.

**Edited:**
> When I got home that night I went into my own bathroom, opened the cabinet under the sink, and started pulling everything out and lining it up on the counter to count it.
> Fourteen things.

Reasoning: the "fourteen things" count stays locked (it's in the headline, "One jar. Fourteen down."), the edit just gives the moment a physical action (opening the cabinet, lining things up) so the image has something concrete to show. The rendered image doesn't need to contain a literally countable fourteen items, just read as "a lot, pulled out and lined up" the way the sentence now describes.
