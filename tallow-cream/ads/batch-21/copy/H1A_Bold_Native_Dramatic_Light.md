## Why this lever, per the brief
Golden-hour light is the single most common thumb-stopping element in genuine amateur photography - feeds are full of it because real people shoot it, not because brands stage it. High visual salience for Meta's classifier, zero production-polish signal for the viewer. Story logic holds: book club is plausibly an evening event.
