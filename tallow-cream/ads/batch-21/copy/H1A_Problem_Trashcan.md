## Copy edit

One paragraph edited to give the trash-can image something specific and countable to depict, instead of the vague original. This replaces the "she'd thrown everything out" line only. Every other paragraph in B9C3 is unchanged.

**Original:**
> She said she'd thrown everything out about nine months earlier. Everything. The toner, the serums, the retinol. She kept one jar.

**Edited:**
> She said she'd thrown everything out about nine months earlier, straight into the bathroom trash. The toner. Two serums. The retinol. Two moisturizers. The spot treatment. Seven bottles in the bin that same afternoon. She kept one jar.

Reasoning: the original already contains a "threw it all out" beat, it just never gave a countable, visualizable version of it. Naming seven specific items gives the image something exact to depict and keeps the number in the realistic 5-10 range the founder flagged, rather than an implied mountain of product that would read as fake.

## Why this swing
This is the problem shot, not a nicer product shot. It matches the native swipe file's actual low-CPM pattern (hip pain in bed, bandaged knee) rather than the empty-counter hero shots B21 was testing before: real mess, no product visible at all, visually says "years of stuff that didn't work" before a single word of copy is read. Seven items keeps it believable instead of a cartoonish overflowing bin.
