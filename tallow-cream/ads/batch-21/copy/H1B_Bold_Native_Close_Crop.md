## Why this lever, per the brief
At thumbnail size on a phone feed, a large near-object with real depth-of-field falloff reads instantly, where the baseline's wide counter shot needs a beat to register. A too-close, slightly-missed-focus snap is arguably MORE camouflaged than the baseline, not less - it's exactly how a real person photographs an object she wants to gossip about, grabbed in two seconds before leaving the bathroom.
