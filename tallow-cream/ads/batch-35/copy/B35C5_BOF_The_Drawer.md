**Sub-Avatar:** SA9 The Actives Casualty · **Angle:** SA9 Angle 1, offer-forward
**Format:** Classy Offer (per `Brand_Mastery.md` SA9 BOF list)
**Length:** ~230 words. BOF is short by design — the reader is already aware.
**Door:** the price objection, answered first rather than deflected

---

## Why this cell exists

The research states the constraint plainly:

> ⚠️ *"we are a $49.99 answer to a $12 question"* — and *"the $49.99 price is
> defensible only to someone who has already spent more than that failing."*

Every native handles that by burying the price at 92% behind a failed drawer.
**This cell does the opposite: it leads with the objection.** That is the correct
BOF move for a reader who already knows what we are and has not bought, because
for her the price IS the remaining obstacle and pretending otherwise wastes the
impression.

The argument is arithmetic, not persuasion: the cheap set already cost more.

## Body Copy

Yes, it is more than the tub from the pharmacy.

I bought the tub from the pharmacy three times.

Then the petroleum jelly. Then the oil. Then the little forty-dollar pot the size of my thumb. Then a French sample my sister swore by.

They are all still in that drawer. Together they cost more than this jar does, and not one of them was still on my face at two in the morning.

My dermatologist told me not to put anything on the peeling. She was protecting me from the wrong things.

That is the entire argument. Water evaporates. It does not matter how good the cream is or how much it cost. If water is the first ingredient it is gone by the middle of the night, and the middle of the night is when the corners of your mouth split.

A fat has nothing in it to leave.

It goes on after, not instead. You change nothing you are already doing. Retinol, retinoid, acid, whatever you are on — you let it dry down and you put this over the top.

Four ingredients. $49.99. Free shipping. 60-day money-back guarantee.

Six weeks. If your face is no different, you email them and you get every dollar back, and you have lost nothing except the time it took to try.

You have already spent more than this finding out what does not work.
