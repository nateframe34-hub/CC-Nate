## Body Copy

I was already sandwiching.

That is what it is called, if you have not been in those threads. Something on first. Then the thing you actually use. Then something over the top to seal it in.

I had been doing it properly for five months. Spaced out, dried down in between, the whole protocol.

And my chin was still flaking onto my keyboard at work.

========

I want to be clear that I was not being careless about this.

I read everything. I waited the twenty minutes. I did the damp-skin thing. I bought the ones that got recommended over and over in those threads, not the expensive nonsense.

That is what made it so demoralising. When you are doing it badly there is at least an obvious thing to fix. I was doing it exactly the way I was told and my face still looked like it was coming apart around the nose.

Five months of that. I had started to think the sandwich was a story people told each other.

========

Here is what I was sandwiching with, in order of how much I wanted each one to work.

The thick cream in the tub that everyone in there recommends. Twelve dollars, and I have bought it three separate times, which tells you something.

Petroleum jelly, for the nights when it was bad enough that I stopped caring what it felt like.

Squalane oil, which I still think is nice and which lasted about an hour.

And a French one my sister brought back that cost more than the other three together.

Four things. Not one of them made it to morning.

========

So then I did the thing where you decide it must be you. That your skin is just the kind this happens to and there is a version of this where you accept flaking as the price and stop complaining about it.

I was wrong about that, and the reason is embarrassingly simple.

The protocol was right. I had one layer wrong.

========

I found it in a comment. Nested three deep, on a post that was not even mine.

Somebody had asked what people put on top. A woman answered underneath, and she was not trying to sell anything or convince anybody. She had clearly just typed it out because she was tired of watching people do what she used to do.

She said: turn over whatever you are putting on top and read the first ingredient. If it is water it is gone by 2am and you are sleeping on nothing. The top layer has to be a fat or there is no point doing the sandwich at all.

She said it took her nine years to work that out.

========

I got up and went and checked all four of mine, standing at the sink at midnight in my dressing gown.

Water. Water. Water was second on the oil. The French one, water.

Every single one of them. Five months of building a careful sandwich with no lid on it.

========

The reason a fat works and water does not is not complicated once somebody says it out loud.

Water evaporates. It is doing exactly what water does. It is not a bad product, it is just not a seal.

A fat does not evaporate, so it is still there at six in the morning. And the fat in the one she named is close enough to what skin makes on its own that it goes in rather than sitting there in a film.

Which is what people are actually reaching for when they use petroleum jelly. Same job. Without the sliding around on your pillowcase.

Nothing about my routine changed. Same order, same waiting, same everything. One layer swapped.

========

I replied to her and asked what it was and she told me and I sat there and laughed.

Beef fat. As the top layer. Over that.

She wrote back: "I know. I said the same thing. Try it for two weeks before you decide."

========

Night 1, I let everything dry down the way I always do and put this over the top instead. It went on like warm honey. It did not sting anywhere, including the two places that sting when anything touches them.

Night 4, I woke up and my face did not feel tight. I lay there for a minute trying to work out what was different.

Night 9, a woman at work asked if I had changed something. I said no. She said, "You have though."

Night 14, my chin did not flake onto anything all day. I noticed at about four in the afternoon and then I checked in the bathroom mirror twice because I did not trust it.

Night 21, I got to the end of a day without touching my face once to see how bad it was.

========

Here is the part I did not expect.

I used to read those threads every night. Not casually. The way you read something when you are looking for a specific answer and you have not found it yet.

I had a saved folder. Forty-odd posts in it.

About a month in I opened the app out of habit and could not think what I had come to look up.

I have not opened the folder since. I could not tell you the last thing I saved to it.

That was the thing I wanted back. Not the skin. The not-looking.

========

Somebody posted last week. Five months in, doing everything right, still peeling.

I wrote four lines underneath about reading the first ingredient on whatever you put on top.

I did not tell her to stop. Nobody should tell her that. It is working.

========

I am not going to tell you a jar of beef fat fixed my life. That sounds like an ad. This is not an ad. I am telling you what a stranger typed at eleven at night in a thread that was not even mine.

Four ingredients. $49.99. Free shipping. 60-day money-back guarantee. If it does nothing in six weeks you email them and get every dollar back.

Link is below if you want it.

It is more than the tub. I know. I bought the tub three times, plus the jelly and the oil and the French one, and together they cost more than this did and not one of them was there in the morning.

What I want you to have is not the jar. It is the version of this where you built the whole careful thing and it finally has a lid on it.

I thought I had been doing it wrong for five months.

I had been doing it right, with the wrong top layer.
