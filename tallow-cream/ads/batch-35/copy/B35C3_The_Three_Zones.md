## Body Copy

It is never my whole face.

It is three places, and it is the same three places every single time.

The creases either side of my nose. The corners of my mouth. The outside corners of my eyes.

If you are nodding right now you already know exactly what I mean, and if you are not, this will not make much sense to you.

========

Nine weeks on a prescription retinoid, and everywhere else was fine. Better than fine.

That is the part nobody prepares you for. You do not get a face that is uniformly bad. You get a face that is mostly working and three small areas that look like they are peeling off you.

And they are all on the front. Nose, mouth, eyes. You cannot hide any of them.

I stopped smiling properly at work because the corners of my mouth would split when I did. That is a stupid sentence to type and it is exactly what happened.

========

I threw money at those three spots specifically.

The thick tub cream everyone recommends, dabbed on just those areas. Petroleum jelly at night, which worked until about two in the morning. An eye one that cost forty dollars for a container the size of a thumb. Squalane oil. A sample of something French my sister swore by.

Every one of them was gone by the time I woke up. That was the pattern and I could not see it at the time because I was too busy buying the next one.

========

Nobody warns you about the three zones. That is the honest thing to say here.

You get told to expect dryness. Nobody sits you down and says: it will be your nose creases, the corners of your mouth and the outside corners of your eyes, and it will be those three for as long as you do this.

If somebody had told me that at the start I would have stopped blaming myself a lot sooner.

========

The first useful thing came from a dermatologist, and it was not even mine.

A woman in a group I am in wrote that before a chemical peel, her dermatologist put something on those exact three spots first. Before anything else. Nose creases, lip corners, eye corners.

She called it a lightbulb moment and I remember thinking, that is a very specific list.

It was the same three. Different woman, different doctor, different country. The same three places.

So it was not me. It was just where this happens to everybody.

========

But the derm trick was about going on first, and my problem was that nothing stayed on afterward.

Marguerite is the one who explained that part. She is 61 and has been on hers for nine years, and she has no reason to tell anybody anything.

She said: turn over what you are putting on those spots and read the first ingredient.

I did. Water. All of them. Even the forty-dollar one.

She said water evaporates. That is all it does. You put it on at eleven and by two those three places are bare again, and bare is when they split.

She said the top layer has to be a fat. Not instead of anything. After. You do what you already do, let it dry down properly, and then put something over the top that has no water in it and therefore has nothing to lose.

She said, "It is one extra step. You do not take anything away."

========

I said, "Cow fat. Near my eyes."

She said, "I know. That is what I said. It is four ingredients and it is the only thing that was still there when I woke up."

========

Night 1, I did everything the same and then put it over the top. Warm honey in my hands. It did not sting on the mouth corners, which is where everything stings.

Night 4, I woke up and touched the creases beside my nose before I got out of bed. Smooth. I did it again the next morning to check.

Night 9, someone at work asked me what was different. I said nothing was. She said, "Something is."

Night 14, I smiled properly at a leaving do and nothing split. I did not think about it until afterwards in the car, which is the point.

Night 21, all three. Nose, mouth, eyes. Three weeks with none of them going.

========

Here is the part I did not expect.

I used to check those three places in every reflective surface I passed. Car window. Lift doors. The dark part of my monitor when the screen went to sleep. It was not vanity. It was reconnaissance. I was checking how much longer I had before somebody noticed.

I do not know exactly when I stopped. I only know that I caught myself walking past the lift doors one morning without turning my head, and it felt strange enough that I noticed it.

That was the thing I wanted back. Not the skin. The not-checking.

========

Dawn posted in that group last week. Nine weeks in. Three places. Fine everywhere else. Asking if anyone had anything for just those.

I wrote back about reading the first ingredient on whatever she puts on top, and about letting the other thing dry down first.

I did not tell her to stop using it. Nobody should tell her that. It is working, and she has done nine weeks.

========

I am not going to tell you a jar of beef fat changed my life. That sounds like an ad. This is not an ad. I am telling you about three places on my face and what a woman who has been doing this for nine years told me to put on them.

Four ingredients. $49.99. Free shipping. 60-day money-back guarantee. If it does nothing in six weeks you email them and get every dollar back.

Link is below if you want it.

It is more than the tub. It is also less than the eye one I bought, which was gone by two in the morning like everything else.

What I want you to have is not the jar. It is walking past the lift doors without turning your head.

I thought those three places were the price.

They were just the part nobody had explained.
