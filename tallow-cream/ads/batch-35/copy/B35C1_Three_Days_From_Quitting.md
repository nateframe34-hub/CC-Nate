## Body Copy

I had the tube in my hand over the bathroom bin on a Tuesday night.

Ten months in. My nose was raw and peeling. The corners of my mouth had split again that morning and I had put a plaster on one of them like a child.

I stood there for a long time with my arm out.

I did not throw it away. But I want to be honest that it was close, and that it was not the first time.

========

It is a prescription retinoid. My dermatologist wrote it for me the first time ten years ago.

I have quit it four times since.

Every single time for the same reason. And not once because it stopped working. That is the part that made me feel insane.

It works. That is not in question. It has always worked. I would look in the mirror in the morning and see the thing I wanted and also see flaking around my nose that no amount of anything would sit over.

So I would stop. My skin would settle down in about three weeks. And then I would look in the mirror again and start over.

Ten years of that.

========

I want to list what was under my sink, because I think some of you have the same shelf.

Two tubs of the thick cream everyone recommends. The blue one from the baby aisle. A jar of petroleum jelly I used for slugging. Squalane oil. A French one my sister brought back that cost more than the rest put together. A gentle cleanser. A second gentle cleanser, because I thought the first one was the problem.

None of them were bad. I want to be fair about that. They just did not last the night.

I would put something on and it would feel fine for twenty minutes and by 2am there was nothing on my face at all.

========

For a long time I thought that was me. That my skin was simply the kind that could not do this.

It is not that. Nobody had told me what was actually happening, and it turns out it is not complicated.

========

Marguerite runs the front desk at my dentist. She is 61. We have been talking about nothing twice a year for six years.

I was in for a cleaning in February with a flake on my chin I had tried to cover and given up on. She looked at me for slightly too long and said, gently, "Are you on a retinoid?"

I said yes. Ten months.

She said, "Nine years for me. Same prescription."

I said, "Nine years?"

She said, "I stopped quitting when I stopped putting water on top of it."

========

Here is what she told me. I have thought about it every day since.

She said, turn over whatever you are putting on afterward and read the first ingredient.

I did it that night. Standing at the sink. Water. Then the next one. Water. The French one my sister brought. Water.

Water goes away. That is what water does. That is not a flaw in the product, it is just what it is. You put it on at eleven and by two there is nothing there.

She said the thing she uses is a fat, and it is close enough to what skin makes on its own that it goes in instead of sitting on top. And because there is no water in it, there is nothing to leave.

She said, "It goes on after. Not instead. You do not change anything you are doing. You just put something on top that stays there until morning."

That was the whole thing. One extra step. Nothing taken away.

========

I said, "What is it?"

She told me and I laughed at her. Out loud, in a dentist's office.

I said, "Cow fat. On my face. On top of that."

She said, "I know. I said the same thing. I was also about four days from quitting when somebody told me."

========

I ordered one that night.

Night 1, I let the other thing dry down properly first, the way she said, and then put this over the top. It melted in my hands like warm honey. It did not sting. I sat on the edge of the bath for a second because I had genuinely expected it to sting.

Night 4, I woke up and my face did not feel like paper. That is the only way I can describe it. For ten years I had woken up and felt paper.

Night 9, my daughter asked me if I had stopped using the tube. I said no. She said, "You look like you stopped."

Night 14, I put makeup on for a work thing. It did not catch on anything around my nose. It just went on, the way it does for other people.

Night 21, I noticed the corners of my mouth had not split in three weeks. I had to count backwards twice to be sure.

========

Here is the part I did not expect.

For ten years I had a number in my head at all times. How many days I had been on it. How many weeks until it was supposed to settle. How close I was to the point where I would give up again.

It was always running. Like a clock in another room.

Sometime around week six I realised I did not know the number anymore. I had stopped counting and I could not tell you when I stopped.

That was the thing I actually wanted. Not the skin. The clock stopping.

========

I told her what Marguerite told me. Turn it over and read the first ingredient. Put something on top that has no water in it and let the other thing dry down first.

I did not tell her to stop what she was doing. Nobody should tell her that. It works.
