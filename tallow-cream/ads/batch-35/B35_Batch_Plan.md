## Why this batch exists

SA9 has been flagged ⭐ RECOMMENDED NEXT LEAD TEST in `Brand_Mastery.md` for
weeks and was never run. B29-B34 all went to SA2 and SA11 instead — we kept
optimising the avatar that was already converting a little rather than testing
the one the research called *"highest urgency of anything in the research."*

Founder strategic read, 2026-08-04, which this batch acts on: SA2's problem is
real but not urgent. People acknowledge a crowded counter and do not act today.
The account's funnel signature agrees — good CTR, poor conversion, zero repeats,
price sensitivity. That is interest without urgency.

SA9 is the opposite shape. Primary emotion is **fear**, and the fear is precise:
*"I will have to quit the thing that's finally working."*

## The strategic case in one line

The tallow *market* is Stage 4-5 and saturated. **Tallow-as-the-layer-over-an-
active is Stage 2-3.** Nobody runs this claim. We are not making a new claim
about the product — we are making a new claim about *when to use it*.

## Angle

**SA9 Angle 1 — "You don't have to choose between the treatment and comfortable
skin."** Named the strongest hook in the document.

- **Belief shifted:** old — *clear skin OR comfortable skin, pick one.* New —
  *the flaking is the cost of what the treatment does; put fat back on top
  afterward and you keep both.*
- **Angle 2 carried inside:** it goes on AFTER, not instead. Nothing is removed.
- **Awareness:** Solution-Aware. They have already failed CeraVe, Aquaphor,
  Vaseline.

## 🚨 CLAIM RULES — tightest in the document

Non-negotiable, from `Brand_Mastery.md` SA9 and the 2026-07-29 findings.

1. **Comfort and feel language ONLY.** Never heal, repair, restore, barrier
   repair, treat, fix, or any named condition.
2. **NEVER NAME THE PRESCRIPTION.** No brand names, no generic drug names. The
   reader supplies it from her own life. Their framing — *"the thing I use at
   night"*, *"the tube"* — is safer AND more native than ours.
3. **Never position the product as an alternative to the treatment.** They are
   proud of it. They want to survive it, not be rescued from it. Trade-pride
   register confirmed: *"it'll be worth it in the end"*, *"trust the process."*
4. Standard account bans still apply: no menopause, no anti-aging, no wrinkles
   or fine lines, no "moisturizer/serum/routine" for our product.
5. **Use their vocabulary, not ours:** "sandwiching", "slugging".

## 🚨 The price problem, and how every cell handles it

Confirmed finding, `Problem_Side_Research_Findings_2026-07-29.md`:

> ⚠️ **CONFIRMED — we are a $49.99 answer to a $12 question.**
> CeraVe tub ~$12-16 · Vaseline ~$5 · Aquaphor ~$10 · Aveeno ~$10

And the resolution, from the same document:

> *"the $49.99 price is defensible **only** to someone who has already spent more
> than that failing. To a first-time buyer it is not. It should target someone on
> their sixth."*

**Consequence applied to every cell in this batch:** the narrator must have
already failed the cheap set, ON THE PAGE, BEFORE the price appears. If an ad
reads as "here is your first thing to try", the price kills it. If it reads as
"here is what I bought after the drawer of tubs didn't work", the price is proof.

## Ownable specificity nobody else uses

**Nose creases. Lip corners. Eye corners.** Dermatologist-taught pre-application
zones, described in-thread as *"a lightbulb moment."* C3 is built on it.
