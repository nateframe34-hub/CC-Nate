# Follower Warmup Campaign — Julie Brennan Page

> **Purpose:** Low-risk engagement campaign to build Meta's trust with the new BM, generate spend history, and grow Julie Brennan's follower count before running conversion campaigns. Not designed for conversions — designed to unlock delivery throttling and build page social proof.
>
> **Rationale:** New BM is currently throttled at ~$2/day despite $100 in funds, approved ads, and clean setup. Follower/engagement campaigns have the lowest delivery bar, which means fastest payment history accumulation + visible social proof on Julie's page for future ad-clickers.

## Creative

### Caption

```
Drop a picture of your pet in the comments 👇
```

That's it. No hashtags. No story. No CTA beyond the comment ask.

### Comment Reply Strategy (do this manually once comments start coming in)

After the first few comments land, reply from Julie's account with a photo of Biscuit and a short line. Examples:
- "Biscuit, reporting for duty. What's this one's name?"
- "This is Biscuit. He's 6 and convinced he's a person."
- "Biscuit says hi. Yours is adorable."

**Why this matters:** Comment threads weight higher than single comments in Meta's algorithm. Replies from the page owner also signal authentic engagement, which lowers CPMs further and increases organic reach on the post.

## Why "Pet Parents" Beats Alternatives

**"48 and figuring it out" / age-based hooks** — FAIL. Calling out age makes viewers acknowledge something they don't want to acknowledge. Works for conversion ads where problem-awareness is the goal. Fails for follows where low-friction relatability wins.

**"Dog moms / Dog parents"** — weaker than "pet parents" because:
- Excludes cat / bird / reptile / small animal owners
- Gendered ("moms") cuts out half the potential follow pool

**"Pet parents"** — wins because:
- Captures every species
- Gender-neutral
- Zero problem-awareness required — pure positive identity
- The "drop a picture" CTA converts a cheap follow into a comment (higher engagement signal)

## Success Metrics

This campaign is NOT about conversions. It's about:

1. **Spend successfully** — first real payment history on the new BM
2. **Daily spend at or near budget** — $5-7/day actually delivering means Meta's delivery throttle is loosening
3. **Follower growth** — Julie's page goes from near-zero to something that looks real (50-200 followers over 4-5 days at this budget)
4. **Comment threads** — social proof that compounds over time

**Don't measure this campaign by CPM or CPA.** Those metrics don't matter here. The goal is unlocking delivery, not performance.

---

## Transition Plan

**Day 1-4:** Let warmup run at $5-7/day

**Day 5:** Check ATC campaign — is it starting to spend now? If yes, the warmup worked and Meta has relaxed throttling. If no, add another 2-3 days of warmup.

**Day 6-7:** Gradually unpause the ATC campaign at **$15-20/day** (not the original $40). Keep warmup running in parallel.

**Day 8+:** If ATC is delivering normally, scale ATC by 20% every 2 days. Keep warmup running until Julie's page has a legitimate-looking follower count (100+).

**Day 12-14:** Once ATC has accumulated 10-15 purchases, switch to Purchase optimization. Kill the warmup campaign once Julie's page is credible.
