## BROADER DTC SKINCARE DATA

### First-Order Discount Benchmarks
- 10%: baseline (Primally Pure, most mid-market)
- 15%: SMS opt-in sweet spot
- 20%: aggressive but standard for budget/mid brands (Lady May)
- 30%+: subscription-first hook, not first-order discount

### Free Gift vs Discount
- Free offers convert **40% better** than equivalent-value discounts
- Sample kit (7-day trial) on orders $50+ drove **23% conversion increase** vs branded GWP
- **41% of sample recipients bought full-size within 60 days**
- "Get 50% more free" framing outperformed "35% off" — 75% more units sold
- Free shipping influences **52.6%** of purchase decisions

### Best Subscription Structures
- Tiered: bigger discount for longer commitment at signup
- Lifetime price lock framing reduces churn anxiety
- Progressive discounts reward loyalty, not just first order
- Toups' 20% → 10% = high hook on first 2 orders, reduces early churn
- Consecutive gifts at orders 2 and 3 combat the highest-churn window

## RECOMMENDED LAUNCH OFFER STACK

| Mechanic | Recommendation | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| **Subscribe & Save** | 20% off orders 1-2 ($39.99), 15% ongoing ($42.49), free shipping | Mirrors Toups (top converter), beats flat 10% |
| **Email Capture** | Free sample sachet OR 15% off | Free gift converts 40% better than discounts |
| **SMS Capture** | 15% off (if discount route) | Extra 5% rewards higher-intent channel |
| **Bundle** | 2-pack at ~$85 (vs $100) or Glow Kit (tallow + eye cream) at $69.99 | Bundles move AOV — proven by Evil Goods + Lady May |
| **Guarantee** | 60 days, conversational language | 60 days is new standard (Primally Pure, Lady May) |
| **Free Shipping** | On orders $50+ | Captures 52.6% of decisions, threshold just above hero SKU |
| **BNPL** | Shop Pay Installments from day 1 | $49.99 is sweet spot where BNPL meaningfully increases conversion |

### What to SKIP at Launch
- Full loyalty/rewards program (add complexity at 6 months)
- Aggressive 30-50% first-order discounts (trains buyers to wait for sales)
- Order bumps/upsells (validate core checkout first, add in month 2+)
