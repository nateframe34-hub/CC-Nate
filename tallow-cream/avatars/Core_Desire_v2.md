## The New Core Desire

**"I want my skin to cooperate again."**

### What changed and why

| | Old Core Desire | New Core Desire |
|---|---|---|
| Statement | "I want to be seen again" | "I want my skin to cooperate again" |
| Locus of control | External (husband / friend / sister sees her) | Internal (her vs. her own skin) |
| Time horizon | Slow / reflective (someday) | Daily / recurring (every morning) |
| Built-in urgency | None | High — the fight is today |
| Validation source | Other people | Her own experience |
| Permanent force (Origins) | Status + Relationships | Health + Comfort + Control |
| Trigger emotion | Sadness / grief (low purchase-urgency) | Frustration / fatigue (high purchase-urgency) |

### Why this re-anchors the strategy correctly

1. **Adversary framing.** The skin is the antagonist, not the missing spouse-gaze. Her enemy is concrete, present, in her bathroom every morning. That's a purchase-trigger.
2. **Self-orientation.** Two of the four validated converters already won on agency/relief language ("the not-carrying," "stop refilling the bucket, get the right bucket"). The old Core Desire mis-read those wins as relational. The new Core Desire matches what's actually working.
3. **Daily recurrence.** "Be seen" can wait. "My skin and I are not on speaking terms" cannot — it shows up at 11am every single day. Urgency is structural, not manufactured.
4. **Breadth without dilution.** Every surface expression of the avatar's problem (tight, cracking, reactive, foundation won't sit, nothing holds) is a form of "the skin is not cooperating." The Core encompasses the whole roster without diluting the specificity.
5. **Voice fit.** Real-woman phrasing ("my skin and I are not on speaking terms," "my face has been picking fights with me since I turned forty-eight") is the brand voice already documented for Julie Brennan.

### What does NOT change

- The avatar IS still the Invisible Woman archetype (post-40 skin, has tried things, tired of trying)
- The product mechanism (tallow as skin's-own-oil match) is unchanged
- The 11 sub-avatars and narrowed sub-sub-avatars stay on the roster (see `Core_Desire_Remapping.md` for how each ladders to the new core)
- The 60-day guarantee, BOGO 50% offer, $49.99 price all unchanged
- The validated converters (B5C1, B7C1, B9C3, B3C2) stay validated — they were arguably already operating on the new core, the *label* was wrong

### What this re-anchor does NOT prove

This is a strategic re-anchor based on convergent adversarial review, not a tested fact. The B16 head-to-head test (see `B16_Spec.md`) is the experiment that decides whether v2 outperforms v1 in actual conversion. If B16 fails to lift conversion, we re-examine. **Locked-in until disproven, not locked-forever.**

## Converter Skeleton v2 — 11 Required Elements

The previous 10-element skeleton (PRINCIPLE 4 in `Tallow_Ad_Creative_Principles.md`) is preserved verbatim with ONE new required element added: the **Urgency Anchor**. All other elements remain non-negotiable.

> **PRINCIPLE 4 update:** Every converter on this account contains ALL 11 of these elements. Non-converters are missing at least 3. The skeleton is the structure — do not skip any.

1. **External noticing as hook** — someone else notices the change first, NOT the narrator looking in the mirror
2. **Skin-specific noticing language** — "your skin looks different/softer," not generic "you look great"
3. **Named peer-adjacent authority** — credibility from character traits or relationship length, NOT credentials
4. **Mechanism explained BY the discovery character** — narrator never lectures
5. **🆕 Urgency Anchor (NEW)** — a beat that makes the daily, recurring cost of the un-cooperating skin visceral. See "What the Urgency Anchor is and isn't" below.
6. **Progressive day-marker transformation** — Day 1, Day 4-5, Day 8-10, Day 14, Day 21
7. **Unexpected habit-cessation beat** — "I didn't notice I stopped..." Functional, NOT vanity
8. **Close mirrors hook** — structural loop back to the opening with an inversion
9. **Identity-level payoff** — closes on what the skin change MEANS, NEVER on "soft skin"
10. **Solution-aware + accidental discovery** — narrator already knows skin is the problem
11. **Zero medical authority, zero shame framing** — doctor only as the dismissing authority

### What the Urgency Anchor is and isn't

**It IS** a beat that makes the daily/ongoing cost of the un-cooperating skin specific and visceral. Placed between the failed-products list and the discovery scene (so the reader feels the cost of inaction BEFORE the solution arrives). 50-120 words.

**It ISN'T** a fake countdown ("only 24 hours left!"), a scarcity claim ("almost sold out!"), or a doom prediction ("your skin is getting worse!"). Those read as scammy AND violate compliance (the last one killed B4).

**Acceptable urgency mechanisms (any one or more):**
- **Daily recurrence** — "Every morning the same morning. Foundation by 7, cracking by noon, reapplied by 1, ineffective by 4. Then tomorrow."
- **Cost-bleed math** — "I spent $94 on skincare in March. Skin still tight by noon."
- **Event anchor** (for specific sub-avatars only — Anniversary, Reunion, Wedding Dreader)
- **Stop-the-grind narration** — "I was so tired of fighting my own face every morning."

**Forbidden urgency mechanisms:**
- Fake countdowns or scarcity
- "Your skin is getting worse" framing (compliance + killed B4)
- Shame framing as the urgency engine
- Medical-sounding doom claims

### Where the Urgency Anchor goes in the body

After failed-products list, before discovery scene. The body structure (PRINCIPLE 7 in Principles doc) updates as follows:

| # | Beat | Words | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hook scene | 50-100 | external noticing, named witness |
| 2 | Narrator context | 150-200 | name, age, brief skin history |
| 3 | Failed products list | 150-200 | what she tried, why each failed |
| **4** | **🆕 Urgency Anchor** | **50-120** | **daily/recurring cost of inaction made visceral** |
| 5 | Discovery scene | 200-250 | discovery character + specific physical setting |
| 6 | Mechanism explanation | 150-200 | discovery character explains WHY |
| 7 | First use + day markers | 250-300 | Day 1, 4, 9, 14, 21 |
| 8 | Habit-cessation beat | 100-150 | unexpected thing she stopped doing |
| 9 | Household/social expansion | 100-150 | others using it, social proof |
| 10 | Close | 200-250 | mirrors hook + identity payoff + product line |

Total: ~1,400-1,720 words. Marginally longer than the v1 skeleton but still within the 1,400-1,600 voice/density target if other beats are tight.

## What writers need to do differently starting now

1. **Anchor every new ad on the new Core Desire.** The opening conflict is woman-vs-her-own-skin, not woman-vs-an-absent-spouse-gaze.
2. **Write the Urgency Anchor as a structural beat.** Not an afterthought. It does the same work as the mechanism explanation — required, non-skippable, occupies its own scene.
3. **Default closes to self-orientation, not external validation.** The husband-notice close is now ONE option, not THE option. See `Core_Desire_Remapping.md` for the close-swap analysis on the 11 existing sub-avatars.
4. **For sub-avatars whose existing close is external-validation-dependent** (Husband-Noticed, Hairdresser, Sister Visit, Group Text, possibly Photo Avoider), the existing converter ad copy is preserved AS-IS for the B16 control test. New ads under these sub-avatars test the close-swap.
