## The Sequenced Plan

### PHASE 1 — Foundation Fix (during BM warmup, ~2 weeks, $0)

These are the items the Next_Move_Council already identified as the highest-leverage waiting-period moves. They happen REGARDLESS of which avatar wins. Doing them now means the race in Phase 2 produces real signal instead of noise.

1. **PDP banned-phrase fix** — Section 4 ("up to 60% less" → "a lot less"; drop "After menopause"), Review Block 1 ("menopausal" → "my skin changed").
2. **BOGO native discount + Liquid block built and TEST-PURCHASED** — validates cart math, checkout discount carry-through, and pixel Purchase-with-value in one 20-minute action.
3. **PDP conversion edits** — swap Section 3/4 order so mechanism comes before ingredient list; add "55 cents a day" money-math at the CTA; pull guarantee badge up next to hero CTA; fix Review Block 4 (cut the 9-year-old's-arm review, lead with husband-noticed and threw-out-8 reviews matching the B9C3 avatar).
4. **Advertorial / pre-sell bridge page** — lightly-reworked B9C3, continues the ad's voice, handles the $50-vs-$20 objection in-narrative, hands off to PDP with visitor already sold. PDP's job shrinks to closing.
5. **Email capture + abandoned-cart email** — already drafted, costs founder-time only, turns clickers into a list.
6. **Stage B16 paused in Meta** so day-one-of-budget is a single toggle.

**Exit criteria for Phase 1:** all six items done, one real test purchase landed end-to-end, pixel fires Purchase with correct value in Test Events.

### PHASE 2 — The 2-Horse Race (4 weeks, $145/day = ~$4,000 budget, or less if cashflow constrained)

Do NOT lock on B9C3 alone yet. The Loyal Opposition is right: the B9C3 signal is from a tiny sample, and committing to it before a fair head-to-head is overfitting.

Run **B9C3 (The One-Jar Discovery) vs B7C1 (Mother's Photo / Pond's)** as a 4-week race. Same offer, same PDP, same advertorial, only the ad differs. Let Meta's auction + your tracker decide.

- **Decision rule:** at end of week 2, the lower-performer drops to 20% spend, higher-performer takes 80%. At end of week 4, if one ad has 2x ROAS over 3 sustained days AND >2x the orders of the other, lock that avatar. If neither hits 2x ROAS but one is clearly ahead on CPA, lock that one. If both are roughly tied, lock B9C3 (lower CPM + visual A/B advantage).
- **Stop-loss:** if both ads are below 0.8 ROAS after 14 days with the fixed funnel, stop spending and reconsider — the issue is not avatar, it's offer or product-market fit.

### PHASE 3 — The Avatar Bible on the WINNER (2 weeks, ~45 founder-hours, ~$160 incidental costs)

After Phase 2 picks a winner, execute the Deep Research Architect's plan against ONLY that avatar. The 6 buckets:

1. **Subreddit deep-dive** — r/skincareover30, r/skincareaddiction, r/30PlusSkinCare, r/SkincareAddictionLux, r/SkincareAddicts. Search-mine for "threw out" / "kept one" / "minimalist" / "simplified routine" language. ~8 hours.
2. **Facebook group ethnography** — 3-5 closed groups for women 45-65 talking skincare. Read 60 days back. Document verbatim language. ~10 hours.
3. **YouTube + podcast comment mining** — minimalist-skincare creators (Dr. Dray, Cassandra Bankson, Hyram), pull top 200 comments per relevant video, sort by length + emotional density. ~6 hours.
4. **3-5 customer Zoom calls (45 min each)** — the 9 real customers we have are the dataset. Ask the 12-question protocol the Deep Research Architect specced. ~5 hours including scheduling.
5. **Competitor intel** — Primally Pure, Vintage Tradition, Toups & Co, FATCO. Read 200 reviews each, extract language patterns + objections + JTBD signals. ~8 hours.
6. **Cultural surround** — minimalism / "less is more" cultural lane, Marie Kondo aftermath, the "I threw it all out" archetype in midlife transition literature. ~8 hours.

**Artifact:** "One-Jar Discovery Avatar Bible" — 12-15 page Google Doc with verbatim quotes, emotional triggers, identity markers, daily-life moments, objections, language inventory, and the "what they know that they don't know they know" insight section.

**Red-team test:** before declaring the Bible done, hand it to one council agent that has NOT seen it and ask "if you wrote an ad from this, would it sound like B9C3 or like a generic skincare ad?" If generic, the Bible is too shallow. Re-do the weakest section.

### PHASE 4 — Scale (months 3-6)

With a verified avatar + a working funnel + a Bible deep enough to write 5-10 fresh variants from, the path to $10K/mo is:

- **Working math** (from Path-to-$10K agent): 174 orders/mo at $57.49 AOV (assuming 30% BOGO mix) at $25 CPA = $4,350 ad spend + $5,650 contribution margin = $10K topline at ~1.7 ROAS.
- **Bottleneck:** LPV→ATC needs to hit 8% sustained. Best historical (on 4 LPV) was 12.5%, so the ceiling is plausibly there.
- **Realistic 90-day path:** Month 1 = -$700 to -$1,500 (funnel + race testing), Month 2 = roughly breakeven (winner found, variants rolling), Month 3 = $10K *if* funnel hit its targets in Month 2.
- **Proof-of-concept milestone before $10K:** **$1K/mo sustained for 3 months.** This is the Loyal Opposition's amendment and it is correct. Hitting $1K consistently proves the funnel + avatar + offer combination works; $10K is then a scaling problem, not a discovery problem. Skip this milestone and you spend the ad budget chasing a finish line you cannot yet see the path to.

## What "Master The Avatar" Actually Means

The user's framing — *"understand their problem better than they do"* — gets operationalized as four falsifiable claims the Avatar Bible has to be able to defend with verbatim quotes:

1. **The trigger event.** What specifically happened in the last 12-24 months that made her finally throw out the 8 bottles? (Not "her skin changed" — the specific moment. Pulling a photo. A daughter's wedding. A mirror in bad lighting at a friend's house. The bible names the top 3 trigger events with quote evidence.)
2. **The shame layer.** What does she NOT say out loud — to her husband, to her sister, to the camera — about how her face feels? (The Bible captures the second-order language, not the first-order. "My skin feels rough" is first-order. "I don't want to be touched" is second-order.)
3. **The identity she is protecting.** Is it "I am still attractive"? "I am still myself"? "I am not vain, I am practical"? "I do not buy into beauty industry nonsense"? The winning angle aligns to ONE of these — the Bible identifies which.
4. **The relief moment she is buying.** Not "soft skin." The specific 30-second experience that signals "this is working." (Likely: looking in the mirror at the end of week 2 and not wincing. The Bible has the quote, not the inference.)

If the Bible can defend those four with real quotes from real women, it is deep enough. If it cannot, it is a positioning document, not a research document.
