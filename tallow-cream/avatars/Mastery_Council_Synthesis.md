### Decision rules, recomputed at $50/day

Reality check on volume: $50/day = $700 per 14 days. At a $35-50 CPA, that is roughly 14-20 orders per fortnight IF things work, and possibly 0-5 if they don't. Order-count rules alone are too slow at this spend level, so leading indicators carry the early reads.

- **Day 7 check (leading indicators only, no kills):** advertorial/LP CTR per cell, cost per ATC, LPV→ATC rate. Any cell with a hard-kill signal per the existing Kill Rules framework gets reviewed, not auto-killed (fresh-BM CPMs may need recalibration per the existing note in the tracker docs).
- **Day 14 check:** if combined orders ≥ 8, shift budget 60/25/15 toward leaders (CBO will largely do this itself; intervene only if CBO concentrates on a cell whose downstream economics are worse). If combined orders < 8, rank cells on cost per ATC and LPV→ATC and continue without locking anything.
- **Day 28 lock:** requires a **minimum combined order floor of 10** before declaring a winner. Above the floor: lock the cell with the best CPA, provided it has 2x or more the orders of the runner-up OR clearly better LPV→ATC. Below the floor: extend 2 weeks rather than lock on noise.
- **Middle-band rule (closes the money-losing-winner hole):** breakeven ROAS is ~1.75 on the BOGO mix. If the leading cell sustains 0.8-1.5 ROAS, it "wins" the race but does NOT trigger the full 45-hour Bible. It triggers a reduced 15-hour Bible plus funnel diagnosis, because the bottleneck is still downstream of the ad. Full Bible investment requires either 1.5+ ROAS sustained or LPV→ATC at 5%+ with CPA trending down.
- **Stop-loss:** if all three cells sit below 0.8 ROAS after 21 days with the fixed funnel and no leading-indicator improvement, pause spend at ~$1,050 total exposure and reconvene. The problem at that point is offer or funnel, not angle, and more spend doesn't buy more information.
- **Scaling trigger (founder's rule, locked):** positive ROAS sustained over 3 days → budget scales +20%. Each subsequent +20% step requires the ROAS to hold at the new level for 3 days. Path math: $50 → $60 → $72 → $86 → $104 → $125 → $150/day takes a minimum of ~3 weeks of continuously positive performance. That is the realistic bridge from race-winner to $10K/mo pace ($145/day at $25-35 CPA).
