## Origins Framework — Key Principles (Distilled)

### The Foundation

- **Mass Desire = "the public spread of a private want."** Advertising channels existing desire, never creates it.
- **Two-product reality:** Physical product (what you deliver) vs. Functional product (what it DOES for people). People buy the functional product.
- **Mechanism matters in sophisticated markets.** Skincare is Stage 4-5 sophistication (mechanism competition + customer resistance). You need ONE of: New Mechanism, New Information, or New Identity. We're using New Mechanism (tallow as skin-oil structural match) + New Information (skin makes oil, after 40 makes less, water can't replace oil).

### The 5 Avatar Categories (in order of importance)

| # | Category | What it is | Strength |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Desires** | "I want X / I need Y" statements | Strongest. Always start here. |
| 2 | **Experiences** | Situations or product-trial events | Very strong. Unites people who "been through the same thing." |
| 3 | **Emotions** | How people FEEL about experiences/desires | Strong. Bypasses logic. Primary (6) + Secondary (infinite). |
| 4 | **Behaviours** | Patterns of action people develop | Strong. Tied to ego/self-identity. |
| 5 | **Demographics** | Static facts (age, gender, location) | Weakest. Use LAST, not first. |

### Key Rules

1. **Start with Desire, NEVER demographic.** Demographics-led avatars alienate buyers based on logistics.
2. **More categories combined = more specific Sub-Avatar.** Specificity matters more when product/brand/offer is less differentiated.
3. **Core Avatar = built from one category.** Sub-Avatar = Core Avatar narrowed with more categories.
4. **Beliefs are derivative** of Experience + Emotion + Behaviour. Not a separate category.
5. **At $0-100k/month, ONE Core Desire, many Sub-Avatars.** Focus and depth compound.

### The Avatar-Market Sophistication Gap

Even with perfect Avatar + clear Desire + product that delivers, you can FAIL to communicate at the sophistication level. This is what kills most ads in modern ecom.

The fix: pick a Core Desire, drill into Sub-Avatars with all 4 non-demographic categories, deploy strategic response (New Mechanism / New Information / New Identity).

## Audit Findings

### What's RIGHT with current strategy (per Origins)

1. **The Invisible Woman is a Desire-based Core Avatar, not a demographic-based one.** Origins says do this. We do this.
2. **All 4 validated converters are Sub-Avatars built with multiple categories.** Origins says go specific. We go specific.
3. **All Sub-Sub-Avatars in development (B12-B15) extend the Invisible Woman Core Desire.** Origins says ONE Core Desire at $0-100k/month. We do this for the active work.
4. **Two-product framing is correctly applied.** Identity-level payoffs close on functional outcomes ("being seen," "not-checking," "not-carrying"), not physical features ("soft cheeks").
5. **New Mechanism + New Information are deployed correctly.** Tallow-as-skin-oil structural match (New Mechanism) + skin-makes-oil-water-can't-replace-it (New Information) is exactly the strategic response Stage 4-5 markets require.

### What's MISALIGNED with Origins

1. **The 11-avatar roster mixes different Core Desires.** Origins recommends ONE Core Desire at our scale. We list 11 Sub-Avatars across at least 5 different Core Desires (be seen, condition relief, family wellness, eating philosophy consistency, functional recovery).
2. **Some avatars are demographics-led, not desire-led.** Postpartum Mom, Tween Daughter's Mom, Working Hands Man are all framed primarily by demographic + life-stage, with the underlying Desire less clear.
3. **Some avatars are conditions-led or beliefs-led.** Eczema Fighter (condition), Clean Eater (belief). Per Origins, beliefs are derivative; conditions can be Experience-based Sub-Avatars but only if the Core Desire is clear.
4. **The 6 hypothesis avatars (Tier 1 + Tier 2) have not been built using all 5 categories.** Many of them have just an Experience or Demographic identifier without the full Desire + Emotion + Behaviour layering.

## Recommendations

### Strategic — Lock the Core Desire

Per Origins ($0-100k/month rule), focus on ONE Core Desire and build Sub-Avatars around it. The Core Desire that's working:

**"I want to be seen again."**

This is the desire that all 4 validated converters tap into. Identity payoffs all return to it:
- B5C1: "It's the Tuesday. Someone who's been looking at your face for twenty-six years looks up and notices."
- B7C1: "She wasn't doing less. She was doing the right thing. I'm doing the right thing now too."
- B9C3: "Not the soft skin. The not-carrying."
- B3C2: "Stop refilling the bucket. Get the right bucket." (slight outlier; less relational)

The Core Desire is Status + Relationships (per Origins desire-power-ranking, these are #2 and #3 most powerful drivers).

### Tactical — Reorganize the roster

**KEEP as active Sub-Avatars (under Invisible Woman Core Desire):**

- Husband Noticed (B5C1 — converter)
- Mother's Photo / Pond's Daughter (B7C1 — converter)
- One Jar / Object Discovery (B9C3 — converter)
- Sebum Mechanism / Anger (B3C2 — converter, weakest)
- Photo Avoider (B13)
- Retinol Burnout (B14)
- Hairdresser / Professional Witness (B12C1, parked but valid)
- Pond's Nostalgia Reframe (B12C2, parked but valid)
- Daughter Sent It (B12C3, parked but valid)
- Sister Visit (B15C1)
- Group Text (B15C2)
- Old Notebook (B15C3)

**PARK (different Core Desires — return when Invisible Woman is fully validated):**

- Working Hands Man — different Core Desire (functional hand recovery). Park.
- Homesteader Mom — already killed for non-conversion. Stay parked.
- Eczema Fighter — different Core Desire (condition relief). Park.
- Postpartum Mom — demographics-led + different Core Desire. Park.
- Tween Daughter's Mom — demographics-led + different Core Desire (parental boundary). Park.

**RE-FRAME or KILL (questionable as standalone avatars):**

- Overnight Changer — currently Experience-only. If we want to keep it, re-frame as Sub-Avatar of Invisible Woman with the experience as "skin changed fast, not gradually" + the Be-Seen Desire.
- Product Graveyard — currently Experience-only. Same fix: re-frame as Sub-Avatar of Invisible Woman with the experience as "tried 10+ products none worked."
- Chemical Refugee — currently Behaviour-led. Underlying Desire may be Health (safety) which is different from Be-Seen. If kept, needs the Desire layer added.
- Clean Eater — Origins says beliefs are derivative. This needs to be re-derived as Experience + Emotion + Behaviour, OR killed.
- Carnivore Woman — Behaviour-led (eating philosophy). May or may not share the Be-Seen Desire. Needs the Desire layer added or park.

### Operational — When to revisit parked avatars

Per Origins: "As a brand, you EARN the right to go broader. The lower the revenue, the MORE specific you need to be."

Threshold for revisiting parked avatars:
- 10+ converters across 5+ different Sub-Avatars of Invisible Woman → enough validation to test a different Core Desire
- OR sustained 2+ ROAS for 30 days on the Invisible Woman Core → financial runway to test new avatars

Until then, **lock the Core Desire to "I want to be seen again."** Do not split focus.
