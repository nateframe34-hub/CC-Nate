# Sub-Avatar Language Validation Report (Claude Chrome, 2026-07-06)

**Source:** live Reddit + Amazon research run against the 7 rebuilt Brand_Mastery sub-avatars. Verbatim quotes with sources. The researcher explicitly flagged thin evidence rather than stretching, treat the thin flags as findings.

## Power ranking (evidence-based)
1. **The Tired-Looking** - very high scope, high urgency, highest staying power. The exact idiom "this is just my face" recurred VERBATIM across unrelated threads spanning ~7 years, as close to a fixed cultural phrase as research finds. Anchor avatar confirmed.
2. **The Product Graveyard** - very high scope (entire subreddit genres), dollar-figure urgency ("$860 in 6 months," "$165 + tax down the drain"), high staying power.
3. **The Unnoticed** - high scope in relationship spaces, rawest emotional language of any avatar ("I'm either completely invisible to him or he genuinely can't come up with a different adjective"), payoff language found ("It sort of surprises me so much when he does that I don't believe him").
4. **The Rough-Handed** - strong in trade/hobby niches, real physical pain (split/bleeding), tool-not-treat tone confirmed ("doesn't smell all foo foo like you borrowed your wife's hand goo," "if it's good enough for an udder"), seasonal staying power.
5. **The Label Reader** - moderate, overlaps heavily with Product Graveyard in real speech (same people count dollars AND self-identify as "ingredients nazis"). May function as ONE audience with Graveyard, not two.
6. **The Sun-Lived** - THIN in our specified emotional register. Sun-damage discourse online is dominated by clinical fix-it vocabulary (lasers, Sofwave, hydroquinone), not "softness back + resignation." The register is underserved (opportunity) but must be BUILT, not mirrored, and needs primary research (interviews/survey), not more Reddit mining.
7. **The Tight-by-Noon** - weakest verified. The pain is real (tight/pilling/cakey language everywhere) but no recurring clock-time idiom or purse-ritual language found publicly. Needs primary research before building a hook on it. NOTE: this avatar still has the account's own CONVERSION evidence behind it (B19C2 converted on the mechanism, habit-cessation language came from our own copy), so it stays valid, it just lacks public verbatim ammunition.

## Hook-ready verbatim lines (top 3 avatars)
**Tired-Looking:** "Nope, this is just my face." / "I just look tired all the time, even when I'm not." / "So annoying. I hate getting asked if I'm tired, NO it's my face."
**Product Graveyard:** "All in all, only $165 + tax down the drain." / "God I always forget just how fucking expensive skin care is." / "I don't even want to count how many new products I've bought and tried. Well over 30, I would think."
**Unnoticed:** "The dress was nice, but I guess I was hoping he'd notice me." / "I'm either completely invisible to him..." / "It sort of surprises me so much when he does that I don't believe him."

## Language patterns per avatar (their words)
- Tired-Looking: "this is just my face," "even when I'm not," "eye bags," "dull skin," "bad lighting," "you look tired/sick"
- Product Graveyard: dollar-and-month counts, "tried everything," "graveyard," "throw money at it," "waste less money"
- Unnoticed: "invisible," "notice me," "stopped trying," "the last time," "feel seen"
- Label Reader: "can't pronounce," "simple ingredients," "ingredients nazi," "skeptical... pleasantly surprised"
- Rough-Handed: "working hands," "cracked/split/bleed," "takes a beating," "not greasy," barnyard analogies
- Before-state blame for Tired-Looking: they blame LIGHTING and their face itself, not their skin ("just tried to tell myself it was bad lighting"), which means the ad's job includes the reveal that it was skin all along, a New Information play

## Surprises (all four matter)
1. **A direct objection to whipped tallow exists:** "whipping tallow is a cheap, amateur way of improving the texture... A 2 oz jar is gone in a couple weeks!" Value-skepticism vector. Counter-ammunition already exists (our own P.S. line "one jar lasts about ten weeks," review language "a little goes miles"), but the objection should be pressure-tested in presell/FAQ copy deliberately.
2. **Sun-damage discourse is owned by the clinical world.** The Sun-Lived emotional register we specified doesn't exist in public language yet, it would have to be created by the ad itself. Higher risk, potentially higher reward, defer until primary research.
3. **Graveyard + Label Reader are one audience in practice.** Messaging can treat "spent a fortune on products" and "can't pronounce the ingredients" as sequential beats for the same person rather than separate targets.
4. **Possible 8th sub-avatar: The Ingredient Guardian** (buys clean for their kids/family, not themselves; ingredient anxiety is more urgent as a caretaker). Logged as a candidate; relevant only if/when family/kid-safe positioning is on the table.

## Honesty notes from the researcher
Tight-by-Noon and Sun-Lived need dedicated primary research (customer interviews or a survey of the existing 9+ buyers) rather than more public-web mining. Several Amazon quotes use medical condition language (eczema/dermatitis/psoriasis relief), flagged as context only, NOT usable in ad copy.
