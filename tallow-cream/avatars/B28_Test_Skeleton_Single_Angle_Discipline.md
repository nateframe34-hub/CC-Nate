# B28 Test Skeleton: Single-Angle Discipline + CPM Fixes

**Date:** 2026-07-15
**Status:** Skeleton/plan only. No ad copy drafted yet.

## The hypothesis

**Founder's theory:** B17C1 doesn't run one angle, it runs five angle deployments across four sub-avatars (SA2 hook, SA2 mechanism, SA1 mid-beat, SA4 habit-cessation beat, SA3 close, mapped precisely in `B17C1_B26_Structural_CPM_Audit.md`). A multi-angle ad gives Meta's delivery system a blurrier "who is this for" signal than a single-angle ad would. To convert, Meta has to find someone who resonates with the fourteen-products frustration AND the tight-by-noon habit AND the being-unnoticed ache all at once, that's a narrower true intersection than any one desire alone, even though the STORY reads as one coherent narrative to a human. A single-angle ad should be easier for Meta to match against a broad, legible population, which should lower CPM.

**Why this is plausible, not just a guess:** it's consistent with the account's own B23 finding that Meta's purchase-optimized pricing responds to predicted BUYING INTENT, not just engagement or story quality. A cleaner, more legible intent signal (one clear desire, consistently reinforced start to finish) is a buying-intent signal. A blended signal is a murkier one. This doesn't contradict the B23 lesson, it extends it: B23 showed bold/generic-appeal imagery confuses intent-prediction, this theory says angle-mixing may do the same thing at the copy level.

**What this test can prove or disprove:** if single-angle cells deliver meaningfully cheaper than B17C1's $210-314 baseline while still converting, angle-mixing is a real CPM tax and every future ad should be written single-angle from now on. If they don't, the CPM problem lives elsewhere (audience cost, classification, stale-object decay, per the other findings already logged) and angle count isn't the lever.

## What's changing, and why

| Change | Why |
|---|---|
| **Each cell carries exactly ONE sub-avatar's desire, start to finish.** Hook, mid-beat, habit-cessation moment, and close are all written FROM that one angle, not borrowed from another. | Direct test of the founder's theory. B17C1's mixing happens because its mid-beat (SA4) and close (SA3) are literally OTHER angles' signature material grafted onto an SA2-led ad. This test removes the graft. |
| **No stated age number in any cell.** Age conveyed through context (grown kids, decades-long friendships, career length) instead of "I'm 48." | Second-strongest untested lever per the CPM audit's swing assessment, directional support already in the data (B26C3, the only cell with no age number, is the cheapest B26 cell). Free to bundle into this batch since it's a new build anyway. |
| **All 3 cells are brand-new ad objects.** | Gets the fresh-object reset for free, the single largest CPM swing already demonstrated in the account's own history (B17C1's 7/5 relaunch: $94-135 CPM vs its $210-314 drifted baseline). Without this, a cheap CPM read could just be fresh-object effect misattributed to single-angle discipline. |
| **Section-break formatting (`-------------`) kept IDENTICAL across all 3 cells.** | Not a variable in this test. Changing it here would confound the angle-count question with the divider question from the CPM audit's finding #3. Test that separately, later, if this batch doesn't fully explain the gap. |
| **No villain-beat industry-blame language in any cell.** | Not because it's proven costly (the audit's own falsification check found B17C1 has none and is still the most expensive ad), but because it's cheap to exclude and keeps this batch as clean a single-angle test as possible, no reason to reintroduce an untested variable into a test designed to isolate a different one. |

## What stays locked (the proven STRUCTURE, not the angle content)

Per `Converter_Pattern_Synthesis.md`, these are structural elements shared by every converter regardless of which desire they carry, and this test is not touching them: Solution-Aware framing, accidental discovery (never sought out), peer-adjacent non-medical authority, water-vs-oil mechanism at 50-60% of body length, day-numbered progressive transformation (Day 1/4/9/14/21 shape), skeptic-convert beat ("I made the same face"), 1,400-1,600 words, 10-element density, full compliance audit, 6th-grade readability. The test variable is angle COUNT and content, not the container these angles are written into.

## Per-cell skeleton

### B28C1 - SA2 The Product Graveyard, single-angle

- **Hook:** the fourteen-things/one-jar contrast. Locked headline: "One jar. Fourteen down."
- **Mechanism:** SA2's own Angle 2 ("why everything you tried didn't work"), water-first ingredient reveal. Native to this angle, no change needed.
- **Mid-beat / habit-cessation, REWRITTEN to stay SA2-native:** NOT the travel-packets beat (that's SA4's signature material). Instead, something inside the Graveyard desire itself: stopped restocking the drawer, stopped reading new product reviews/ads for alternatives, a friend asks what she uses now and she realizes she hasn't bought a "backup" in months. The habit being extinguished is BUYING-CYCLE behavior, not application-frequency behavior, keeps it inside SA2's "stop the cycle of buying and being disappointed" desire.
- **Close, REWRITTEN to stay SA2-native:** NOT the husband-table scene (that's SA3's signature material). Instead, return to the graveyard/inventory image: she looks at her now near-empty counter, or tells someone else about the switch from fourteen to one, or someone asks to look at her "routine" and she has nothing to show them. Payoff is inventory-based, not relational-noticing-based.

### B28C2 - SA3 The Unnoticed, single-angle

- **Hook, REWRITTEN (this is the real test of `Brand_Mastery.md`'s "poor hook" flag):** open on the specific remembered LAST time she was noticed, or the absence of it, not the bathroom-counter object-discovery scene (that's SA2's signature material). Candidate direction: a specific dated memory of the last unprompted compliment, then the long silence since.
- **Mechanism:** same water-vs-oil reveal, delivered through whatever peer-adjacent authority character this cell uses, no change to the mechanism content itself.
- **Mid-beat / habit-cessation, REWRITTEN to stay SA3-native:** NOT the travel-packets beat. Instead, something inside the noticing/being-seen desire: she stops flinching or bracing before a mirror/photo, stops deflecting when someone starts to compliment her, catches herself NOT performing the self-deprecating deflection she used to do automatically. The habit being extinguished is a DEFENSIVE/deflecting behavior tied to not expecting to be seen, not a product-restocking or reapplication behavior.
- **Close:** the husband-table noticing scene. This IS SA3's own proven close, keep it, it's not a graft here, it's native.

### B28C3 - SA4 The Tight-by-Noon, single-angle

- **Hook:** the daily-repetition problem, "tight by ten, cracked by two, reapply, repeat." Candidate: "Apply at 7am. Still working at 2pm." or a scene built around one specific losing Tuesday.
- **Mechanism:** same water-vs-oil reveal, framed through the daily-maintenance-burden lens rather than the buying-cycle lens.
- **Mid-beat / habit-cessation:** the travel-packets-in-three-places beat. This IS SA4's own signature material (per `Brand_Mastery.md`'s own listed hook for SA4 Angle 2), keep it, it's native here, not a graft.
- **Close, REWRITTEN to stay SA4-native:** NOT the husband-table scene (SA3's signature material). Instead, something inside the maintenance-fatigue desire resolving: she gets through a full day without thinking about her skin once, someone asks what her "routine" is now and the honest answer is "nothing, that's the point," or she realizes she hasn't reapplied anything in weeks and didn't notice she'd stopped checking. Payoff is the ABSENCE of maintenance, not a relational-noticing moment.

## Success criteria

1. **Primary read: CPM and CPC vs B17C1's current baseline ($210-314 CPM, $2.51-2.97 CPC).** If any single-angle cell holds materially below that range at comparable CTR, the single-angle theory has real support.
2. **Secondary read: CPA/ROAS vs B17C1's lifetime $31.30 CPA / 1.60x ROAS.** A cell that's cheap to deliver but doesn't convert isn't a win, cost and conversion both need to hold.
3. **Cross-cell read: which single angle converts best,** the original B28 purpose, still fully intact under this stricter version.
4. **Isolation discipline, unchanged from the original briefs:** 3 separate ad sets, own budgets, never sharing a CBO with B17C1 or with each other until each has a real, uncontested read.

---

## What this test does NOT resolve on its own

If CPM comes down, this test can't fully separate "single angle" from "fresh object" as the cause, both are baked in simultaneously by design (see the fresh-object row in the changes table above). If a longer-run follow-up is needed to isolate them individually (single-angle on a stale object vs. multi-angle on a fresh object), that's a future test, not this one, flagged here so it isn't forgotten if the results come back ambiguous.
