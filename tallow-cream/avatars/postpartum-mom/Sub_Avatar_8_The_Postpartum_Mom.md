## What Makes This Sub-Avatar Different

The postpartum mom is the ONLY avatar where the product must be safe for a SECOND PERSON (the baby). Every other avatar is evaluating the product against her own skin. This avatar evaluates it against her baby's face pressed against her neck during nursing.

This dual-safety requirement is a completely different purchase calculus:
- The baby might taste it if her mother's chin brushes the baby's lips
- The baby will absolutely touch her mother's face with her fingers, then put those fingers in her mouth
- The baby's head is against her mother's neck for hours a day
- ANY product has to pass the "would I be okay if my baby ingested a tiny amount?" test

This is why tallow is radically well-suited. "Four ingredients: beef tallow, honey, olive oil, calendula" is a label she can hand to her pediatrician. Nothing needs explaining.

---

## The Gap

Products marketed as "pregnancy-safe" (Beautycounter, Earth Mama, Burt's Bees) are often still 15-25 ingredient formulations with emulsifiers, preservatives, and "natural fragrance" she doesn't fully trust. Products marketed for BABY (Mustela, Aquaphor Baby, Weleda Baby) are marketed to the baby, not the mom — the mom feels weird putting "baby cream" on her own face.

**The gap is:** there is no product marketed as "safe enough for your baby to touch, nourishing enough for your postpartum skin." Tallow bridges both sides — radically safe AND radically nourishing. But no brand is positioning it this way. They're positioning tallow for anti-aging (Invisible Woman), for clean beauty (Chemical Refugee), or for homesteaders (Sub-Avatar 6). Postpartum is sitting empty.
