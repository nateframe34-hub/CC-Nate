## The one register, three entry points

Per the research, this is ONE avatar with one desire (comfort/tending for drug-dried face and neck skin) and one register (trade-honoring: proud of the loss, tending the cost, never asking to be fixed). The three angles below are different SCENES into the same register, not different desires, same model as B26's Gardeners/Walkers/Bleacher Parent sharing one Sun-Lived desire.

### Angle A: The Compliment That Stings (lead angle, strongest research support)
- **Scene:** someone she loves says the thing that means well and lands wrong. Research-grounded options: a family member calling her face "gaunt and tired," a version of "you have ozempic face" said by someone close, or Mark's own version of "you look tired" that she has to sit with.
- **Reason to buy:** not fixing what they said, just refusing to let it be true when she looks in the mirror herself. Comfort as the answer to someone else's careless word.
- **Awareness:** Problem Aware (she knows something changed) to Solution Unaware (assumed nothing but a procedure could touch it).
- **Hook direction:** "My own mother said I looked tired. I wasn't tired. I'd just lost 40 pounds and nobody warned me what my neck would do."

### Angle B: The Sunburn That Won't Heal (most vivid sensory language, most honest claim fit)
- **Scene:** the actual physical sensation, tight/burning/dry, that multiple research sources independently called "sunburn" without ever having been in the sun. This is the ONE zone where hydration is the fully honest, uncontested answer (claim boundary confirmed: hydration/barrier claims are never challenged in this community).
- **Reason to buy:** direct sensory relief for skin that feels sunburnt for no reason, no laxity/tightening promise needed or made.
- **Awareness:** Problem Aware, mid-loss, present-tense ("what do I do about this right now," the louder/larger on-drug population per research).
- **Hook direction:** "My face has felt sunburnt for three months and I haven't been in the sun once."

### Angle C: The Trade I'd Make Again (identity/close angle, ties the whole avatar together)
- **Scene:** the "would you do it again" reckoning, landing on the community's own dominant verdict, 100% worth it, paired with the neck/turkey-neck zone specifically (research's #1 complaint zone, PDP-compatible).
- **Reason to buy:** this isn't about undoing the trade, it's tending the part of the trade nobody warned her about.
- **Awareness:** Solution Aware to Product Aware, works best as the CLOSE register for A or B rather than a standalone lead (mirrors how SA3 Unnoticed functions in this account, a close, not a hook).
- **Hook direction:** "I'd lose the weight again tomorrow. I just wish someone had told me what to do about my neck while I did it."

## The claim script (non-negotiable, from research Section 3)

**Say freely:** hydration, comfort, barrier, softness, "the tight/dry/sunburnt feeling," fat-based moisture that holds through a day, tending, evening/nightly care.
**Say once, deliberately, as the trust move:** something in the shape of "nothing in a jar tightens skin, and if someone tells you otherwise they're selling you something. This isn't that." This sentence is not hedging, per the research it is THE highest-trust move available in this specific community.
**Never say:** fixes, reverses, restores collagen, tightens, lifts, firms, "for Ozempic/GLP-1 skin," any packaging or targeting language that names the drug as the product's audience. The story does the targeting. The product stays a plain 4-ingredient balm.

## Body part order
