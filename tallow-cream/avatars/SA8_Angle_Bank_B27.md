## The claim script (non-negotiable, from research Section 3)

**Say freely:** hydration, comfort, barrier, softness, "the tight/dry/sunburnt feeling," fat-based moisture that holds through a day, tending, evening/nightly care.
**Say once, deliberately, as the trust move:** something in the shape of "nothing in a jar tightens skin, and if someone tells you otherwise they're selling you something. This isn't that." This sentence is not hedging, per the research it is THE highest-trust move available in this specific community.
**Never say:** fixes, reverses, restores collagen, tightens, lifts, firms, "for Ozempic/GLP-1 skin," any packaging or targeting language that names the drug as the product's audience. The story does the targeting. The product stays a plain 4-ingredient balm.
