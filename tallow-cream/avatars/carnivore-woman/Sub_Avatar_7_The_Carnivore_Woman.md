## What Makes This Sub-Avatar Different

She is the MOST philosophically pre-sold customer in the entire project. Unlike every prior sub-avatar, she does not need to be convinced that:
- Animal products are healthy
- Seed oils are a problem
- Simple ingredients are better than complex ones
- Grass-fed/pasture-raised sourcing matters
- Traditional food practices are worth preserving

She is already there. The Homesteader Mom is *philosophically* pre-sold but needs cultural recognition. The Carnivore Woman is *both* philosophically pre-sold AND culturally self-identified. The ad's job is the smallest it could possibly be: *show her this product exists and fits her existing framework.*

**The "seed oil" vocabulary is her signature.** If an ad uses "seed oil free" or names the category of seed oils she avoids, she recognizes the brand as one of hers immediately.

---

## The Gap

She already eats nose-to-tail. She takes beef organ capsules. She uses grass-fed butter in her coffee. And then she stands at the bathroom sink and applies a moisturizer that contains — most often — sunflower oil, safflower oil, or soybean oil. The seed oils she eliminated from her diet are still on her face.

**The gap is:** Primal Queen has proven the economics of selling animal-based health products direct-to-consumer to this avatar. But Primal Queen sells ingestibles (liver capsules, bone marrow), not topicals. No brand has specifically claimed the "carnivore-native tallow skincare" position. Toups & Co is close but speaks homesteader, not carnivore. The position is literally empty.

## Angles & Hooks (HYPOTHESIS — To Be Validated via Research)

### Angle 1: The Seed Oil on Your Face ⭐ HYPOTHESIZED STRONGEST
**Reason to Buy:** She cut seed oils out of her diet. Her skincare is still full of them. Name the gap. Offer the fix.

**Hooks (inferred):**

### Angle 2: Nose-to-Tail Skincare ⭐ HYPOTHESIZED SECOND
**Reason to Buy:** Whole-animal philosophy extends beyond food. Tallow is the skincare version of liver capsules — using more of the animal, wasting less.

**Hooks (inferred):**
- "Nose-to-tail isn't just a diet. It's a philosophy. Your skincare is the next step."
- "I've been eating the whole animal for three years. This is the first thing I've found that lets me use more of it."

### Angle 3: The Ancestral Reframe
**Reason to Buy:** Her ancestors ate and moisturized from the same animal. Modern separation of food and skincare is the aberration, not the norm.

**Hooks (inferred):**
- "Your great-grandmother rendered tallow for Sunday dinner AND for her hands. We split the two somewhere along the way."
- "Beef fat is not a trend. It's what your body recognizes because it's what your ancestors used."
