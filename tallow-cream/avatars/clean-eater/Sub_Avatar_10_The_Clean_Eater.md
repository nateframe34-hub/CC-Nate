## What Makes This Sub-Avatar Different — And The Risk

She is the most HOOK-DEFINED of the proposed avatars. Her central identity ("I eat clean") is less culturally distinct than Homesteader ("I homestead"), Carnivore ("I am carnivore"), or Postpartum ("I just had a baby"). This is the research risk: **she may not be a distinct avatar at all.**

Two possible outcomes of the deep research:

**Outcome A: She is a distinct avatar.**
She has her own communities (fitness + nutrition + wellness), her own specific Meta audience classification, her own creator ecosystem, her own purchase behavior. In this case, research confirms and we write Batch 12 for her.

**Outcome B: She is really an ANGLE that works across multiple avatars.**
The "cut the BS out of your diet" hook is strong but it's actually the same hook that works for Carnivore Women (tighter audience), Homesteader Moms (already validated), and even some Postpartum Moms. In this case, research concludes that "clean eater" is better served as a cross-avatar messaging angle than as a standalone targeting avatar.

**The research needs to answer specifically:** does this woman have a distinct community ecosystem and purchase pattern that differs from the other four avatars, or is she their intersection?

## The Gap (Preliminary)

Clean beauty brands (Beautycounter, ILIA, Osea) target her broadly but don't speak her specific food-to-skincare coherence language. Carnivore brands (Primal Queen, Heart & Soil) speak the food philosophy but exclude the non-carnivore clean eater. Homesteader brands (Toups & Co) require a farming identity she may not have.

**The gap (if she's a distinct avatar):** a brand that speaks the food-to-skincare coherence specifically without requiring a lifestyle identity she doesn't have. She's not a homesteader. She's not a carnivore. She's not a new mom. She's a woman who eats real food and wants her face products to match.

## Angles & Hooks (HYPOTHESIS — To Be Validated via Research)

### Angle 1: The Processed BS on Your Face ⭐ HYPOTHESIZED STRONGEST (and potentially the entire avatar's reason to exist)
**Reason to Buy:** Names the hypocrisy she's felt but not articulated — her food is clean, her skincare isn't.

**Hooks (inferred):**
- "You cut the processed BS out of your diet. Why are you still putting it on your skin?"
- "I stopped eating seed oils two years ago. It took me another year to realize they were all over my face."
- "Your kitchen is clean. Your face isn't. Something's off about that."

### Angle 2: The Grass-Fed Standard
**Reason to Buy:** Extends her food-sourcing vocabulary to skincare. "Grass-fed" as a criterion for what she EATS applies to what she puts ON her body too.

**Hooks (inferred):**
- "I spent three years sourcing grass-fed meat for my family. I'd never sourced grass-fed anything for my skin."
- "Your food is grass-fed. Your moisturizer should be too."

### Angle 3: The Ingredient Label You Can Read
**Reason to Buy:** The simplification thesis, applied through her label-reading discipline.

**Hooks (inferred):**
- "I read every ingredient on everything my kids eat. I finally read the label on my night cream. I had a lot to unlearn."
- "Four ingredients. Same standard as my breakfast."
