# The Sun-Lived: Primary Language Research (Claude Chrome, 2026-07-07)

**Why this research happened:** `SubAvatar_Validation_Report_2026-07-06.md` flagged SA6's specified emotional register ("earned-it pride tangled with resignation") as THIN in general skincare/sun-damage spaces, dominated by clinical fix-it vocabulary instead, and recommended targeted primary research rather than more general mining. This is that research: ~25 searches across r/gardening, r/homestead, r/farming, r/sailing, r/RVLiving, iRV2, Cruisers Forum, r/golf, r/GenX, r/Ranching, Twitter/X, and Amazon reviews (sun hats, UPF shirts, gardening gloves).

## The honest finding: the hypothesized fused register doesn't exist

Pride and resignation, in one voice, about one's own skin, did not turn up as a naturally-occurring pattern anywhere searched, including these more targeted spaces. Instead, three separate, non-overlapping registers exist:

1. **Short, funny, pure pride, decoupled from resignation.** "Wear it as a badge of honor," "earned the hard way," "proud owner of a terrific farmer's tan." Comes from people still mid-lifestyle (active farmers, golfers, ag-Twitter), often performing identity for an audience.
2. **Resignation fused with REGRET, not pride.** "We were so stupid," "I wish I could go back," "not worth it," sometimes alongside melanoma/medical language. Comes from a different population entirely, people who tanned recreationally/vainly in youth, not people who worked outside for decades.
3. **Silent functional protection, no emotional register at all.** The actual working-outdoors population (farmers, ranchers, boaters, landscapers) essentially doesn't narrate their own skin in first person online. When their skin gets described, it's almost always someone else doing it, in third person ("hands like leather," "built different").

**Regret register (do not use, different population, adjacent to medical claims):**
- "We were so stupid. And now, I have moles and dark spots everywhere because all the sunscreen in the world wont help me anymore." - r/SkincareAddiction, "50 years working in the sun" thread
- "If I could turn back time, I would never have tanned... Never again." - r/GenX
- "I have melanoma... my body is now covered in scars..." - r/GenX

**Functional/protective (majority of what was found, no emotion attached):**
- "long sleeves are even better! No reapplication" - r/sailing
- "Zinc-based sunscreen... put it on well before you need to be in the sun" - r/sailing
- Amazon reviews for sun hats/UPF shirts/gloves: almost entirely transactional ("perfect solution for the yard," "saved my hands from pricklers"), explaining need in clinical/practical terms (fair skin, skin cancer history, daily yard work), never narrative or reflective.

**Hand-specific:** thinner than expected. Gardening threads reference hands for injury/dryness/calluses, not sun aging. The one vivid image found, "hands like leather," was consistently third-person (describing a grandfather, a coworker), never self-descriptive.

## What communities were unusable
r/farming (skin references are almost entirely about animal hides and produce), r/RVLiving/iRV2 ("skin"/"wrinkles" hits are about fiberglass and vinyl siding), Cruisers Forum (relevant thread blocked by bot-verification). Facebook parent groups and Escapees forums were inaccessible without login, a real gap, not a finding.

## Recommendation: pivot the angle, don't force the fusion

Drop "resignation" from the brief entirely, it's the wrong population's language and it sits next to real compliance risk (melanoma/medical adjacency). Lead with the PRIDE register instead: badge-of-honor, self-deprecating, dry humor, exactly the account's established voice. Reframe the product benefit as comfort/softness for skin that's earned its stripes, a functional claim, not a repair/reverse claim, which keeps this fully inside the compliance lane `Brand_Mastery.md` already set for this avatar ("functional/experiential language only, no repair/reverse/fade claims").
