# Big-Swing Council — 2-10x Moves While Waiting

**Date:** 2026-05-21
**Method:** 5 in-house agents (4 generative big-swing proposers + 1 dedicated objector). Goal held constant: profitable ROAS + max sales. The founder explicitly rejected incremental ("5% booster") thinking and asked for 2-10x candidates. The objector's job was to kill the weak ones.

---

| # | Lens | Proposal | Claimed upside |

| 1 | Channel Disruptor | **Pivot to Amazon** (FBA + Brand Registry + Vine + Sponsored Products) | $500K-$1M in 12 months |
| 2 | Offer Architect | **Subscription-first ("Skin Cooperators Club")** — $34/mo default, $59.99 one-time anchor | 2-4x ATC + 2.8x LTV = 5-10x compound |

| 4 | Audience Pivot | **Tattoo aftercare** — same product as parallel "Ink Balm" SKU, artist distribution channel | $300K-$1M, deadline-driven high-intent buyer |

## The Objector's Verdict (and how much of it holds)

The objector hit all four with overlapping attacks:

1. **Capital constraint.** Three of four ($4-7K for Amazon, $2-5K for ancestral creator seeding, $4-8K for tattoo sampling) require cash the founder doesn't have — he just had a car repair eat the ad budget.
2. **Splits founder attention.** Each adds a new front while the existing one is unfinished (pixel unverified, BOGO unbuilt, PDP banned-phrase risk, ban-pattern unresolved).
3. **Abandons validated assets.** 3 of 4 throw out (or dilute) the 4 converters, 11 sub-avatar docs, B15+B16 written ads, the v2 Core Desire work.
4. **Productive procrastination pattern.** Same red-team conclusion the prior two councils reached — "big swing" risks being the new "build avatar #23."

**Where the objector is fully right:** the capital constraint is real and binding right now. Amazon and Tattoo both need money the founder doesn't have. They die for that reason alone, regardless of strategic merit.

**Where the objector overreaches:** the "all four are escapism" framing flattens real differences between the proposals. The capital constraint applies asymmetrically.

| Proposal | Capital required to TEST | Survives objector's strongest attack? |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Amazon | $4-7K minimum | No — capital constraint kills it |
| 2. Subscription-first | **$0 incremental capital** (Shopify config + copy rewrites) | Partially — the *option* costs nothing, the *defaulting* changes cashflow |
| 3. Ancestral | $0 to test in copy + a single A/B ad | **Yes — testable for free inside B16** |
| 4. Tattoo aftercare | $4-8K (new SKU, samples) | No — capital + new SKU + occlusive-on-fresh-wound risk |

The objector's blanket "don't ship any of this" gets weaker when you separate "test the hypothesis cheaply" from "bet the brand on it." Three of four can be tested for less than a single B16 ad's daily budget.

## The Survivors (in order of cheapest-real-test)

### 1. ANCESTRAL POSITIONING TEST — the cheapest 2-10x hypothesis we can run

This is the one big swing that genuinely survives the capital constraint and the objector's "split attention" critique. Why:

- **The hypothesis has a quantifiable mechanism.** Ancestral-coded creative lands in Meta's *food/lifestyle/values* auction, not the beauty auction. CPM differential is real and observable ($40-90 typical for ancestral content brands vs $200-450 the cooked BM saw for women's-skincare). That's a 3-5x leverage event on cost alone, before any conversion change.
- **Zero new assets needed.** Same product, same 4 ingredients, same Julie partner page (Fort Collins is already pre-aligned demographically), same converter skeleton. The only change is framing.
- **Testable for free inside B16.** Add a fourth ad (B16C4) that tests an ancestral-framed hook ("I stopped seed oils on my face the same week I stopped them in my pan") against the three already written. Tracker tells us in 14 days whether the CPM escape thesis holds.
- **Objector's "political risk" critique is real but the test doesn't require committing.** If the ancestral hook outperforms on CPM but underperforms on conversion (left-coded buyer loss), kill it. If it wins on both, expand. The test costs $0 to find out.

**What this is NOT:** a full brand rebrand. It's a single ad-copy hypothesis tested cheaply against the locked converter skeleton. The objector's "this is a brand reset" critique applies to *committing* to ancestral, not to *testing* it.

### 2. SUBSCRIPTION-FIRST as an OPTION (not the default)

The objector killed "subscription-first as default" on cashflow grounds and was right — making it the only option subsidizes the broken funnel and worsens unit-1 economics. But adding subscription as a *secondary* option on the PDP costs nothing and lets the brand collect real data on whether sub-buyers exist for this product. The LTV hypothesis only gets tested if the offer is on the menu.

**Conditional survival:** Add a "subscribe & save 15%" option below the one-time-purchase as the SECONDARY option. Costs ~2 hours of Shopify config. If 10%+ of buyers choose subscription, the LTV play is real and can be made default later. If <5% do, kill it.

### 3. AMAZON — survives ONLY as a 90-day delayed move, not a now move

The objector is right that the capital doesn't exist today. But Amazon's strategic logic (search intent vs scroll, no ban risk on the BM platform that's burned the founder 3x, native Subscribe & Save infrastructure, ancestral/wellness keywords with real search volume) is the soundest pure-strategy of the four proposals — it just requires money. If B16 (with the foundation fixes + the ancestral test) produces a sustained 1.5x+ ROAS over 30 days, the resulting cashflow funds the Amazon entry as a Q3 move. **Park, don't kill.**

### 4. TATTOO AFTERCARE — dies

Capital + new SKU + the genuine occlusive-on-fresh-wound risk (Aquaphor and Hustle Butter aren't oil-based for a reason) compound. The objector won this one outright. The hypothesis is interesting but the execution risk for a 9-sale broke brand is fatal.

## The Meta-Insight the Council Surfaced

The most valuable thing this council produced isn't any one of the four proposals. It's the realization that **escaping the women's-skincare auction is itself a multi-x lever** — and three of four proposals (Amazon, Ancestral, Tattoo) are different routes to the same escape.

The brand has been bleeding budget at $200-450 CPMs *because of what it's classified as*, not because of the creative. Every previous council ranked this as a downstream symptom. This council surfaced it as a primary lever: there are paths to 1/3-1/5 the CPM without changing the product, by changing the auction classification through creative, channel, or audience.

The Ancestral test is the cheapest probe of that lever. Amazon is a different probe of the same lever (different auction entirely). Tattoo is a third probe (medical-adjacent vs beauty). The lever itself is real. The cheapest probe is the right next test.

---

## The Honest Synthesis

The objector's strongest case is also the dullest case: foundation first. He's right on that — pixel, BOGO, PDP fixes have to happen regardless of which big-swing path follows. **None of the big swings remove the foundation work; they sequence after it.**

But the user is also right that "fix the funnel and run B16" alone is a 5% optimization story, not a 2-10x story. The data ceiling at 9 lifetime sales doesn't tell us whether the existing strategy can hit profitable ROAS at scale — and if it can't, *no amount* of funnel fixes saves it.

The reconciliation:
- **Foundation work happens regardless** — it's required for any path to be legible
- **B16 ships as designed** — it tests the v2 Core Desire + narrowing thesis on the existing strategy
- **Add ONE extra ad (B16C4) with an ancestral-framed hook** — same skeleton, same product, just the auction-escape hypothesis tested cheaply alongside the rest
- **Add subscription as a PDP option** during the foundation work — 2 hours of config, measures whether the LTV play has demand
- **Park Amazon for 90 days** — re-evaluate after a stable 30-day run produces (or fails to produce) cashflow

That gives the founder TWO 2-10x hypotheses (ancestral CPM escape + subscription LTV demand) tested at near-zero incremental cost, while still doing the foundation work the objector correctly flagged as gating. Neither bets the brand. Both produce real signal in 14 days.
