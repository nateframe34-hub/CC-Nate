# SECTION 6 — Real Talk (Objection Handling)

**[H2 — Section Header]**

Questions you're already asking.

**[BODY — Q&A blocks, each question bold]**

**Does it smell like beef?**
No. The tallow is properly rendered and whipped with manuka honey. You'll smell honey and a subtle warmth — not a steakhouse. If you've tried cheap tallow that smelled off, this isn't that.

**Will it feel greasy?**
For about 2-3 minutes, yes. Then it absorbs completely. That's actually how you know it's working — your skin is pulling it in. Water-based lotions feel light because they evaporate. This feels rich because it stays.

**Will it break me out?**
If you have oily or acne-prone skin, tallow may not be for you. We're honest about that. This is made for dry, mature, sensitive, or damaged skin — the skin that drinks moisture and is dry again in an hour.

# SECTION 9 — FAQ

**[H2 — Section Header]**

Still have questions?

**[ACCORDION ITEMS]**

**How long does one jar last?**
2-3 months with daily use on face and neck. Longer if you use it only at night. A little goes a long way.

**Can I use this on my body too?**
Yes. Face, neck, hands, elbows, anywhere dry. Many customers use it head to toe.

**Is this safe for sensitive skin?**
Yes. 4 ingredients, no fragrance, no chemicals. Tallow is one of the most gentle moisturizers that exists. If your skin reacts to everything else, this is worth trying.

**What does it smell like?**
Warm honey with a subtle natural scent. It's not perfumed and it doesn't smell like meat. The manuka honey is the dominant note.

**Will this clog my pores?**
For dry, normal, or mature skin — no. Tallow has a comedogenic rating of 2 (low). However, if you're oily or acne-prone, we recommend patch testing first.

**Where is the tallow sourced?**
From grass-fed cattle. The tallow is combined with New Zealand manuka honey, cold-pressed olive oil, and calendula flower extract.

**How should I store it?**
Room temperature. It may firm up in cooler weather and soften in warmth — that's completely normal for a natural balm with no synthetic stabilizers.

**Can I use this with other skincare products?**
Yes, but most customers find they don't need to. That's kind of the point.
