# PDP Swipe Analysis — 11 Skincare Brands

> **Purpose:** Analyze competitor PDPs to determine what our tallow balm PDP needs, how to position the product, and what visual/copy patterns convert in skincare.
> **Date:** April 5, 2026

---

## BRAND-BY-BRAND BREAKDOWN

### 1. Evil Goods (Direct Competitor — #1 Tallow Brand)

**Hero Section:**
- Product photography: Jar with lid off showing whipped golden texture — the product IS the hero visual
- Warm yellow/gold color palette throughout the entire page
- Bold yellow CTA buttons
- Social proof counter near top ("385,000+ people switched")

**Product Title:** Not visible in detail but positioned as "World's BEST Beef Tallow Balm"

**Page Structure (top to bottom):**
1. Hero with jar + purchase options
2. "It's actually for you" — ingredient callout icons (lemon, honey, etc.)
3. Before/after transformation photos (real customers)
4. "Why Tallow?" education section with mechanism explanation
5. Ingredient breakdown with individual icons
6. UGC testimonial grid (real women's faces, not stock)
7. Video testimonials
8. Reviews section with star ratings
9. FAQ
10. Final CTA

**What they do well:**
- The ENTIRE page is warm gold/yellow — feels like honey, warmth, naturalness
- Real customer face photos everywhere — trust through faces, not text
- B&A photos are prominent and bold
- Product texture is shown extensively (whipped, golden, luxurious)
- Heavy UGC — the page feels like a community, not a storefront

**What they're missing:**
- Page is LONG and busy — could overwhelm
- No clear "who is this for" section
- No scent/texture objection handling (smell is the #1 complaint)
- No size comparison or "how long does it last" info

### 2. Primally Pure (Biggest Scaled Tallow Brand — $10.3M/mo)

**Hero Section:**
- Clean, minimal, earth tones (beige/cream/sage)
- Multiple product variants shown side by side (different scents)
- Lifestyle photo of woman applying product — aspirational, not clinical
- Tabbed content below: "Details / Ingredients / How to Use"

**Product Title:** "Squalane Mix" (or specific balm variant)

**Page Structure:**
1. Hero with product photos + purchase options + tabs
2. "How to Use" section with lifestyle photo
3. "TRIED+Trsted" trust badges (certifications)
4. "Frequently Bought Together" cross-sell module
5. Press/editorial quote
6. Product recommendations grid

**What they do well:**
- CLEANEST design of all 11 brands — white space, elegant, premium
- Tabs for Details/Ingredients/How to Use — keeps hero section tight
- Lifestyle photography (woman touching face, natural light) feels editorial, not commercial
- "Frequently Bought Together" is the bundle upsell done subtly
- Trust badges (certifications) are visual, not text-heavy

**What they're missing:**
- Almost NO social proof on the PDP itself (no reviews visible, no UGC)
- No "Why tallow?" education — assumes the buyer already knows
- No transformation stories or B&A
- Feels premium but COLD — no emotional connection
- No objection handling

## KEY PATTERNS ACROSS ALL 11 BRANDS

### What Every High-Converting PDP Has:
1. **Product texture shown** — not just the jar, but the cream/balm itself
2. **Lifestyle photos** of real application on real skin
3. **Reviews/stars visible early** — not buried below the fold
4. **Ingredient section** that's visual (icons), not text walls
5. **Cross-sell / "you may also like"** section
6. **Free shipping + guarantee** mentioned near CTA

### What Separates the Best from the Rest:
- **Evil Goods + Based:** UGC/real faces as social proof > text reviews
- **Drunk Elephant:** "What's In / What's Out" addresses fears directly
- **The Ordinary:** "Targets / Suited to" answers "is this for me?"
- **Toups & Co:** Shortest page, highest conversion — proves you don't need a novel
- **Glossier:** Diverse models = diverse avatars seeing themselves

### What NO Tallow Brand Does Well:
1. **Nobody handles the smell objection** on the PDP
2. **Nobody shows the product on women 45-60** — all photography is 25-35
3. **Nobody has a "Who is this for" section** targeting specific avatars
4. **Nobody educates on the sebum replacement mechanism** (the strongest selling point)
5. **Nobody addresses greasiness** — the #2 complaint

## WHAT OUR PDP NEEDS (Recommended Structure)

Based on what works and what's missing:

1. **Hero:** Product jar (lid off, showing whipped texture) + lifestyle photo of woman 45-55 applying it. Warm gold/cream palette. Star rating visible. Price with crossed-out anchor.

2. **"Is This For You?"** section — The Ordinary's "Targets/Suited to" adapted:
   - Best for: menopausal dryness, damaged barriers, sensitive skin, "tried everything"
   - Not for: oily/acne-prone skin (honesty builds trust)

3. **"What's In / What's Out"** — Drunk Elephant format:
   - IN: Grass-fed tallow, manuka honey, olive oil, calendula (4 ingredients)
   - OUT: Water, parabens, sulfates, synthetic fragrance, petroleum, mineral oil

4. **"Why Tallow?"** mechanism section — the sebum replacement education:
   - "Your skin makes an oil called sebum. After 40, production drops up to 60%. Tallow is 87% identical to your skin's sebum. That's why it absorbs — your skin recognizes it."

5. **Objection handling** — address smell + greasiness:
   - "Whipped for absorption — melts into skin in 2-3 minutes"
   - "Subtle honey scent — NOT the beefy smell of raw tallow"

6. **Transformation testimonials** with real language from our research

7. **60-day guarantee badge** near CTA — "No drama. No hoops."

8. **Bundle selector** — Starter / Ritual / Glow Kit

9. **Reviews** section

10. **"You May Also Like"** cross-sell

## COLOR DIRECTION

Based on what works in this space:

| Brand | Palette | Feeling |
|---|---|---|
| Evil Goods | Warm yellow/gold | Honey, warmth, natural |
| Primally Pure | Cream/beige/sage | Clean, premium, editorial |
| Toups & Co | Warm cream/tan | Earthy, simple, trustworthy |
| Based | Black/gold | Luxury, masculine (wrong for us) |
| Lady May | Soft green/cream | Gentle, natural, approachable |

**Our direction:** Warm cream base + honey gold accents. Similar to Evil Goods' warmth but with Primally Pure's cleanliness. NOT purple (that was NMN). NOT black (that's masculine). The product itself is golden — let the product's color be the brand's color.
