# PDP Design Principles + Redesign Direction

> **Date:** 2026-06-29
> **Source:** Claude Chrome design research across 15 PDPs (4 tallow/ancestral, 5 beauty DTC, 6 best-in-class non-category). Goal: lift PDP from ~3% CVR toward ~5% by fixing what makes our page stale/boring. #1 target = CONGRUENCE.
> **Pairs with:** `PDP_Elevation_Plan.md` (offer/copy/conversion mechanics — separate from this visual-design doc).

---

## The Headline Finding

The two highest-cohesion pages in the entire study — **Nécessaire (10/10)** and **Graza (10/10)** — win the same way: **total commitment to ONE color + type system, applied to every element on the page.** Opposite aesthetics (Nécessaire = stark white minimalism; Graza = saturated green maximalism), identical principle. Our current PDP has NO committed accent color (flat beige boxes, default-ish serif) and that is the single biggest reason it reads as stale.

**The gap in one sentence:** the best pages feel like one designed world; ours feels like a clean Shopify template with content dropped in.

---

## The 8 Principles To Steal (ranked by impact for us)

1. **Commit to ONE accent color and thread it EVERYWHERE.** Primally Pure (forest-green CTA + checkmarks + badges), Graza (green), Tower 28 (terracotta), Magic Spoon (purple). The accent appears in the CTA, rating stars, icons, section bands, hover states. → We need a committed tallow accent. Recommend **a deep botanical green OR a honey/amber gold** as the single brand color, used on every CTA, checkmark, and accent. Kills the "default beige" problem instantly.

2. **Break monotony with full-bleed bands.** Every great page alternates: airy product sections ↔ full-bleed texture/lifestyle/color bands. Toups (macro cream texture w/ ghosted text), Graza (dark-green fact bands), Ancestral (Irish field imagery), Primally Pure (split light-text/dark-photo). → Our page is flat-beige-box stacked on flat-beige-box. Add 2-3 full-bleed editorial bands (a tallow-melt texture macro, a band in the accent color) to create rhythm.

3. **Build PROOF into the page flow — not just a reviews section.** Primally Pure "After 14 Days" stat block (94%/93%/90%), Tower 28 before/after photo+quote blocks, Nécessaire systematic attribute grid, Ancestral lab-test badge, Jones Road review-count-as-design. → Proof should appear as designed content mid-scroll, repeatedly, not only in a reviews module at the bottom.

4. **Typography as voice, not utility.** Graza (chunky sans + editorial serif mix), Nécessaire (confident proprietary-feeling type), Jones Road (huge bold all-caps name). → Our generic serif is forgettable. Pick a header face with personality and a clear size-contrast system. One distinctive pairing.

5. **Systematic credentialing grid.** Nécessaire's TARGETS / TREATMENT / TESTED / TEXTURE / pH / USAGE / FOOTPRINT grid turns claims into structured data that mirrors the product's precision. → A tallow version: WHAT IT IS / 4 INGREDIENTS / HOW IT ABSORBS / WHO IT'S FOR / SOURCING / GUARANTEE as a clean attribute grid. Reads as confident and designed.

6. **Review VOLUME or DATA as a visual element.** Jones Road puts "(85,143 Reviews)" right under the H1 — the number itself is the design. Ancestral shows a lab-test badge ("247 substances tested"). → Lead with our strongest proof number, styled large.

7. **Retail/authority logos as trust (if/when available).** Nécessaire shows Nordstrom + Sephora "find near me." → Park for later; we have no retail yet, but lab-test or press as we get them.

8. **Confident whitespace = premium.** Nécessaire proves emptiness, used deliberately, signals luxury. → Don't fill every box. Let the hero and key claims breathe.

## ★ The Congruence Unlock — match the PDP to the WHITEBOARD ADS

This is the most important strategic point and the thing that makes our redesign different from just "copy Graza." Our ads are now the **whiteboard diagram format** — educational, mechanism-driven, clean (water-vs-fat, 14→1 counter math, flip-the-label). The PDP must be congruent with the world the click came from.

**So: put the actual ad diagrams ON the PDP.** The whiteboard visual language becomes the PDP's design language:
- The **water-vs-fat absorption diagram** (from B19C2) becomes the "Why This Works" section — same diagram, redrawn clean.
- The **14 → 1 counter-math** (from B20C1) becomes the "What's In / What's Out" / simplicity section.
- The **label comparison** (from B20C3) becomes the ingredient section (long unreadable label vs our 4).

When the ad teaches a mechanism with a diagram and then the PDP continues that exact diagram, the visit feels like one continuous lesson → trust compounds → CVR rises. No other tallow brand does this because none of them run diagram ads. It's our unique congruence weapon.

This also resolves the aesthetic question: we are NOT pure soft-apothecary (Toups) and NOT stark-clinical (Nécessaire). We're the **hybrid that matches our ads: "warm evidence-forward apothecary"** — the natural warmth/credibility of tallow (cream + botanical/amber accent, texture macros) + the systematic, diagram-driven, proof-in-the-flow structure of Nécessaire/Primally Pure that mirrors our educational ads.

---

## Recommended Direction: "Warm Evidence-Forward Apothecary"

- **Color:** Cream/linen base (keep — it's right for tallow). ONE committed accent: **deep botanical green** (credibility + natural, like Primally Pure's proven CTA color) with **honey-amber** as a secondary warmth accent. Accent on every CTA, checkmark, diagram highlight, section band.
- **Type:** Distinctive editorial serif for headers (more character than current) + clean sans body. Consider one chunky/confident moment for the product name (Jones Road scale).
- **Imagery:** Warm texture macros (the whipped melt), real in-hand lifestyle, AND clean redrawn diagrams from the ads. Consistent warm treatment.
- **Rhythm:** Alternate airy cream product sections with full-bleed bands (one amber/green band, one texture macro band, one diagram band).
- **Proof in flow:** a stat/"after 2 weeks" block, before/after photo+quote pairs (real customers — see integrity flag in elevation plan), a systematic attribute grid, lead review number styled large.
- **Motion:** light — sticky mobile ATC, subtle scroll fade-ins, image zoom. Not heavy; just enough to feel alive (the thing our static page lacks).

---

## Two Other Directions (if founder wants a different feel)

- **B — "Clean Clinical" (Nécessaire-inspired):** near-white, stark, systematic attribute grids, confident whitespace, monochrome + one accent. Maximally congruent with the *educational/clinical* read of the whiteboard ads; less warm. Risk: tallow buyers may want more natural warmth.
- **C — "Bold Modern" (Graza/Magic Spoon-inspired):** saturated accent color owns the page, chunky type, playful confident bands, maximal personality. Highest "pop," most differentiated in the tallow space (all competitors are muted). Risk: could read as less premium/serious for a skin product; furthest from current proven page.

---

## Build Priority (once direction is locked)

1. Lock the accent color + type pairing (the foundation everything else hangs on).
2. Redraw the 3 ad diagrams as clean PDP sections (congruence unlock).
3. Add 2-3 full-bleed bands to break the beige monotony.
4. Proof-in-flow: stat block + before/after pairs (real reviews) + attribute grid.
5. Sticky mobile ATC + light scroll motion.
6. (From elevation plan) single-jar hero offer architecture + real-reviews integrity swap.
