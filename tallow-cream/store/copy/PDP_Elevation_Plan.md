# PDP Elevation Plan — Maximize Conversions on the Proven Page

> **Date:** 2026-06-29
> **Premise:** The current PDP (v2) is proven to convert — do NOT rebuild it. This is elevation: keep the bones, fix the highest-leverage friction, make it pop, and (critically) match it to the winning ads + the proven offer. Two founder data points drive this plan: (1) the PDP converts, (2) the SINGLE-jar offer outperforms the bundles.

---

## THE BIG ONE — Offer Architecture (highest-impact change)

**Problem:** The hero leads with a 3-way bundle selector (Try It / Stock Up / Best Value) and repeats it at the bottom. But the data says the SINGLE jar outperforms bundles, and every winning ad (B19/B20) is built on "ONE jar replaced everything." So the PDP is:
1. Fighting its own best offer by giving the 3-way choice top billing (decision friction / paradox of choice → deliberation instead of buying)
2. Breaking message-match — the ad promises "one jar, $49.99," the PDP opens with a bundle grid

**Fix — lead with the single, demote bundles to a post-add upsell:**
- Hero shows ONE clear offer: ~~$59.99~~ **$49.99**, one **ADD TO CART**. No selector above the fold. Remove the choice that causes hesitation.
- Move "buy more, save more" to a **post-ATC upsell** (after they click Add to Cart: "Add a 2nd jar, save $10 — most people reorder anyway") or a small in-cart nudge. Bundles become an upsell, not a gate.
- This matches the ad ("one jar"), respects the data (single wins), and cuts friction at the exact decision moment.

**Why not just keep bundles smaller?** Because the hero's job is ONE decision: buy this jar or not. Every additional option at that moment costs conversions. Capture the bundle revenue AFTER the yes, when the buyer is already committed and an upsell feels like a deal, not a fork.

---

## Guiding Principles for the Elevation

1. **Message-match the winning ads.** A reader clicking from B20 just saw "fourteen products → one jar," "$600 a year," or "flip the label." The PDP's first screen should echo that exact frame so the visit feels continuous, not like a different pitch.
2. **One decision above the fold.** Buy or not. Everything else (bundles, education, FAQ) comes after the yes-or-keep-reading fork.
3. **Mobile-first.** Most traffic is mobile. Sticky ATC, thumb-reachable, fast.
4. **Pre-handle the two category objections early** (greasy/shine + "smells like beef") — these are the #1 and #2 pre-purchase fears per review mining. Don't bury them in Section 6.
5. **Real social proof.** (See integrity flag below.)

## Section-by-Section Elevation

### Hero (Above the Fold) — make it POP
- **Hero image:** lead with a texture shot — the whipped balm mid-scoop (the "warm honey melt" the copy describes). Texture is the scroll-stop on a PDP the way the diagram is on the ad. Pair with one clean jar shot.
- **Anchored price, prominent:** ~~$59.99~~ **$49.99** · Free Shipping — the strikethrough does real work; make sure it renders (HANDOFF's open PDP-pricing verify item).
- **One-line promise — tighten:** current "For skin that changed and nothing else works." is good. A/B against a counter-math echo: **"One jar. Four ingredients. The last thing your skin needs."**
- **Star rating + count** right under the title.
- **Single ADD TO CART**, sticky on mobile.
- **Trust strip** (already good): Free Shipping · 60-Day Guarantee · 4 Ingredients · Grass-Fed.
- **Micro-objection line under ATC:** "Absorbs in 2 minutes. No shine. Doesn't smell like beef." — kills the two top fears before they scroll.

### Section 2 — "Sound familiar?" — keep, tighten
- Strong as-is. Trim to 5 checks max (lists over 5 lose scannability). Lead with the two most universal: "used to work, now it doesn't" + "tight and dry no matter what."

### Section 3 — "What's In / What's Out" — elevate to match the ads
- This section IS the B20C3 "Label Test" ad. Lean into it. Make "Flip over your current moisturizer. Count the ingredients. Now count ours." a bold visual moment — a side-by-side: a long blurred 30-ingredient label vs. our 4. Direct message-match with the Label Test ad.

### Section 4 — "Why This Works" — keep the mechanism, soften one claim
- Mechanism is good and matches B9C3. One edit: "After 40, your body produces less" is fine on a PDP, but consider "As skin changes, it makes less of its own oil" to stay age-neutral and broaden.

### Section 7 — Reviews — ★ INTEGRITY FLAG ★
- The current reviews (Karen M., Diana L., Rachel T., etc.) appear to be fabricated — HANDOFF lists a "Review-integrity fix: real photo-backed reviews from the 9 customers, replace fabricated PDP reviews" as a queued task. **This elevation is the moment to do that swap.** Real photo-backed reviews convert better than polished fake ones AND remove the integrity/legal risk. Pull the 9 real customers' words + photos. Keep the structure (the 4-star "texture takes getting used to" review is great for believability — keep one real equivalent).

### Section 8 — Guarantee — make it bigger
- 60-day MBG is a strong risk-reversal. Make the badge larger, place one guarantee mention directly under the hero ATC too (risk reversal at the decision point, not just at the bottom).

### Sections 5, 6, 9, 10 — keep
- How-to-use, Real Talk, FAQ, Final CTA all solid. Final CTA repeats the single-jar offer (with the bundle upsell as a secondary "or stock up" link, not a co-equal grid).

## Build Priority Order (highest ROI first)

1. **Offer architecture** — single in hero, bundles to post-ATC upsell. (Biggest lever, ties to the data.)
2. **Verify the strikethrough price renders** ($59.99 → $49.99) — open item, breaks message-match if it shows bare $59.99.
3. **Hero texture image + micro-objection line under ATC.**
4. **Real reviews swap** (integrity + conversion).
5. **Section 3 side-by-side label visual** (message-match to B20C3).
6. **Sticky mobile ATC + guarantee under hero.**

---

## Open Decision for Founder

The bundle demotion is the one strategic call. Recommended: **lead single, bundles become a post-ATC upsell.** Alternative if you want to preserve bundle AOV more aggressively: keep a small "1 / 2 / 3 jar" toggle but default-select the single and shrink it well below the ATC. Either way, the single should not share equal billing with bundles at the hero. Confirm which before building.
