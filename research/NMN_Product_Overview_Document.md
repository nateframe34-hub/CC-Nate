## Step 2: Product In Action

**HOW does your product work?**

NMN (Nicotinamide Mononucleotide) is a naturally occurring molecule and a direct precursor to NAD+ (Nicotinamide Adenine Dinucleotide). NAD+ is a coenzyme present in every living cell that is essential for hundreds of metabolic processes — most importantly cellular energy production, DNA repair, and the regulation of circadian rhythms. NAD+ levels decline by approximately 50% between the ages of 40 and 60. When you take NMN orally, it is absorbed in the gut and converted into NAD+ inside your cells, effectively replenishing declining NAD+ stores. This supports mitochondrial function (the "powerhouses" of your cells), activates sirtuins (longevity-associated proteins involved in DNA repair and inflammation regulation), and supports overall cellular health. The outcome is sustained cellular energy, supported healthy aging processes, and maintained physical and cognitive vitality.

**6th-grade reading level version:**

Your body has a molecule called NAD+ that keeps your cells running — it helps them make energy, fix their own DNA, and stay healthy. The problem is, by the time you're 50, you've lost about half of it. NMN is what your body uses to make more NAD+. When you take our NMN capsule, your body turns it into NAD+, which helps your cells work like they did when you were younger. More NAD+ = more energy at the cellular level, better repair, and healthier aging.
