### 1. "US VS THEM" (Comparison Ads)

**Templates:** 1, 2, 3, 4, 51, 52, 53, 54

**What It Is:** Side-by-side comparison of your product vs. the competition (or the old way vs. the new way). Uses visual contrast — two columns, split screen, or before/after layout.

**Variations Seen:**
- **Direct Product Comparison** — Two products side by side with checkmarks vs. X marks (Template 1, 2)
- **Lifestyle Comparison** — "Life with [product]" vs. "Life without [product]" split imagery (Template 3, 4)
- **Simplified Object Comparison** — Uses universally understood objects as metaphors (Template 51, 52)
- **Ingredient/Feature Comparison** — Clean list of what's in yours vs. what's in theirs (Template 53, 54)

**Why It Works:**
- Humans process information faster through contrast
- Positions your product as the obvious winner without needing to "sell"
- The viewer draws their own conclusion — which feels more convincing than being told

**NMN Application:**
| Concept | Headline | Visual |
|---------|----------|--------|
| NMN vs. Multivitamin | "What your multivitamin does vs. what NMN does" | Multivitamin side: surface-level vitamins. NMN side: cellular energy, NAD+ restoration, mitochondrial repair |
| NMN vs. Energy Drinks | "Borrowed energy vs. real energy" | Left: coffee cup, energy drink can, crash at 2pm. Right: NMN capsule, steady energy all day |
| NMN vs. "Anti-Aging" Creams | "They cover the surface. We repair the cell." | Split: face cream on skin vs. NMN molecule entering a cell |
| With NMN vs. Without NMN | "Your cells at 30 vs. your cells at 50. NMN closes the gap." | Vibrant cell illustration vs. depleted cell |

### 2. "TESTIMONIAL / REVIEW" Ads

**Templates:** 5, 6, 7, 8, 26, 27, 28, 55, 56

**What It Is:** Real (or real-looking) customer testimonials displayed as the primary ad creative. The ad looks like a screenshot of a review, a text message, or a social media comment — not like a brand-created ad.

**Variations Seen:**
- **Star Rating + Quote** — 5-star visual with a pull quote (Template 5, 6)
- **Screenshot Style** — Made to look like a real Facebook comment, text message, or Amazon review (Template 7, 26)
- **Before/After Testimonial** — Photo grid with customer quote overlaid (Template 8, 27)
- **Video Testimonial Thumbnail** — Static frame that looks like a paused video with a quote (Template 28)
- **Multi-Review Collage** — 3-4 short reviews stacked together for social proof density (Template 55, 56)

**Why It Works:**
- Third-party credibility — someone else saying it is 10x more believable than the brand saying it
- The "screenshot" format triggers pattern recognition: "this is a real conversation, not an ad"
- Star ratings are a universal trust shorthand

**NMN Application:**

### 3. "QUESTION HOOK" Ads

**Templates:** 9, 10, 11, 29, 30, 57, 58

**What It Is:** The ad opens with a provocative or relatable question that forces the viewer to stop and mentally answer. The question creates an "open loop" that can only be closed by engaging with the ad.

**Variations Seen:**
- **Pain Point Question** — "Still tired after 8 hours of sleep?" (Template 9, 10)
- **Curiosity Question** — "What if everything you knew about aging was wrong?" (Template 11, 29)
- **Challenge Question** — "Can you name one supplement that actually works?" (Template 30)
- **Statistic + Question** — "50% of your cellular energy is gone by age 50. What are you doing about it?" (Template 57, 58)

**Why It Works:**
- Questions activate the brain's answer-seeking reflex — the viewer can't help but engage
- A well-chosen question pre-qualifies the viewer (only people with the problem will stop)
- Creates an open loop that drives click-through

**NMN Application:**
| Concept | Question | Visual |
|---------|----------|--------|
| Energy Decline | "Why are you more tired at 45 than you were at 25?" | Tired person at desk, coffee cups lined up |
| NAD+ Education | "What if aging isn't about time — it's about one molecule?" | Molecular structure visual, clean and scientific |

### 6. "PROBLEM → AGITATE → SOLVE" Ads

**Templates:** 17, 18, 35, 36, 63, 64

**What It Is:** The classic PAS copywriting framework in visual ad form. Names a problem the viewer recognizes, agitates the pain of that problem, then presents the product as the solution.

**Variations Seen:**
- **Pain Headline + Product** — "Tired of [problem]? Meet [product]" (Template 17, 18)
- **Symptom List → Solution** — Lists painful symptoms, then introduces the product (Template 35, 36)
- **Before State / After State** — Visual contrast of the problem world vs. the solution world (Template 63, 64)

**Why It Works:**
- Mirrors the viewer's internal monologue — "yes, that's exactly how I feel"
- Agitation raises the emotional stakes so the solution feels like relief
- The product arrives at the exact moment of peak frustration

**NMN Application:**

**Templates:** 25, 43, 44, 71, 72

## SECTION 5: Template Adaptation Rules for NMN

When adapting any of the 100 templates, follow these rules:

### Visual Standards
1. **Color palette:** Match the NMN brand identity (reference Brand_Identity.md)
2. **810x810px format** — All templates are designed for Meta square feed placement
3. **Product photography must feel premium** — Dark backgrounds, dramatic lighting, gold/white accents
4. **QR code visibility** — Include the QR-to-COA element in at least 30% of all static ads

### Copy Standards
1. **Lead with the mechanism, not the product** — "NAD+ decline" before "NMN supplement"
2. **Specificity always** — "50% NAD+ decline by age 50" not "your energy decreases as you age"
3. **One concept per ad** — Don't try to be educational AND promotional AND funny in one ad
4. **Headlines: 5-12 words max** — Shorter always wins in static ads
5. **Avoid supplement clichés** — No "game-changer," "life-changing," "superfood," or "revolutionary"

### Testing Protocol
1. **Test 3-5 frameworks per funnel stage simultaneously**
2. **Run each ad for minimum 3 days / $50 spend before judging**
3. **Winning metrics by stage:**
   - Cold: CTR > 1.5%, CPM efficiency, thumb-stop rate
   - Warm: CPC < $2, landing page conversion > 3%
   - Hot: ROAS > 2.5x, CPA < $30
4. **Iterate winners** — Take winning framework, test 3 variations of headline/visual

## Key Takeaways

1. **100 templates, 16 frameworks, one system.** The templates aren't random — they map to a complete funnel from awareness to conversion. Every framework has a specific job at a specific stage.

2. **Education → Trust → Offer is the NMN funnel.** Cold traffic needs the NAD+ knowledge bomb (Education, Question Hook). Warm traffic needs proof (Testimonial, Transformation, Ingredient). Hot traffic needs a push (Offer, Urgency, Social Proof).

3. **Transformation ads are the dominant format for a reason.** At 12% of all templates, Before/After is the single largest category. For supplements, visual proof of results is the ultimate persuasion tool. Build your testimonial pipeline early.

4. **QR-to-COA is your unique visual element.** No other NMN brand has this. It should appear in Ingredient Spotlight, Founder Story, Unboxing, and even Us vs Them templates as a consistent visual differentiator.

5. **Start with 15 ads across 3 funnel stages.** Don't try to create all 100 variations at launch. Use the Top 15 Priority list above, test aggressively, and scale the winning frameworks.

6. **These templates + the Origins frameworks = a complete ad library.** The 1000 Winning Templates give you the visual formats. The Origins analysis (Camouflage Ads, World Class Statics, Body Copy frameworks) gives you the copywriting structures. Combine them: use a "Question Hook" template format with a "Problem → Mechanism → Solution" copy structure, for example.
