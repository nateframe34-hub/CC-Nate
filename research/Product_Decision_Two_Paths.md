# Product Decision: Two Paths Forward

> **NOTE (March 2026):** The current product is the **Baseline NMN 10-in-1** supplement. References to QR-to-COA batch verification throughout this document were developed for the original pure NMN concept and are **not a feature of the current 10-in-1 product**. QR-to-COA remains a planned future feature.

> **Context:** The Baseline brand strategy was built around radical transparency, single-ingredient purity, and verification (QR-to-COA). The current supplier's product is a "10-in-1 Ultimate" NMN formula with a proprietary blend. This document compares both paths so you can make an informed decision before finalizing ad creative.

---

## The Conflict

| What the Brand Strategy Says | What the Current Product Actually Is |
|------------------------------|--------------------------------------|
| Single-ingredient purity ("Just NMN. Nothing else.") | 10-in-1 formula with 9+ ingredients |
| Radical transparency (exact doses disclosed) | 450mg proprietary blend (individual doses hidden) |
| "1,000mg NMN" implied | Actually 500mg NMN + 450mg blend + 50mg Vitamin C |
| QR-to-COA batch verification system | Standard third-party testing (no QR, no batch-level COA) |
| Enzymatic synthesis as differentiator | Synthesis method unknown/unconfirmed |
| "For people who read the label" | The label obscures individual ingredient doses |
| Anti-proprietary-blend positioning (Batch 3 attacks blends) | The product IS a proprietary blend |

**The core problem:** 3 of the 5 new mechanisms in the brand strategy (QR-to-COA, enzymatic synthesis, 64% fake stat as bridge to product proof) cannot be run with the current product without being dishonest. And the entire brand identity — "radical transparency" — contradicts a proprietary blend.

#### Critical Quality Note: Alpha-NMN vs Beta-NMN

A supplement labeled "99% pure NMN" can actually contain a 50/50 mix of alpha-NMN and beta-NMN. **Only beta-NMN is biologically active.** Chemical synthesis often produces both isomers, while enzymatic synthesis preferentially produces beta-NMN. For Baseline's transparency positioning, specifying **"99%+ pure beta-NMN"** on labels and COAs is essential — and a differentiator most competitors don't specify.

## PATH B: Rebrand for the Current 10-in-1 Product

Keep the current supplier and 10-in-1 formula. Completely rework the brand strategy, mechanisms, and ad creative to match what the product actually IS — a comprehensive longevity stack, not a pure NMN.

### New Positioning (Path B)

**From:** "Baseline — Pure NMN. Radical Transparency. Single Ingredient."
**To:** "Baseline — The Complete NAD+ Stack. 10 Synergistic Longevity Ingredients in 1."

The value proposition shifts from PURITY to COMPREHENSIVENESS. Instead of "we have less," the message becomes "we have everything in one."

### What Changes in the Brand Strategy

## THE HYBRID PATH (Optional)

There is a third option: **Launch with the current 10-in-1 product to generate revenue while simultaneously sourcing a pure NMN product, then transition the brand.**

### How It Works

1. **Week 1-2:** Rebrand the 10-in-1 as a "pilot" product — NOT the Baseline hero SKU. Call it something like "Baseline Complete" or "Baseline Stack" — a supporting product, not the flagship.
2. **Week 1-4:** Contact Abinopharm, Regalabs, and Pure Private Label. Order a small pure NMN test run (144-1,000 units).
3. **Week 2-6:** Run modified ad batches (Path B versions) for the 10-in-1 to start generating data: which sub-avatars respond? Which hooks stop the scroll? Which copy converts? You're testing MESSAGING, not the product yet.
4. **Week 6-12:** Receive pure NMN product. Launch as "Baseline" (the flagship). Transition all ad creative to Path A versions with full mechanism stack.
5. **Ongoing:** Keep the 10-in-1 as an upsell/cross-sell product ("Already taking Baseline NMN? Add the Complete Stack for full-spectrum support.") or discontinue it.

### Hybrid Pros
- You start generating data immediately
- You learn which sub-avatars convert before investing in the pure product
- Ad testing insights carry over to the pure NMN launch
- No wasted time — you're building muscle with ad spend even on the interim product

### Hybrid Cons
- Brand confusion risk if customers buy the 10-in-1 expecting "radical transparency"
- Two supplier relationships to manage initially
- You're spending ad money on a product that isn't the long-term hero
- Risk of the 10-in-1 experience being mediocre (underdosed ingredients) and creating negative word-of-mouth before the real product launches

### Hybrid Verdict
This works ONLY if you clearly position the 10-in-1 as a temporary/supporting product and DO NOT run the QR-to-COA or purity mechanisms on it. Use it as a messaging testing vehicle, not a brand-building vehicle.

## MY RECOMMENDATION

**Path A — Switch to pure NMN.**

Here's why:

### 1. The brand strategy IS the competitive advantage

Everything we built — the mechanisms, the hooks, the identity, the competitive positioning — is designed around transparency and proof. These aren't nice-to-have features. They ARE the strategy. Removing them doesn't just weaken the ads — it removes the reason the brand exists.

### 2. The 10-in-1 can't support the brand name

"Baseline" literally means "the starting point, the foundation, the minimum." A 10-in-1 proprietary blend with 9 ingredients at unknown doses is the opposite of foundational. The name and the product would fight each other in every piece of copy.

### 3. The margin hit is survivable

Even at $20 COGS (mid estimate), your margins on the 120ct are 71% with a breakeven ROAS of 1.41x. That's still better than most supplement brands. Your offer structure was built with room for this.

### 4. The MOQ is manageable

144-1,000 units at $2,000-$15,000 is a reasonable startup investment. You don't need to order 10,000 units on day one.

### 5. The regulatory timing is perfect

NMN was just re-legalized in the US (September 2025) and has always been legal in Canada. The market is reopening. Brands that launch now with QUALITY positioning will capture the trust gap left by the years of regulatory uncertainty.

### 5b. Being Canadian is actually an advantage

Health Canada's NPN (Natural Product Number) system requires **pre-market approval** — unlike the US where supplements can launch without FDA review. Having an NPN on your label is a trust signal that US-only brands literally cannot match. You can use this in your ads: "Approved by Health Canada. Tested by independent labs. Verified by you." The NPN becomes part of the proof stack alongside QR-to-COA. And there's a Canadian contract manufacturer — **NutraLab Canada** in Markham, Ontario — with 25+ years experience, Health Canada GMP approval, an in-house analytical lab, and 500+ pre-registered NPN formulas.

### 6. The ad creative is already done

All 5 batches, all 15 creatives, all body copy, all mechanisms — they're all built for a pure NMN product. Switching to Path A means you can launch the ad creative as-is. Switching to Path B means rewriting half of everything.

### 7. The burned supplement buyer is your biggest opportunity

Sub-Avatar #2 (The Burned Supplement Buyer) is the highest-conversion opportunity because they have both the desire AND the budget — they just need PROOF. QR-to-COA gives them proof. A proprietary blend gives them more reasons to distrust.

### If budget is the constraint

Go with the **Hybrid Path**: run the 10-in-1 under a neutral name for 4-6 weeks to test messaging while your pure NMN order is being manufactured. Use the data (which sub-avatars convert, which hooks work) to optimize the pure NMN launch. But do NOT build the Baseline brand on the 10-in-1 product.
