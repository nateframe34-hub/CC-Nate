## CRITICAL: FDA Regulatory Update (September 2025)

On **September 29, 2025**, the FDA reversed its 2022 position and **confirmed NMN is lawful for use in dietary supplements** in the United States. However, NMN is classified as a **New Dietary Ingredient (NDI)** — meaning companies must submit an NDI Notification (NDIN) to the FDA before marketing NMN products, unless sourcing from a supplier that has already filed one. This is a compliance step to address before launch.

## Supplier Vetting Checklist

Before placing any order, verify:

1. **COA:** HPLC purity test confirming 99%+ NMN, absence of solvents (especially acetonitrile), heavy metals, microbial testing
2. **Third-party lab testing:** Independent lab, not just in-house
3. **Beta-NMN confirmation:** Must be 100% beta-NMN (the bioactive form), not alpha/beta mix
4. **cGMP certification:** Mandatory for US market
5. **FDA facility registration:** For US-based manufacturers
6. **NDI notification status:** Confirm whether supplier has filed NDIN with FDA
7. **Synthesis method:** Enzymatic/biosynthetic preferred over chemical synthesis
8. **Additional certs:** ISO 9001, HACCP, KOSHER, HALAL as relevant
