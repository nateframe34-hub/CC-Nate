## Desires (Wants / Needs)

### Primary Desire Cluster 1: Energy Restoration (Core Desire: HEALTH)
*The #1 desire across all sources. People don't want "more energy" — they want their OLD energy back.*

- "I want to feel like I did in my 30s"
- "I want my old focus back"
- "I want less fatigue and more focus during the day"
- "I want the energy of a 20-something"
- "I want that 3pm wall to disappear"
- "I want to not be wrecked for two days after a hard workout"
- "I want to wake up without hitting snooze three times"
- "I want consistent energy throughout the day — not a caffeine spike and crash"
- "I want to stop feeling tired all the time"
- "I need something that actually moves the needle"
- "I want to feel normal again"

**Desire evaluation:**
- Urgency: HIGH — people are experiencing this pain daily
- Staying Power: PERMANENT — energy decline is ongoing and worsening with age
- Scope: MASSIVE — virtually everyone 35+ experiences declining energy

### Primary Desire Cluster 2: Trust & Confidence in Supplements (Core Desire: CONTROL)
*The second strongest desire. People are SCARED of being scammed — this is the #1 barrier to purchase.*

- "I want to know what I'm actually putting in my body"
- "I need to know this isn't another supplement that promises everything and delivers nothing"
- "I want proof that this stuff is real, not fake"
- "How do I know what purity I'm getting?"
- "I want to trust a brand again"
- "I need a supplement I can verify — not just a pretty label"
- "I want to feel smart about my health choices, not manipulated by marketing"
- "I don't want to be the sucker who buys empty capsules"
- "I need to see the lab results before I believe anything"

**Desire evaluation:**
- Urgency: HIGH — 64% of NMN products are fake, and people know it
- Staying Power: PERMANENT — trust deficit in supplements is only growing
- Scope: LARGE — anyone who has ever bought a supplement has been burned or fears being burned

### Primary Desire Cluster 3: Aging Well / Staying Sharp (Core Desire: HEALTH)
*Not "anti-aging" vanity — this is about maintaining FUNCTION and independence.*

- "I want to age gracefully without falling apart"
- "I want to still be active and sharp at 70"
- "I want my brain to work like it used to — the fog needs to go"
- "I want to do something proactive about my health before it's too late"
- "I want to invest in my future self"
- "I don't want to look back in 10 years and realize I did nothing"
- "I want to maintain what I have — strength, clarity, energy"
- "I want to be around for my kids and grandkids — healthy, not just alive"

**Desire evaluation:**
- Urgency: MODERATE-HIGH — intensifies as people hit 40, 50, 60
- Staying Power: PERMANENT — aging is irreversible and accelerating
- Scope: MASSIVE — universal concern once you hit midlife

## New Information (Learned During Research)

### Market Intelligence
1. **The 30-45 demographic is the fastest-growing NMN buyer segment** — most brands still market to 60+ with "anti-aging" messaging. Huge opportunity to own the "invest in your future self" angle for younger buyers.

2. **"Feel normal again" is the dominant desire language** — NOT "reverse aging" or "live forever." The realistic, grounded language converts better than hype. People want their OLD baseline back, not superpowers.

3. **Trust is the #1 barrier to purchase** — the 64% fake stat is well-known in the NMN community. Brands that lead with proof over promises will win. QR-to-COA is a genuine differentiator that no competitor has made central to their brand.

4. **Subtle, gradual effects are the reality** — and customers who understand this stick around. Setting expectations correctly in ads ("not a miracle pill, but your baseline goes up") will reduce churn and attract high-LTV customers.

5. **Morning dosing is critical** — multiple users report insomnia from afternoon/evening NMN doses. This should be in onboarding emails and product instructions. It's also an angle: "Take 2 capsules with breakfast. That's it."

6. **Doctor validation is the most powerful social proof** — users who got bloodwork showing NAD+ improvement became the strongest evangelists. Consider: "Show your doctor your before/after NAD+ levels" as a retention strategy.

7. **Stacking with resveratrol + TMG is common** — this is a future product line extension opportunity. The NMN community already does this. Building a "stack" offer could significantly boost AOV.

8. **Amazon's NMN ban created a trust vacuum** — Amazon pulled NMN products, and the community knows the platform was flooded with fakes. DTC brands that lead with transparency fill this exact gap.

### Competitive Insights
9. **ProHealth is the most active NMN advertiser on Meta** — they lead with clinical proof messaging ("Increased NAD+ levels by 38%, equaling a decrease in biological age of 12 years"). Study their ads closely.

10. **OMRE (formerly Elysium) drives ~20% of sales through influencer partnerships** — 25+ influencer relationships, 4.5% engagement rate on Instagram. Influencer strategy is proven in this space.

11. **Only 6.6% of supplement brands use quiz funnels** — "What's your cellular age?" quiz is an untapped opportunity that self-qualifies buyers and educates simultaneously.

### Avatar Insights
12. **Two distinct buyer personas confirmed:**
    - **The Optimizer (30-45):** Proactive, biohacker-adjacent, listens to Huberman/Attia, wants to "invest in future self," research-driven, higher income
    - **The Restorer (50-65+):** Reactive, triggered by noticeable decline, wants to "get back to normal," values simplicity over science-speak, needs more trust-building

13. **Women over 40 are underserved** — most NMN branding is male-coded (biohacker bro aesthetic). Women describe the same desires (energy, clarity, aging well) but in different language: "I feel like I've aged 10 years in the last 2," "I'm not the mom I want to be when I'm this exhausted."

14. **The "informed skeptic" identity is powerful** — many NMN buyers see themselves as people who "do their own research" and "don't fall for marketing." The QR-to-COA feature speaks directly to this self-image.
