### Key Principles From World Class Statics

1. **Visual congruence is mandatory** — The image must match the headline concept. Ryanair shows pizza vs Pisa. Tesla shows a blurred car (speed). Nutella shows two pieces of toast.
2. **Leverage what the avatar already knows** — Don't educate. Tap into existing cultural knowledge (common sayings, shared experiences, known brands).
3. **The "ah-ha" moment > the hard sell** — Force the viewer to make a connection. When they "get it," they feel smart. That feeling creates affinity.
4. **Simplify relentlessly** — The best ads have 5-15 words. One concept. One visual. No clutter.
5. **Tag each ad by awareness level** — Origins explicitly labels every ad by awareness stage (Most Aware, Brand Aware, Product Aware, Solution Aware, Offer Focused). This determines the messaging strategy.

## SECTION 2: Meta Static Body Copy Frameworks

The Body Copy PDF contains 18+ real ad copy examples from successful DTC brands. Here are the patterns:

### Body Copy Framework Categories

#### A. "Testimonial as Ad" (Most Common Pattern)
The copy reads like a real person's story, not a brand message. Used by: ComfortWear shoes, The Living Urn, VetSmart, Huel, RYZE.

**Structure:**
1. Personal credential or situation ("I'm a nurse with plantar fasciitis")
2. Specific details that prove authenticity (shift lengths, color names, pricing)
3. Emotional payoff
4. Soft CTA

#### B. "Day-by-Day Transformation"
Maps out what happens on each day after starting the product. Creates anticipation and makes results feel inevitable.

**Structure:**
- Day 1: Initial feeling
- Day 7: First noticeable change
- Day 14: Visible transformation
- Day 21: Major milestone
- Day 28+: Life changed

**Used by:** Happy Mammoth (puffy face), Ultimate Weight Control, RYZE

#### C. "Problem → Mechanism → Solution" (Educational)
Teaches the reader WHY the problem exists (a mechanism they didn't know about), then introduces the product as the only thing that addresses that specific mechanism.

**Structure:**
1. Name the problem everyone knows
2. Reveal the hidden mechanism ("It's not estrogen or progesterone... it's a little hidden hormone that controls both of them")
3. Explain why everything else failed (they didn't address the mechanism)
4. Introduce the product as the mechanism-specific solution

**Used by:** Happy Mammoth Hormone Harmony

#### D. "Meme / Ultra-Short"
1-2 sentences max. Designed for engagement, not conversion. Brand awareness play.

**Used by:** Bones Coffee ("What does your coffee taste like on Mondays?"), Ruggable ("Washable rugs. Made for the whole family to enjoy"), Grüns gummies

#### E. "Feature Stack with Benefit Bullets"
Lists product features as benefits with clear formatting. Direct-response style.

**Used by:** Alpaca Compression Socks, RYZE Hot Cocoa, Huel

### Key Principles From Body Copy

1. **Specificity = Credibility** — "4 12s in a row at the ER" is 10x more believable than "long shifts."
2. **The "New Mechanism" is the money move** — When you can name a cause the reader didn't know about (cortisol controlling estrogen, nutritional deficiency causing teen moodiness), you own the conversation.
3. **Day-by-day timelines sell supplements** — They make the transformation concrete, reduce risk perception, and create a mental "movie" of the reader's future.
4. **Short copy for awareness, long copy for conversion** — Meme-style for cold traffic brand awareness. Long-form testimonial for warm traffic conversion.

## SECTION 3: The "Camouflage Ad" — Origins' Crown Jewel Framework

The Native Swipe folder contains the most valuable asset: **10 full "Camouflage Ad" scripts** that demonstrate Origins' signature ad format for health/supplement brands.

### What Is a Camouflage Ad?

A Camouflage Ad is a **long-form native ad disguised as a personal story**. It reads like a real person's Facebook post or forum comment — not an advertisement. The reader doesn't realize they're being sold to until they're emotionally invested.

### The Universal Camouflage Ad Structure

Every single ad in the swipe file follows this exact structure:

```
1. HOOK (Pattern Interrupt)
   → Provocative first line that stops the scroll
   → Often a taboo, controversial, or emotionally raw statement

2. THE PAIN (Relatable Suffering)
   → Vivid, specific description of the problem
   → Details so granular only someone who lived it would know them
   → Builds empathy and "this is MY story" recognition

3. THE FAILURE MONTAGE (Everything Else Failed)
   → Lists everything the person tried (doctors, pills, home remedies)
   → Each failure builds frustration and hopelessness
   → Positions conventional solutions as broken

4. THE DOCTOR / AUTHORITY FIGURE
   → A credible expert explains the REAL cause
   → Introduces the "new mechanism" — the thing nobody told them
   → Names specific science (strains, compounds, clinical data)

5. THE DISCOVERY
   → Found the product through organic means (forum, friend, Reddit)
   → NOT through an ad — this is critical for the "camouflage"
   → Social proof from other real people who found it first

6. THE SCIENCE (Ingredient Breakdown)
   → Clinical-grade ingredient names with trademarked symbols (®, ™)
   → Specific dosages and clinical study references
   → Explains WHY each ingredient works on the mechanism

7. THE SKEPTICAL PURCHASE
   → Ordered despite doubt ("Another supplement?")
   → Emotional moment with spouse/family showing what's at stake

8. THE DAY-BY-DAY TRANSFORMATION
   → Specific timeline of improvement
   → Concrete, measurable changes
   → Emotional payoff moments woven in

9. THE VALIDATION
   → Follow-up with the doctor confirming results
   → Before/after data (blood work, measurements, etc.)
   → Spouse/family noticing the change

10. THE EMPOWERMENT CTA
    → "It's not your fault"
    → Systemic blame (medical system, pharma, gender bias)
    → "You deserve better"
    → Link with guarantee, trust signals, no-subscription language
```

### The 7 Persuasion Patterns Inside Every Camouflage Ad

**1. The Gender Inequality Trigger**
Ads 1-5 all use the same outrage pattern: "When men have [problem X], they get immediate treatment. When women have [problem Y], they're told to try yoga." This creates righteous anger that bonds the reader to the narrator and positions the product as fighting the system.

**2. The "3% vs 90%" Data Bomb**
Every ad has one shocking statistic that makes the reader gasp. "Your Lactobacillus is at 3%. Healthy is 90%." "78% of women are misdiagnosed." "45 minutes of deep sleep vs 120 minutes." This creates the "I had no idea" moment that opens the mind to the solution.

**3. The Spouse Witness**
The husband/wife always serves as the emotional mirror. "I just want my wife back." "You seem like you're actually here." "You're back. You're really back." The spouse's reaction validates that the transformation is real and externally visible.

**4. The Failing Doctor Parade**
Every ad features 3-5 doctors who failed before the ONE doctor who understood. This builds the narrative that the problem isn't the person — it's the system. And it positions the product as something the system doesn't want you to know about.

**5. The Reluctant Discoverer**
The narrator always finds the product accidentally — through a Reddit thread, a friend's text, a hotel pillow. NEVER through an ad. This is the "camouflage" — the ad pretends it's not an ad.

**6. The Ingredient Authority Stack**
Every supplement ad names trademarked, clinical-grade ingredients: HOWARU® HN001™, KSM-66®, Cran d'Or™, 95% Curcuminoids, L. Gasseri SBT2055. The ® and ™ symbols signal "this is pharmaceutical-grade, not drugstore junk."

**7. The Fork-in-the-Road Close**
Every ad ends with two paths: "Down one path? More infections. More shame. More prescriptions. Down the other? Relief. Confidence. Freedom." This forces a binary choice and makes inaction feel like a deliberate decision to keep suffering.

## Key Takeaways

1. **The Camouflage Ad is Origins' most powerful weapon** — and it's perfectly suited for NMN. The health supplement buyer needs education (new mechanism), emotional validation (it's not your fault), and social proof (real stories) before they'll buy.

2. **Every great Origins ad has a "New Mechanism"** — For NMN, that mechanism is NAD+ decline. Most people have never heard of it. That's your advantage.

3. **The Day-by-Day Timeline sells supplements** — It makes the invisible (cellular repair) feel tangible and inevitable.

4. **QR-to-COA is your "Ingredient Authority Stack"** — Where Uflora has HOWARU® HN001™ and Provitalize has 95% Curcuminoids, you have "scan-the-bottle transparency." That's your trademarked-feeling differentiator.

5. **Static ads stop the scroll. Camouflage ads close the sale.** Run both in sequence — statics for cold traffic awareness, camouflage long-form for warm traffic conversion.
