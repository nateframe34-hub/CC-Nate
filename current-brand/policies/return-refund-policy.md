# Return & Refund Policy

**Baseline NMN — Return & Refund Policy**

*Last updated: March 22, 2026*

---

We want you to feel confident trying Baseline NMN. If you're not satisfied, we'll make it right.

## 30-Day Satisfaction Guarantee

If you're not happy with your purchase for any reason, contact us within **30 days** of delivery and we'll issue a full refund — no questions asked.

## How to Request a Return or Refund

1. Email us at **support@trybaseline.com** with your order number
2. We'll respond within 1–2 business days with return instructions (if applicable)
3. Refunds are processed within 5–7 business days after we confirm your request

## Conditions

- Products must be returned within 30 days of the delivery date
- Opened bottles are eligible for a refund (we stand behind our product)
- Return shipping costs are the responsibility of the customer unless the product arrived damaged or defective
- Items that arrive damaged or defective will be replaced or refunded at no cost to you, including return shipping

## Subscription Orders

- You can cancel your subscription at any time — no penalties, no hoops
- If a subscription order has already shipped, you may return it under the same 30-day policy above
- To cancel or modify your subscription, email **support@trybaseline.com** or manage it through your account

## Refund Method

- Refunds are issued to the original payment method
- Please allow 5–10 business days for the refund to appear on your statement, depending on your bank or credit card provider

## Exchanges

We don't currently offer direct exchanges. If you need a different product or have an issue with your order, contact us and we'll help you sort it out.
