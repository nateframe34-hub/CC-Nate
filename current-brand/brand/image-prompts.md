## Usage Notes

**Aspect ratios:**
- 4:5 — Standard for Shopify product images and Instagram
- 16:9 — Use for the NAD+ infographic and any banner/header placements
- 1:1 — Square crops work for comparison grid and trust strip icons

**Generation tips:**
- Run each prompt 3-4 times and pick the best output — AI image generation has variance
- For the product bottle, you may need to iterate on the label text rendering — most models struggle with precise text on products. Consider generating the bottle without text and adding the label in post-production (Photoshop/Figma)
- The lifestyle portraits may need face refinement — upscale with an AI enhancer if needed
- For the infographic, consider generating the visual style/background and adding the actual data/chart in Figma for precision

**Brand color reference:**
- Primary: Warm charcoal (#3A3A3A)
- Background: Off-white / warm cream (#F7F5F0)
- Accent: Muted sage (#8B9E8B) — use sparingly
- Text: Near-black (#2B2B2B)
- Avoid: Bright blues, neon greens, golds, any "supplement industry" colors
