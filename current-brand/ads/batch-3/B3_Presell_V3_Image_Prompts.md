# B3 Presell V3 — Image Prompts (Javvy-Style)

> **Format:** Small inline images, roughly square, with a colored badge/label overlay at bottom-left.
> **Badge style:** Rounded rectangle, brand teal (#0c3b3f) background, white text, 2-4 words summarizing the point.
> **Image sizing on page:** ~70-75% width on mobile, centered, rounded corners (10px). NOT full-width hero banners. Small enough to scan past quickly, large enough to ground each point visually.
> **Tool:** Nano Banana Pro. Badges added in Canva post-generation.

## Badge Specs (Canva)

- **Background:** #0c3b3f (brand teal)
- **Text:** White, bold, 12-14pt
- **Shape:** Rounded rectangle, 8px border-radius
- **Position:** Bottom-left of image, 12px from edges
- **Padding:** 6px vertical, 12px horizontal
- **Opacity:** 95% (very slight transparency so it doesn't look pasted on)
