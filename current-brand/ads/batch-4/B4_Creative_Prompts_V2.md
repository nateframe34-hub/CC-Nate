# B4 Creative Prompts — Revised (Scroll-Stopping)

> **Batch:** 4 (Avatar Call Out — 3pm Wall Hitter)
> **Principle:** The visual creates an involuntary reaction BEFORE the brain registers it's an ad. Image and headline are ONE fused idea.
> **Tool:** Nano Banana Pro. Text overlays added in Canva post-generation.
> **All images:** Generate WITHOUT text. Headlines and sub-copy added in Canva.

---

## B4C1 — "THE 3PM WALL"

**Scroll-stop mechanism:** Recognition + visual exaggeration. Everyone who hits the wall has a desk covered in coffee. But not 3 cups — an absurd number. The exaggeration is the scroll stop (same mechanism as the army of gummy bears in the Ozempic ad).

**Prompt:**

```
A photorealistic overhead photograph of an office desk absolutely COVERED in takeout coffee cups. At least 10-12 paper coffee cups with green Starbucks-style logos and brown cardboard sleeves scattered across the desk in various states — some upright, some knocked over, some crushed, some with lids off showing dried coffee residue. Between the cups: a laptop barely visible under the mess, a phone face-up showing 2:47 PM on the lock screen, scattered papers, a pen, a crumpled granola bar wrapper. The desk is standard office gray laminate. Overhead fluorescent lighting — flat, harsh, institutional. The sheer VOLUME of cups is absurd and slightly funny — it's clearly an exaggeration but the feeling it captures is real. Shot from directly above, smartphone camera quality, slightly washed-out office color palette. No people visible. 4:5 aspect ratio.
```

**Canva overlay:**
- Top: **THE 3PM WALL** — Plus Jakarta Sans Bold, white with subtle drop shadow, 72pt
- Mid: "Your cells make less energy fuel after 40. Coffee masks it." — 18pt, white at 80%
- Bottom: Small Baseline bottle cutout + "500mg NMN + 9 ingredients" in 14pt

## B4C2 — "EXHAUSTED AT 43."

**Scroll-stop mechanism:** Face detection (involuntary biological response) + emotional resonance. A real woman's face showing genuine exhaustion stops the scroll before the brain processes anything else. Same reason the B2C2 tired mom on the couch worked for delivery.

**Prompt:**

```
A photorealistic candid close-up portrait of a woman, age early 40s, resting her forehead against her hand with her eyes closed. She's sitting at a kitchen table. Her expression is pure exhaustion — not sad, not crying, just completely depleted. She has natural brown hair with a few gray strands, no makeup, visible under-eye shadows, natural skin texture with pores visible. She wears a plain gray crewneck sweatshirt. Her other hand loosely holds a white coffee mug on the table. Behind her, slightly blurred: a kitchen with morning light, a school backpack on the counter, a fruit bowl. The light is soft and natural from a window to the side, casting gentle shadows across her face. She looks like a real mom before the school run — not a model, not posed, not performing. Raw, private, unguarded. Shot at eye level from across the table, shallow depth of field with her face razor-sharp and the background soft. 4:5 aspect ratio.
```

**Canva overlay:**
- Top: **EXHAUSTED AT 43.** — Plus Jakarta Sans Bold, white, 72pt, with dark text shadow
- Mid: Review-style text bubble with rounded corners, light background: *"Tried everything. Magnesium. B-vitamins. Greens powder. Nothing worked until I found out WHY."*
- Bottom: Small Baseline bottle cutout + "30-day guarantee"

## B4C3 — "3 COFFEES BY 2PM."

**Scroll-stop mechanism:** POV shot creates instant first-person identification ("that's MY hand, MY cup"). The branded coffee cup is universally recognizable. The car/desk context grounds it in a specific moment everyone has lived.

**Prompt — Version A (car POV):**

```
A photorealistic first-person POV photograph of a woman's hand holding a large Starbucks-style takeout coffee cup with a green logo and brown cardboard sleeve inside a parked car. The cup has a lipstick mark on the white lid. She's in the driver's seat — steering wheel partially visible at the bottom of frame. In the center console cupholder: two MORE crushed/empty coffee cups from earlier in the day, lids off, crumpled. The car dashboard clock or radio display shows 1:52 PM. Through the windshield: a blurred parking lot, maybe an office building. Afternoon light hits the windshield creating a slight glare. The scene says: this is her THIRD coffee and it's not even 2pm. Shot from her eye level looking down at the cup in her hand, smartphone camera quality, warm afternoon light. 4:5 aspect ratio.
```

**Prompt — Version B (desk POV):**

```
A photorealistic first-person POV photograph looking down at a woman's hand gripping a large Starbucks-style takeout coffee cup with a green logo on an office desk. The cup has a lipstick mark on the lid. To the left of the cup: two MORE empty coffee cups from earlier — one crushed, one with the lid off showing dried residue. A laptop is open in the background showing a blurred spreadsheet. A phone sits face-up showing 1:58 PM. The desk is cluttered but real — a pen, some papers, a hair tie, a hand sanitizer bottle. Overhead fluorescent lighting, flat and institutional. The vibe is: she's reaching for her third coffee and it's not even 2pm and she KNOWS it won't help. Shot from her seated eye level looking down at the desk, smartphone camera quality. 4:5 aspect ratio.
```

**Generate both versions. Pick whichever feels more real.**

## Generation Tips

- **Generate each prompt 3-4 times** and pick the most natural output
- **Coffee cups:** If the AI generates generic cups instead of recognizable branded ones, that's fine — the brown cardboard sleeve + white cup + green logo is enough visual shorthand for "Starbucks." Don't stress about exact branding.
- **Faces (B4C2):** If the face looks too polished, too young, or too "model," regenerate. She should look like someone you'd see at school pickup. Real skin, real tiredness, real life.
- **Text:** Generate ALL images without text. Add headlines, sub-copy, and product shots in Canva after.
- **Aspect ratio:** 4:5 (1080×1350) for feed placement.
