## Generation Tips

- **Generate each prompt 3-4 times** and pick the best output
- **Label text:** AI will get the label text wrong. That's fine — the general bottle shape, color, and feel is what matters. The brand detail reads at a glance (white + purple + gold badges = Baseline). If the label text is too garbled, you can composite your real bottle photo over the AI-generated scene in Canva.
- **Left-side empty space is critical** for B4C1 and B4C3. If the AI fills the left side with props or details, regenerate or crop.
- **All text overlays in Canva** — the AI generates the mood/scene/product, Canva handles every word.
- **Aspect ratio:** 4:5 (1080×1350) for Meta feed.
