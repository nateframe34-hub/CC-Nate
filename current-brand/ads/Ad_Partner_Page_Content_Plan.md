# Ad Partner Page — Content Plan

> **Purpose:** This is the generic health/wellness Facebook Page that B1-B3 camouflage ads run through via Meta's partner feature. It exists so that paid native ads don't come from an empty page — which would immediately signal "fake" to anyone who clicks through.
>
> **This is NOT Sarah Lawson's page.** Sarah's page (see `../partner-page/Partner_Page_Identity_and_Post_Plan.md`) is a separate organic content engine with its own identity. This page is simpler, more generic, and exists solely to make paid ads look native.
>
> **Why it matters:** When someone sees a native/camouflage ad and thinks "this looks real," the first thing they do is check the page it came from. If that page has zero posts, zero followers, and was created last week — the camouflage effect collapses. The page needs enough organic content to pass a 10-second credibility check.

| Attribute | Detail |

## Content Requirements Before Ads Go Live

**Minimum viable page: 12-15 posts spread over 2-3 weeks before the first ad runs.**

This page doesn't need to be a content machine. It needs to pass the credibility sniff test. Someone who clicks through from a B1C1 ad and checks the page should see: "Oh, this is a health/wellness page that shares articles and personal stories. Makes sense."

### Content Mix (Pre-Launch)

| Type | # of Posts | Purpose |
|------|-----------|---------|
| Health/science shares | 5-6 | Repost or summarize NAD+, longevity, cellular health articles from reputable sources (Nature, NIH, Harvard Health) |
| Relatable lifestyle | 3-4 | Generic 40+ energy/health observations. "The supplement cabinet graveyard," "When did 9pm become late?" |
| Personal health tips | 2-3 | General wellness advice — sleep hygiene, movement, hydration. NOT supplement-specific |
| Community engagement | 1-2 | Polls or questions: "What's your biggest energy challenge after 40?" / "Coffee: necessity or habit?" |

## Ongoing Posting (After Ads Go Live)

**Cadence: 2-3 posts per week.** Just enough to stay active. This page doesn't need to grow organically — it just needs to not look dead.

### Weekly Rhythm

| Post | Type | Examples |
|------|------|---------|
| Post 1 | **Science/health share** | NAD+ research, longevity articles, cellular health content |
| Post 2 | **Lifestyle/relatable** | 40+ energy humor, wellness observations, relatable moments |
| Post 3 (optional) | **Community/engagement** | Questions, polls, response to comments |

### Content Rules

1. **Never mention Baseline directly in organic posts.** The ads mention the product. The page provides the credibility context. If the organic posts are also selling Baseline, it blows the camouflage.
2. **Keep it generic and multi-voice.** The page represents a community/content hub, not one person. Posts can feel like they come from different contributors. This aligns with B1-B3 having multiple different narrators.
3. **Match the tone of the ads.** Anti-hype, science-forward, honest. No "game-changing" or "miracle" language. The page and the ads should feel like they belong together.
4. **Repost real content when possible.** Share actual health articles, NIH studies, reputable wellness content. This builds the page's content graph in Meta's system and makes it look legitimate.
5. **Engage with comments on the page.** If people comment on ads and visit the page, seeing unanswered comments signals "fake." Respond to everything, even if briefly.
