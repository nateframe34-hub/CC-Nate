# Listicle C Image Prompts — B6: The Reluctant Decliner

> **Listicle:** "How a 47-Year-Old Accidentally Replaced Her Entire Supplement Shelf"
> **Batch:** 6 (Accidental Ideal Outcome — Reluctant Decliner)
> **Tool:** Nano Banana Pro (Google Gemini). Also compatible with GPT Image, Midjourney, DALL-E 3, Flux.
> **Critical rule:** These images must feel casual and editorial — like lifestyle photography from a real health article. Not aspirational wellness-influencer energy. Real, warm, lived-in. Matches the "accidental discovery" tone of the listicle.

---

## Image 1: The Cluttered Supplement Shelf (Before)

**Placement:** Top of article, below the headline and intro paragraph about the full cabinet.

**Purpose:** Shows the "before" — the chaos of 5+ bottles accumulated over time. The reader sees her own cabinet. This image anchors the entire listicle narrative: she started HERE and ended up with one bottle.

**Prompt:**

```
A photorealistic photograph of a medicine cabinet or bathroom shelf with 5-6 supplement bottles of different heights, colors, and brands crowded together. A bottle of CoQ10 with an orange label, a resveratrol supplement with a dark red label, a vitamin D bottle, a generic multivitamin with a green label, and a magnesium bottle with a blue cap. Some bottles are half-empty. One has the cap off. A greens powder tub is squeezed in at the end, slightly too tall for the shelf. A few loose capsules sit on the shelf surface between the bottles. The shelf itself is white or light wood, slightly dusty in the corners. The arrangement is not styled — it looks like months of accumulation without organization. Natural bathroom lighting, slightly cool overhead tone. Shot straight-on at eye level, smartphone camera quality. The mood is: this is what happens when you keep trying and nothing works. 4:5 aspect ratio.
```

**Post-production notes:**
- This pairs with Image 4 (the "after" — one bottle on a clean shelf). The contrast is the visual story of the listicle.
- None of these bottles should look like Baseline. They're the separate supplements she was buying before.
- If any AI-generated labels reference real brands, blur them in post.

---

## Image 2: The Late-Night Research Moment

**Placement:** Inside Section 1 ("She Didn't Know About NAD+"), after the line about her doctor and friend never mentioning it.

**Purpose:** Captures the quiet discovery moment — the night she started reading. This is relatable for the Reluctant Decliner: the late-night scroll, the "wait, what is this?" moment. It's how this audience actually discovers new information.

**Prompt:**

```
A photorealistic photograph of a laptop screen in a dark room, photographed from behind someone's shoulder. The screen shows a blurred health article or research page — white text on a screen with what appears to be a scientific article layout. The room is dark except for the screen glow and a small table lamp casting warm light on a coffee mug beside the laptop. The person is barely visible — just a shoulder and the back of their head, slightly out of focus. A phone sits face-down on the table beside the laptop. The time on the laptop menu bar reads 11:47 PM. The mood is: quiet discovery, alone, after everyone else is asleep. The blue-white screen glow contrasts with the warm lamp light. Intimate, private, curious. Smartphone camera quality, shot from slightly behind and to the side, shallow depth of field with the screen slightly blurred. 4:5 aspect ratio.
```

**Post-production notes:**
- The screen content should be blurred enough that no specific text is readable. It's about the MOMENT, not the article.
- The time (11:47 PM) reinforces the "discovery happened quietly, late at night" narrative.
- No branding, no product visible. Pure editorial.

---

## Image 3: NAD+ Decline Graph

**Placement:** Inside Section 1, after the NAD+ mechanism explanation.

**Purpose:** Visualizes the decline she just read about. Same function as the other listicle graphs — makes the science tangible.

**Build method:** Canva — same style as B4's graph.

**Canva specs:**
- **Size:** 1080×720 (3:2) or 16:9
- **Background:** White with subtle gray gridlines
- **Graph:** Single smooth curve in deep teal (#0c3b3f) showing NAD+ decline from age 20-70
- **Annotation at age 40:** Dotted vertical line with "Decline accelerates"
- **Additional annotation at age 47:** Small teal dot on the curve with "← She started here" in small text
- **Typography:** Plus Jakarta Sans, clean, minimal
- **Bottom:** "Based on published research. Illustration simplified for clarity." in 10pt gray

**Post-production notes:**
- The "She started here" annotation at age 47 personalizes the graph to the listicle's character. Small touch, big impact — the graph stops being abstract and becomes her specific story.
- No branding.

## Image 4: One Bottle on Clean Shelf (After)

**Placement:** Inside Section 4 ("She Found a Formula That Had Everything"), after the ingredient list.

**Purpose:** The visual punchline. Same shelf as Image 1, but now it's clean — one white bottle with purple accent standing alone with space around it. The contrast with Image 1 IS the argument: simplicity after chaos, one solution after dozens of failures.

**Prompt:**

```
A photorealistic photograph of the same medicine cabinet or bathroom shelf from Image 1, but now almost completely empty. A single white supplement bottle with subtle purple/violet label accents stands alone on the shelf, with clean open space on either side. The shelf is visibly cleaner — no dust, no spilled capsules. One glass of water sits beside the bottle. The lighting is slightly warmer than Image 1 — same bathroom, but morning light is coming through a window now, casting a gentle warm tone across the clean shelf. The bottle is not perfectly centered — placed casually, slightly to the left, as if someone just put it back after taking their morning dose. The mood is: resolution, simplicity, one answer instead of many. Smartphone camera quality, shot straight-on at eye level, warm color temperature. 4:5 aspect ratio.
```

**Post-production notes:**
- This ONLY works in contrast with Image 1. Same shelf, same angle, different reality.
- The bottle should be recognizable as Baseline (white + purple) but label text doesn't need to be perfectly readable.
- Optional small caption below: "One bottle. Two capsules. That's the whole routine."

---

## Image 5: The "Week 3" Moment (Optional)

**Placement:** Inside Section 5 ("She Almost Quit at Week 2"), at the "week 3" realization.

**Purpose:** Captures the subtle moment — not a dramatic transformation photo, but a quiet everyday moment where she realizes something changed. Matches the listicle's tone: "It's not a lightning bolt. It's more like someone quietly turned the lights back up."

**Prompt:**

```
A photorealistic candid photograph of a woman in her late 40s sitting at a kitchen table with a coffee mug, looking out a window with calm morning light on her face. She has a neutral, slightly surprised expression — as if she just realized something small but significant. Her posture is relaxed, upright, not slumped. She wears a simple crew neck sweater, hair in a loose ponytail. The kitchen is warm and lived-in — a few dishes drying on a rack, a fruit bowl, a school schedule pinned to the fridge. The coffee mug is her first of the day and she hasn't reached for a second. The image communicates a quiet, private moment of realization — not celebration, not dramatic change, just: something is different today. Natural morning window light, warm tones, shallow depth of field. 4:5 aspect ratio.
```

**Post-production notes:**
- This is the most delicate image. It should NOT look like a "wellness transformation" photo. No glow, no yoga pose, no aspirational energy.
- She looks normal. Rested, but normal. The point is the absence of exhaustion, not the presence of vitality.
- If the AI makes her look too polished or too "wellness influencer," regenerate. She should look like someone you'd meet at book club.
- Optional image — the listicle works without it.

---

## Image Priority

1. **Cluttered Shelf (before)** — anchors the entire narrative, instant recognition
2. **One Bottle (after)** — visual punchline, only works if Image 1 is used
3. **NAD+ Decline Graph** — makes the science tangible. Build in Canva.
4. **Late-Night Research** — sets the discovery mood, secondary but atmospheric
5. **Week 3 Moment** — optional. Only include if it looks genuinely natural and unposed.
