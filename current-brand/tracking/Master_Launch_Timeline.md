## PHASE 4: AD LAUNCH + DISCOVERY (Day 0 to Day 28)

### Day 0 — Launch Day

- B1-B3 purchase campaigns go live at 12:01 AM (Meta's timezone)
- $50/day CBO budget
- **Do not touch anything for 7 days.** Resist the urge.

### Week 1 (Day 0-7) — Hands Off

| Action | Frequency |
|--------|-----------|
| Check Meta dashboard | 1x/day max |
| Continue Brand Page organic posts (Week 4 content) | 5 posts |
| Continue Sarah's posts (Week 4 — natural recommendation loop) | 4-5 posts |
| Continue Ad Partner Page posts | 2-3 posts |
| Reply to all comments/DMs across all pages | Daily |
| **Do NOT edit ads, budgets, or targeting** | — |

### Week 2 (Day 8-14) — First Read

| Action | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| **Read the data** | Which ad set is getting spend? Which ads have purchases? Which have high CTR but no conversions? |
| Kill obvious losers | If an ad has $0 in purchases after $50+ spend with bad CTR — pause it (not the ad set, just the ad) |
| Continue all organic posting | Normal cadence across all 3 pages |
| **Brand Page hits Week 5** — first real CTA/offer post | Brand Post Plan, Week 5 Day 5 |

### Week 3-4 (Day 15-28) — Pattern Recognition

| Action | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| **Identify winning ad set** | One ad set should be getting majority of CBO spend + purchases |
| **Identify winning angle** | Within the winning ad set, which of the 3 angles converts best? |
| Continue organic content | Both pages ongoing monthly rhythm |
| **Set up post-purchase survey** after 20+ sales | "How did you hear about us?" |
| Prepare Champion Ad Set brief | Winning sub-avatar + winning angle → 3 new format variations |
