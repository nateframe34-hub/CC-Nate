## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The launch copy is strong — emotionally specific, avatar-targeted, and structurally sound within the Origins 3:2:2 framework. However, seven independent analyses converged on the same core finding: **structural repetition across batches creates a sameness problem that will limit Meta's ability to find winners.**

### Top 5 Action Items (Priority Order)

1. **Break the narrative skeleton** — All 5 native copies use the same 7-step structure. Rewrite at least 2 batches in a different format (listicle, Q&A, thread-style)
2. **Fix compliance risks** — Remove specific percentages ("50%", "85%"), soften "DNA repair" language, add disclaimers
3. **Produce 1 UGC video** — The biggest performance gap is zero video creative. Even one 30-60s testimonial-style video will dramatically improve CPM
4. **Reduce from 5 to 3 ad sets at launch** — 5 simultaneous ad sets at $20-30/day each dilutes budget. Start with B1, B3, B4 (strongest differentiation)
5. **Differentiate CTAs** — "Restore your baseline" appears across almost every batch. Each batch needs a CTA matched to its specific emotional payoff

## 1. COPYWRITING REVIEW

### Strengths
- Voice is consistent: clean, scientific, anti-hype. Not a single "revolutionary" or "game-changing"
- Authority figure pattern works well (molecular biologist, biochemist, colleague) — feels organic, not manufactured
- The "Week 1: nothing / Week 3: wait / Week 6: results" timeline is the strongest proof pattern in the document
- Batch 3 (Skeptic's NMN) is the best batch overall — validation of skepticism before asking for belief is powerful
- Customer language integration is strong — "I turned 44 and my body just... stopped cooperating" reads as real

### Structural Issues

**CRITICAL: All 5 native body copies use an identical 7-step narrative structure:**

| Step | Pattern | Appears In |
|------|---------|------------|
| 1 | Personal pain hook (1-2 sentences) | All 5 batches |
| 2 | Expand the pain with specifics | All 5 batches |
| 3 | List failed solutions (3-4 items) | All 5 batches |
| 4 | Authority figure introduces NAD+ | All 5 batches |
| 5 | NAD+ mechanism paragraph | All 5 batches |
| 6 | "Started taking Baseline NMN. Week X..." | All 5 batches |
| 7 | Emotional resolution + CTA | All 5 batches |

Someone scrolling through Facebook who sees Batches 1 and 2 back-to-back will recognize the pattern. This kills the camouflage effect — the ads start looking like ads.

**The mechanism paragraph is near-identical across 8 of 10 body copies:** *(RESOLVED — mechanism paragraphs rewritten with varied language per batch)*
- ~~"NAD+ — a molecule your cells use to produce energy and repair DNA"~~ → Now uses "cellular maintenance" consistently
- ~~"NMN is the precursor your body uses to restore/rebuild NAD+ levels"~~ → Each batch now uses a distinct mechanism delivery (tank metaphor, fuel reframe, direct statement, etc.)
- "Not a stimulant. Not a [X]." — reduced but retained where it serves the specific angle

**"Not X. Not Y." pattern appears 7 times across the document:**
- "Not a stimulant. Not a vitamin."
- "Not a stimulant. Not a band-aid."
- "Not caffeine. Not willpower."
- "Not a miracle. Not a cure."
- "Not a cosmetic fix. A cellular one."
- "Not a spike. A baseline."
- "Not another supplement. A precursor."

### CTA Weakness
Almost every CTA is a variation of "Restore your baseline" or "Baseline NMN." The CTAs don't differentiate by avatar or emotional state:
- B1: "Restore your baseline" / "Restore what was lost"
- B2: "Shop Baseline" / "Try Baseline NMN"
- B3: "Try Baseline NMN" / "Baseline NMN"
- B4: "Restore your baseline" / "Show up"
- B5: "Shop Baseline" / "Baseline NMN"

**Recommendation:** Match CTAs to the emotional payoff of each batch:
- B1: "Stop declining. Start restoring."
- B2: "Ditch the third cup."
- B3: "One more try. This time it's different."
- B4: "Be the parent who says yes."
- B5: "Function first. Everything else follows."

### Line-Level Edits

**B1 Body 1, Line 55:** "By age 50, you've lost roughly half of it. Not 'maybe.' Published research." → Compliance issue. Replace with: "By your 40s and 50s, levels decline significantly. Multiple peer-reviewed studies have documented this."

**B1 Body 2, Line 75:** "~50% of NAD+" → Remove the percentage. Use "significant decline" or "measurable decline."

**B4 Body 1, Line 313:** "Last Saturday my daughter asked me to take her to the park. I said maybe later. She stopped asking." → This is the single strongest opening line in the entire document. Don't touch it.

**B5 Body 2, Line 397-399:** "That's not a diagnosis. That's a dismissal." → Powerful, but risks alienating women whose doctors ARE helping them. Consider: "That answer felt incomplete."

## 3. MARKETING PSYCHOLOGY REVIEW

### Sophistication Ranking (Highest → Lowest)

| Rank | Batch | Score | Key Mechanism |
|------|-------|-------|---------------|
| 1 | B3 — Skeptic's NMN | 9/10 | Cognitive Dissonance Resolution — validates existing belief before introducing exception |
| 2 | B4 — Exhausted Parent | 8.5/10 | Parental Guilt Activation + Identity Threat (not being the parent you want to be) |
| 3 | B1 — Reluctant Decliner | 7/10 | Loss Aversion framing (you've already lost something) |
| 4 | B2 — 3pm Wall Hitter | 6.5/10 | Problem Reframing (not a caffeine problem, a cellular fuel problem) |
| 5 | B5 — Invisible Woman | 6/10 | Medical Gaslighting frame — powerful but potentially dangerous |

### Dangerous Patterns Flagged

**B5 — "The Invisible Woman" (Medical Gaslighting Risk):**
- "Your doctor said 'it's just perimenopause'" / "That's not a diagnosis. That's a dismissal."
- This positions Baseline NMN as an alternative to medical advice. If someone with a genuine medical condition reads this and delays treatment because they bought NMN instead, the liability is significant.
- **Recommendation:** Reframe from "your doctor is wrong" to "your doctor is right about the diagnosis, but there may be more you can do at the cellular level."

**B4 — "Exhausted Parent" (Ironic Process Theory):**
- "She stopped asking" triggers guilt so intense it can create psychological reactance — the reader may reject the ad to protect themselves from the feeling
- The copy works, but it's emotionally sharp enough that a subset of readers will associate Baseline with the bad feeling, not the solution
- **Recommendation:** Keep the line (it's the strongest hook) but ensure the resolution is equally powerful. Currently, "She just grabbed my hand and ran to the swings" delivers. Don't weaken the opening — just make sure the payoff lands.

**Authority Figure Pattern — Formulaic:**
- B1: "a friend — a molecular biologist, not a supplement guy"
- B2: "My sister sent me an article"
- B3: "my brother-in-law — biochemist, not a health guru"
- B4: (no authority figure — uses personal discovery)
- B5: "A colleague mentioned NAD+"
- The "credentialed friend/relative explains science" pattern is used 4/5 times. Vary the discovery mechanism. Some options: podcast episode, accidental discovery, doctor who actually recommended it, research rabbit hole at 2am.

### Psychological Principles Successfully Deployed
- **Loss aversion** (you've already lost NAD+) — stronger than gain framing
- **Cognitive specificity** (naming NAD+ as the exact cause) — moves from vague problem to specific solution
- **Social proof via narrative** (first-person testimonial format) — more credible than "1000+ customers"
- **Delayed gratification framing** ("Week 1: nothing") — sets honest expectations, builds trust
- **Identity-based motivation** (B4: "I'm not the parent I want to be") — strongest behavioral driver

## 4. PAID ADS STRATEGY REVIEW

### Budget & Structure

**Current plan:** 5 ad sets × $20-30/day = $100-$150 CAD/day total
**Problem:** At $20/day per ad set with 12 variations each, Meta needs ~50 conversions per ad set to exit learning phase. At a hypothetical $30 CPA, that's $1,500 per ad set = $7,500 total minimum before you have statistically valid data. At $100-150/day, that takes 50-75 days.

**Recommendation:** Launch 3 ad sets, not 5.
- **B1 (Reluctant Decliner)** — broadest audience, fastest to get data
- **B3 (Skeptic's NMN)** — strongest creative differentiation, best hook
- **B4 (Exhausted Parent)** — highest emotional intensity, likely highest CTR
- Hold B2 and B5 for Week 3-4 rotation

### Meta Ads Manager Setup

**Use Advantage+ Creative** instead of separate creative/copy/headline variations. Advantage+ lets Meta dynamically combine your assets. Upload:
- All 3 creatives per batch as separate images
- Both body copies as text options
- Both headlines as headline options
- The description
- Meta assembles combinations automatically. This replaces the manual 3×2×2 structure and gives Meta more optimization flexibility.

**Targeting:** Go broad. Age + gender + geo only. No interest targeting. Let the algorithm find your buyers through the creative, not the targeting. For B5, restrict to Women 40-55.

### Creative Format Gaps

| Format | Status | Impact |
|--------|--------|--------|
| Static images | 15 concepts ready | Good baseline |
| Video (UGC) | 0 | **Critical gap** — video typically delivers 30-50% lower CPM on Meta |
| Carousel | 0 | Medium gap — good for the "Week 1/3/6" timeline |
| Stories/Reels (9:16) | 0 | Medium gap — all creatives are 1:1 only |

**Minimum viable video:** Film a 45-second selfie-style testimonial matching B1's body copy. iPhone, natural light, no production value. This single video will likely outperform all 15 static creatives.

### Compliance Risks (Meta Ad Review)

| Issue | Location | Risk Level | Fix |
|-------|----------|------------|-----|
| "50% of NAD+" | B1 Body 1, B1 Body 2 | HIGH — specific health claim | Replace with "significant decline" |
| "85% of women" | B5C2 creative | HIGH — unattributed stat | Remove or add "[Source]" |
| "DNA repair" | B1 Body 1, B1 Body 2 | MEDIUM — implies disease treatment | Use "cellular maintenance" |
| "Your doctor said..." | B5 Body 2 | MEDIUM — anti-medical framing | Soften to "felt incomplete" |
| "She stopped asking" | B4 Body 1 | LOW — emotional manipulation claim possible | Likely fine, monitor |
| Before/after implied | B3 Timeline creative | LOW — no images, text only | Fine |

### Retargeting Setup (Missing)

**No retargeting plan exists.** Add:
- **Day 0-7 visitors:** Serve B3 (Skeptic's NMN) creative to people who clicked but didn't buy
- **Add-to-cart abandoners:** Serve direct response copy (shorter, CTA-focused)
- **Email capture:** Consider a "Learn more about NAD+" lead magnet for top-of-funnel traffic that isn't ready to buy

## 5. MARKETING STRATEGY (PMM) REVIEW

### Product-Brand Alignment Risk (Priority #1)

**The copy positions Baseline as a pure NMN brand.** Every body copy, creative, and mechanism centers NMN as a single-ingredient play. But the actual launch product is a 10-in-1 proprietary blend.

**Risk scenario:** Customer reads ad about NMN → visits website → sees "10-in-1 formula" → cognitive dissonance → bounces. Or worse: buys it, flips the bottle, reads the label, feels deceived.

**Mitigation options (choose one):**
1. **Label it honestly:** "NMN + 9 supporting compounds for cellular energy" — positions the blend as NMN-first with helpers
2. **Lead with NMN, acknowledge the blend:** Add one line to landing page: "Built around 500mg NMN, supported by 9 complementary ingredients for absorption and cellular function"
3. **Don't mention the blend at all** — let the NMN story carry and only address on FAQ page

**Recommendation:** Option 2. The copy doesn't lie — Baseline IS an NMN formula with 500mg NMN. But the landing page needs to handle the "what else is in it?" question before trust erodes.

### Funnel Gap

**Current funnel:** Ad → Product Page → Purchase
**Missing:** No middle-of-funnel content exists.

The copy does a great job educating about NAD+ decline, but there's no bridge for people who are interested but not ready to buy. Consider:
- An NAD+ education landing page (not product page) for top-of-funnel traffic
- An email sequence for people who clicked but didn't convert
- A "Why NMN?" comparison page for the skeptic audience (B3)

### Mechanism Redundancy

**Mechanisms #4 (NAD+ Decline) and #5 (Restoration Not Stimulation) appear in every batch.** This is intentional — they're the only two mechanisms available at launch — but it limits testing signal. You can't test which mechanism resonates more when both appear in every ad.

**Recommendation:** Isolate mechanisms by batch:
- B1, B3, B5: Lead with Mechanism #4 (NAD+ Decline — the "why")
- B2, B4: Lead with Mechanism #5 (Restoration Not Stimulation — the "how")
- This gives clearer signal on which angle converts

### Landscape Snapshot

| Hello100 | Liposomal delivery premium | ~$90 USD | Patented delivery system |
| Tru Niagen | Pharma-grade, NR not NMN | ~$50 USD | ChromaDex patents, clinical trials |

### Where Baseline Sits

Baseline occupies a unique position none of these competitors own: **emotionally honest, outcome-driven, anti-hype.** This is a "fourth tier" in the market:

- **Tier 1:** Science/longevity (ProHealth, Tru Niagen) — speaks to biohackers
- **Tier 2:** Premium lifestyle (Wonderfeel, Hello100) — speaks to wellness-forward consumers
- **Tier 3:** Value/trust (GenuinePurity, Renue) — speaks to skeptical shoppers
- **Tier 4 (Baseline):** Emotional specificity — speaks to real people with real problems

### Competitive Advantages
1. **Emotional specificity** — No competitor writes to sub-avatars this precisely. ProHealth writes to "longevity enthusiasts." Baseline writes to "the dad who said 'maybe later' and his daughter stopped asking."
2. **Anti-hype voice** — Competitors use "revolutionary," "breakthrough," "cutting-edge." Baseline's refusal to do this IS the differentiation.
3. **Restoration framework** — "Restoration not stimulation" is a categorically different positioning than "anti-aging" or "longevity." It's warmer, more accessible, less intimidating.
4. **Camouflage format** — No competitor is running ads that look like personal posts. They all run ads that look like ads.

### Competitive Gaps
1. **Same NAD+ stat as everyone** — "By 50, you've lost 50% of NAD+" appears on ProHealth, Wonderfeel, OMRE, and GenuinePurity's sites. It's not differentiated. When you remove the percentage (for compliance), you actually become MORE differentiated.
2. **No quantified clinical proof** — Tru Niagen has published clinical trials. ProHealth cites Uthever studies. Baseline has no product-specific evidence. The "published research" claim in B1 is about NAD+ decline generally, not about Baseline specifically. Be careful here.
3. **No ownable phrase** — "Restore your baseline" is close but not locked in. **"Raise your baseline"** is stronger — it implies improvement, not just maintenance. Consider owning this.
4. **Missing prevention angle** — No batch targets 30-somethings who want to prevent decline. Competitors target 40-60. A "start before the drop" angle for 30-35 year olds is an untapped segment.

### Ownable Category Phrase

**"Raise your baseline."** This should become the brand tagline, not just a CTA. It's:
- Action-oriented (unlike "restore," which implies damage)
- Brand-embedded (Baseline is literally in the phrase)
- Aspirational but grounded (not "reverse aging" or "unlock longevity")
- Unique in the NMN market (no competitor uses it)

## 8. NAD+ FACT-CHECK

### Claims Reviewed

| Claim | Accuracy | Risk | Action |
|-------|----------|------|--------|
| "By age 50, you've lost roughly half of NAD+" | PARTIALLY ACCURATE — based on Massudi et al. (2012) human skin tissue study. Widely cited but methodology debated. Not a universal whole-body measurement. | MEDIUM | Remove specific percentage. Use "significant age-related decline" with citation to general NAD+ research |
| "85% of women over 40 report unexplained fatigue" | UNVERIFIED — no clear source for this exact statistic. Various fatigue surveys exist but this number appears to be interpolated or fabricated. | HIGH | Remove entirely or replace with a verifiable statistic from a named study |
| "NMN is the precursor your body uses to rebuild NAD+" | ACCURATE — NMN (nicotinamide mononucleotide) is a direct biosynthetic precursor to NAD+ via the salvage pathway | LOW | No change needed |
| "Responsible for energy production and DNA repair" | ACCURATE but OVERSIMPLIFIED — NAD+ is involved in hundreds of enzymatic reactions including sirtuins (DNA repair/longevity), PARPs (DNA repair), and mitochondrial function (energy) | MEDIUM | "DNA repair" specifically may trigger FDA/FTC scrutiny as a disease-prevention claim. Use "cellular maintenance" instead |
| "Not a stimulant" | ACCURATE — NMN does not act on the central nervous system like caffeine | LOW | No change needed |
| "The actual raw material your cells need" | ACCURATE — NMN is enzymatically converted to NAD+ intracellularly | LOW | No change needed |

### FDA/FTC Compliance Notes
- **Structure/function claims** (what NMN does in the body) are generally permissible for dietary supplements under DSHEA
- **Disease claims** (treats, cures, prevents disease) are NOT permissible without FDA drug approval
- "DNA repair" edges toward disease territory — cellular maintenance is safer
- "Energy production" is a permissible structure/function claim
- Any specific percentage claim ("50%", "85%") requires substantiation on file
- **Required disclaimer** (if making structure/function claims): "These statements have not been evaluated by the FDA. This product is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease."

## 9. BRAINSTORMING — CREATIVE GAPS

### 5 Expansion Opportunities Identified

1. **Format diversity** — Add at least one of: listicle format, Q&A format, "myth vs fact" format, Reddit-style post, or thread/carousel format. Breaking the narrative skeleton is the single highest-ROI change.

2. **Social proof creative** — None of the 15 creatives use customer reviews, ratings, or social proof. Consider: a creative showing a 5-star review screenshot or a "476 people ordered this month" social proof bar.

3. **Recovery/athlete sub-avatar** — Missing from launch copy. Sub-avatar #3 (The Weekend Warrior) or #5 (The Performance Optimizer) could open a fitness-adjacent audience. This could be Batch 6 after initial results.

4. **Video content** — Zero video in the launch plan. Even one UGC-style testimonial video would likely outperform all static creatives. This is the #1 gap echoed across paid ads, frontend design, and competitive analysis.

5. **"What's in the bottle" transparency creative** — A clean, minimal shot of the actual product with just "500mg NMN. 120 capsules. That's it." addresses the skeptic and the blend concern simultaneously without lying.

1. **B4 opening line** — "Last Saturday my daughter asked me to take her to the park. I said maybe later. She stopped asking." (Strongest hook in the entire document)
2. **B3 closing line** — "I'm still skeptical of supplements. I'm just not skeptical of this one." (Perfect cognitive dissonance resolution)
