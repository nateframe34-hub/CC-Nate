Brand Identity — Phase 1

Baseline

Three color palette directions grounded in customer research. Each maps to the brand pillars of transparency, proof, simplicity, and restoration — while differentiating from every major NMN competitor.

Palette A
Trusted Signal
Navy · Warm Amber · Clean White
Primary — Deep Navy #0F2440
Secondary — Slate Blue #1B3A5C
Accent — Warm Amber #D4943A
Background — Warm Ivory #F6F4F0
Text — Near Black #1A1A1A
Rationale

Navy is the strongest trust-signaling color in consumer research — critical when the #1 purchase barrier is distrust. Warm amber prevents clinical coldness and ties to "energy restoration." No major NMN competitor owns deep navy. Warm ivory background feels premium without sterility.

Palette B
Quiet Authority
Charcoal · Sage · Warm Stone
Primary — Charcoal #1E1E1E
Secondary — Graphite #4A4A4A
Accent — Sage #7A9B7E
Background — Warm Stone #F2EDE6
Text — Near Black #1A1A1A
Rationale

Charcoal communicates confidence and premium positioning — the brand doesn't need to shout. Sage bridges health and nature without the "crunchy supplement" cliché. Warm stone keeps it approachable for The Restorer avatar. This palette says "Apple Store meets apothecary" — clean, quiet, certain.

Palette C
Modern Apothecary
Deep Teal · Copper · Cream
Primary — Deep Teal #0C3B3F
Secondary — Verdigris #165C5A
Accent — Copper #C07840
Background — Cream #F5F0E8
Text — Near Black #1A1A1A
Rationale

Teal uniquely bridges science (blue) and health (green) — no NMN competitor uses it. Copper signals precision and craft, evoking lab instruments and measured quality. Cream keeps it warm and human. The most distinctive and ownable palette of the three — strongest shelf differentiation.
