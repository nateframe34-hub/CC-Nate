# PDP Audit: From Solid to Irresistible

> **NOTE (March 2026):** We are currently running **only the Baseline NMN 10-in-1** supplement. This audit was written against an earlier PDP version that positioned the product as "pure NMN" with single-ingredient and QR-to-COA language. Those references describe an **original concept that is not the current product**. A standalone pure NMN product is a **future goal, contingent on the 10-in-1 performing well**. The rewrites in this document have been updated for the 10-in-1, but legacy references to "pure NMN," "one ingredient," and "enzymatic synthesis" remain in the audit sections for context.

## The 3 Questions This Audit Answers

1. **Are the ads and PDP congruent across all 5 batches?**
2. **Does the PDP capture desire in the first 3 seconds?**
3. **Does the PDP answer every question the buyer has?**

**Bottom line:** The PDP is currently a 2-3% CVR page. It's clean, compliant, and well-structured. But it's a *product page*, not a *conversion page*. The difference between 2% and 5% isn't more information — it's the RIGHT information in the RIGHT order hitting the RIGHT emotional state at the EXACT moment the visitor arrives.

## AUDIT 1: Ad-to-PDP Congruence

### The Core Problem

Every ad batch sells an EMOTIONAL OUTCOME. The PDP hero sells PRODUCT SPECIFICATIONS. That's the single biggest CVR killer on the page.

When someone clicks an ad, they're in an emotional state. The PDP's job is to CONTINUE that emotional thread, not start a new one. Right now, the PDP breaks the thread in the first second.

### Batch-by-Batch Breakdown

---

#### BATCH 1: The Reluctant Decliner → PDP

**Ad emotional state:** "I'm quietly scared about my decline. I just found out there might be a specific, addressable cause."

**Ad hooks that brought them here:**
- "What if 'just getting older' is actually just running low on one molecule?"
- "You can feel the trajectory. This changes it."
- "I didn't expect miracles. I just wanted to feel normal again."

**What they land on (PDP Hero):**
> "Baseline NMN — Pure NMN Supplement, 500mg, 60 Capsules"
> "Support your body's natural cellular energy with pure NMN. Not a stimulant — a foundation. 99%+ purity, enzymatic synthesis, independently tested every batch."

**Congruence Score: 3/10**

**The disconnect:** They clicked because of HOPE — "wait, there's a reason AND a fix?" They land on a supplement facts sheet. The emotional thread snaps instantly. The word "NMN" means nothing to someone who just discovered NAD+ from the ad. Leading with "Pure NMN Supplement, 500mg, 60 Capsules" is like answering "what's wrong with me?" with a SKU number.

**What they need to see first:** The SAME reframe that hooked them — their decline has a name, a cause, and a solution. Then the product.

## AUDIT 2: First 3 Seconds

### What the PDP Hero Currently Says

```
Title:     Baseline NMN — Pure NMN Supplement, 500mg, 60 Capsules
Subtitle:  Support your body's natural cellular energy with pure NMN.
           Not a stimulant — a foundation. 99%+ purity, enzymatic
           synthesis, independently tested every batch.
Who:       For adults 40+ who want to support their cellular health
           with real science, not hype.
Price:     ~~$55~~ $49/mo with subscription | $55 one-time purchase
```

### The 3-Second Test (What the Eye Hits First)

On mobile (where 80%+ of Meta traffic lands), the visitor sees:
1. Product image (bottle shot)
2. Title: "Baseline NMN — Pure NMN Supplement, 500mg, 60 Capsules"
3. Maybe the first line of the subtitle

That's it. That's the 3-second window. And here's what it communicates:
- **"NMN"** — means nothing to most cold traffic. They just learned what NAD+ is from the ad.
- **"500mg, 60 Capsules"** — product spec. Zero emotional resonance.
- **"Support your body's natural cellular energy"** — structure/function claim that sounds like every supplement ever made.

### What a 5% CVR Hero Communicates in 3 Seconds

A 5% hero answers THREE questions instantly:

1. **"Am I in the right place?"** — The visitor needs to see the SAME emotional thread from the ad. If the ad said "your decline has a cause," the hero needs to continue that thought.

2. **"Is this for someone like me?"** — Not "adults 40+" (nobody identifies as that). Specific experiences: "If you hit a wall every afternoon..." "If you've tried supplements that did nothing..."

3. **"Can I trust this?"** — Social proof (star rating + count) and risk reversal (guarantee) visible without scrolling.

### What's Missing Above the Fold

| Element | Current PDP | 5% CVR PDP |
|---------|------------|------------|
| **Emotional headline** | Product name + specs | Desire-mirroring statement |
| **Star rating + review count** | Not above fold | "4.8/5 from 200+ reviews" visible at title level |
| **$/day reframe** | Not above fold | "$0.83/day — less than your afternoon coffee" |
| **Guarantee badge** | Not above fold | "30-Day Money Back" badge near CTA |
| **"Who it's for" specificity** | "Adults 40+" | Specific experiences they recognize |
| **Supply duration** | Not emphasized | "60-Day Supply — Feel the difference before you run out" |

### The "Who It's For" Line is Actively Hurting CVR

Current: *"For adults 40+ who want to support their cellular health with real science, not hype."*

Nobody identifies as someone who "wants to support their cellular health." That's marketing language. People identify as:
- "I hit a wall every afternoon and coffee doesn't fix it"
- "I've tried supplements that promised everything and delivered nothing"
- "I feel like I've aged 10 years in the last 2"
- "I just want enough energy to keep up with my kids"

The "Who It's For" line should be a self-selection filter that makes the RIGHT person think "that's me" — not a demographic label.

**Q: Is this the same NMN from longevity research?**

**Q: What's the difference between NMN and NR?**

Both NMN and NR (nicotinamide riboside) are NAD+ precursors. NMN is one step closer to NAD+ in the metabolic pathway — your body converts NMN directly into NAD+ without the extra conversion step that NR requires. Published research on both molecules continues to evolve. We chose NMN because of the growing body of human clinical evidence supporting it.

**Q: What happens if I stop taking NMN?**

NMN isn't addictive and doesn't create dependency. If you stop, your body continues producing NAD+ naturally — just at the lower age-related levels it was producing before supplementation. Most people who stop notice a gradual return to how they felt previously over a few weeks. There's no withdrawal or negative effect from stopping.

**Q: Is Baseline NMN vegan / allergen-free?**

Yes. Baseline NMN 10-in-1 uses vegetable cellulose capsules. No gelatin. No gluten. No dairy. No soy. No nuts. No artificial colors or preservatives. Suitable for vegan diets.

> **[EDIT NOTE: Removed "one ingredient (NMN)" — the current product is a 10-in-1 formula with 500mg NMN + 8 synergistic ingredients, not a single-ingredient product. "One ingredient" language is for a future pure NMN product, contingent on the 10-in-1 performing well.]**

**Q: Can I take NMN during perimenopause or menopause?**

Yes. NMN is commonly used by women in perimenopause and menopause. NAD+ decline happens alongside hormonal changes — both contribute to the fatigue and reduced energy many women experience during this transition. NMN supports NAD+ production at the cellular level, which addresses the energy side of the equation. As always, consult your doctor if you take prescription medications.

**Q: Why capsules instead of sublingual or powder?**

Capsules provide consistent, measurable dosing — you get exactly 250mg per capsule, 500mg per daily serving. Recent research has shown that oral NMN is well-absorbed, reaching the bloodstream effectively. Capsules are also more convenient for daily use and more stable for storage than powder or sublingual formats.

## The Principle Behind All 10 Changes

**The ads sell the FEELING. The PDP must continue the feeling, then add the PROOF.**

Current PDP: Product specs → Education → Trust → Reviews → Compare → CTA
5% CVR PDP: **Emotional mirror → "That's me" → How it works → Trust proof → Social proof → Compare → Irresistible offer**

The order matters as much as the content. Lead with desire. Educate second. Prove third. Close last.

Every element on the page should answer one of three questions:
1. **"Do they understand me?"** (Sections 1-2)
2. **"Can I trust them?"** (Sections 3-5)
3. **"Is this worth it?"** (Sections 6-7)

If you answer those three in that order, you get a 5% PDP.
