## Executive Summary

The NMN/NAD+ supplement advertising landscape on Meta is moderately competitive but surprisingly underdifferentiated. Most brands cluster around the same 2-3 copy angles (science/clinical proof, NAD+ decline with age, energy/vitality), use similar visual formats, and largely ignore emotional storytelling, lifestyle aspiration, and community-building in their paid creative.

**Key findings:**
- **ProHealth Longevity** is the most aggressive Meta advertiser in the NMN space, with dedicated Facebook ad landing pages and heavy clinical-proof messaging
- **Wonderfeel** leads on premium brand positioning and patented formulation storytelling but appears to invest more in content marketing/SEO than Meta paid ads
- **Elysium Health (Basis)** and **Tru Niagen (Niagen Bioscience)** are the most established players but have pulled back on aggressive health claims due to NAD (National Advertising Division) regulatory actions
- **Omre** relies heavily on syndicated PR and SEO rather than Meta ads
- **Renue By Science** and **Alive By Science** (now the same company) have minimal visible Meta ad presence and were flagged by both FDA and BBB for advertising compliance issues
- **GenF20** operates in the adjacent HGH space with aggressive direct-response tactics but faces class-action scrutiny for false advertising
- **Cymbiotika** is the dark horse -- running longevity supplement campaigns for 400+ days on Meta, indicating strong ROAS

**The biggest opportunity:** Authentic, UGC-driven creative with emotional storytelling that bridges the gap between clinical science and felt human experience. Almost no NMN brand is doing this well on Meta.

## Common Patterns Across All Competitors

### Messaging Patterns
1. **NAD+ decline with age** -- Nearly every brand uses the "NAD+ declines by 50% by age 50" framework
2. **Clinical study citation** -- Brands compete on who has the most rigorous clinical proof
3. **Purity/quality reassurance** -- Third-party testing, pharmaceutical-grade, percentage purity claims
4. **Specific numerical claims** -- "38% NAD+ increase," "40% increase," "12-year biological age reversal"
5. **Authority figures** -- Harvard, MIT, Nobel laureates, named doctors, David Sinclair association

### What Everyone Does:
- Lead with science/clinical proof
- Show product photography
- Offer subscription pricing
- Target 40-65 age demographic
- Reference NAD+ decline

### What Almost Nobody Does:
- Authentic UGC/testimonial video
- Emotional storytelling about lived experience
- Lifestyle/aspiration imagery (how life FEELS with more energy)
- Community-building through ads
- Humor or entertainment-forward creative
- Before/after storytelling (beyond superficial claims)
- Quiz funnels for personalization
- Creator/influencer partnerships visible in Meta ads

## Gaps & Opportunities

### 1. EMOTIONAL STORYTELLING GAP (Massive)
Every brand leads with science. Almost no brand shows what it actually FEELS like to have more energy, clearer thinking, better recovery. There's a huge opportunity for "day in the life" UGC showing real people experiencing tangible benefits.

### 2. UGC/CREATOR GAP
Despite UGC being 29-33% of top supplement ads on Meta, NMN brands are dramatically underindexing on this format. Most rely on product shots and clinical claims. A UGC-heavy creative strategy would be highly differentiated.

### 3. WOMEN-SPECIFIC MESSAGING GAP
Only Tru Niagen (with their Beauty launch) is pursuing women-specific NMN/NAD+ messaging. Omre has a blog post on "Best NMN for Women" but no visible Meta ads targeting this segment. Given that the primary supplement audience is 75-80% women aged 40-65, this is a massive miss.

### 4. BEAUTY/SKIN/APPEARANCE ANGLE GAP
Tru Niagen Beauty just launched but hasn't saturated Meta yet. No other NMN brand is running beauty-focused Meta ads despite NAD+'s connection to skin health and cellular repair.

### 5. MENTAL CLARITY / COGNITIVE ANGLE GAP
While Wonderfeel has website content on "NMN for a Sharper Mind," this angle is virtually absent from Meta ads in the NMN space. Cognitive enhancement is a powerful, under-exploited creative angle.

### 6. QUIZ FUNNEL GAP
Only 1.4% of all supplement brands use quiz funnels. Zero NMN brands appear to be using quiz-based Meta ads for personalized recommendations.

### 7. ENTERTAINMENT/MEME GAP
Meme-style content is just 1.4% of supplement ads. NMN brands use zero humor or entertainment-forward creative. This could be a pattern interrupt in a space dominated by clinical messaging.

### 8. SUBSCRIPTION VALUE STORYTELLING GAP
Brands offer subscriptions but don't tell subscription stories. No brand shows the journey of Month 1 vs Month 3 vs Month 6 on NMN.

### 9. COMPARISON/SWITCHING ANGLE GAP
Despite consumers being active comparison shoppers, no brand runs "why we're different from Brand X" style Meta ads (Omre does this on their blog but not in ads).

### 10. SLEEP / RECOVERY ANGLE GAP
NAD+ has connections to circadian rhythm and sleep quality. No NMN brand is running sleep-focused Meta ads despite sleep being one of the top wellness concerns.

## Hook Analysis

### Most Common Opening Lines/Headlines in NMN/NAD+ Ads

**Science/Authority Hooks (Most Common):**
- "In a double-blind, placebo-controlled clinical study..."
- "Harvard-developed..." / "Created by a Harvard MD..."
- "Nobel Prize-winning scientists discovered..."
- "Clinically proven to increase NAD+ by [X]%..."
- "The first NMN proven to..."

**Fear/Problem Hooks:**
- "By age 50, you've lost half your NAD+..."
- "NAD+ is an enzyme needed by all human cells... but it declines with age"
- "In a market flooded with fake and adulterated NMN..."
- "More than half of NMN products tested contained ZERO NMN"
- "After 40, your cells are running out of energy"

**Specificity/Claim Hooks:**
- "38% increase in NAD+... 12-year decrease in biological age"
- "40% NAD+ increase in 30 days"
- "900mg NMN daily -- the dose backed by the largest clinical trial"
- "4.5 million bottles sold"

**Authority/Social Proof Hooks:**
- "TIME magazine calls it 'the supplement experts take'"
- "80% of doctors reported taking an NAD supplement"
- "35 consecutive years BBB A+ rated"
- "8 Nobel laureates on our advisory board"

### What's MISSING from Hook Strategies:

**Curiosity-driven hooks** -- "What if one molecule could..." / "The enzyme your body stopped making at 30..."
**Story-driven hooks** -- "Three months ago, I couldn't get through the afternoon without..."
**Contrarian hooks** -- "Forget antioxidants. Scientists say aging is actually an energy crisis."
**Identity hooks** -- "For people who refuse to age like their parents did"
**Comparison hooks** -- "I tried 6 NMN supplements. Here's what actually worked."
**Question hooks** -- "Why do some 60-year-olds have the energy of a 40-year-old?"

## What's Likely Working

Based on ad longevity, number of variations, and continued investment:

### 1. ProHealth's "12-Year Biological Age Reversal" Claim
They have dedicated Facebook AND Google ad landing pages built around this single claim. The specificity (38% NAD+ increase = 12-year reversal) is extremely compelling and likely their highest-converting hook. **Signal: Multiple landing page variants and continued investment suggest strong performance.**

### 2. Cymbiotika's Long-Running Meta Campaigns (400+ Days)
Campaign longevity of 400+ days is in the top tier of ALL supplement ads on Meta. This indicates strong ROAS, effective creative rotation, and sustainable customer acquisition. **Signal: Duration is the strongest indicator of ad performance.**

### 3. Elysium's Social Proof Stack
"4.5 million bottles sold" + "TIME magazine recognition" + "8 Nobel laureates" creates a trust cascade that's hard for new entrants to match. **Signal: Continued use of these proof points suggests they test well.**

### 4. Subscription-First Pricing
Every major brand has moved to subscription-first pricing ($15-20/month savings). This model clearly works for LTV optimization. **Signal: Universal adoption = validated approach.**

### 5. Tru Niagen's Amazon Full-Funnel Strategy
Documented 7x daily sales during homepage takeover, 50% NTB order increase, 67% Subscribe & Save growth. **Signal: Case study with verified metrics.**

### 6. Single Image Ads (Industry-Wide)
268-day average duration -- highest of any format. Clean product images with overlaid claims continue to perform as the workhorse format. **Signal: Longevity data from 500-ad analysis.**

### 7. ProHealth's Counterfeit Fear Angle
"More than half of NMN products tested contained zero NMN" is a unique, fear-based differentiator that creates urgency and positions ProHealth as the safe choice. **Signal: Prominent placement on dedicated ad landing pages.**

## What's Likely NOT Working

### 1. Renue By Science / Alive By Science's Entire Approach
Multiple FDA warning letters, BBB actions, rebranding twice, and minimal visible Meta ad presence suggests their previous advertising tactics failed both regulatorily and commercially. **Signal: Brand name changes and enforcement actions.**

### 2. GenF20's Aggressive Direct-Response Tactics
Class-action investigation for false advertising, ingredient dosing far below clinical study levels, identical formulas sold under different brand names. Meta's health ad restrictions increasingly penalize this approach. **Signal: Legal action and regulatory scrutiny.**

### 3. Pure Science/Clinical Copy Without Emotional Connection
While science claims are the dominant angle, the Evolut Agency analysis found that UGC and storytelling formats have significantly higher campaign longevity. Brands relying ONLY on clinical claims without human connection likely see faster creative fatigue. **Signal: Short campaign durations for clinical-only creative.**

### 4. Tru Niagen's Previous Energy/Performance Claims
Permanently discontinued "increases energy" claims and Shannon Sharpe testimonial after NAD challenge. Direct energy/performance claims are a compliance risk on Meta. **Signal: Regulatory forced discontinuation.**

### 5. Elysium's Aggressive Age-Reversal Claims
NAD recommended they "make clear that any noticeable aging-related benefits of taking Basis has not been shown in humans." Broad anti-aging claims without human evidence are a regulatory liability. **Signal: Forced claim modification.**

### 6. Omre's Syndicated PR-Only Strategy
Heavy reliance on syndicated press releases across financial news sites (declaring themselves "#1 ranked") without corresponding Meta ad investment suggests limited DTC acquisition capability. **Signal: PR without paid media typically indicates weak paid performance.**

## Regulatory & Platform Considerations

### Meta Health & Wellness Ad Restrictions (2025-2026)

**Current restrictions impacting NMN/NAD+ brands:**
1. **Conversion optimization blocked** -- Health/wellness brands cannot optimize for "Purchase" or "Add to Cart" events. Must use "Landing Page Views" or "Engagement" instead.
2. **Custom/lookalike audience restrictions** -- Second wave of enforcement in early 2026 targeting custom conversions and audience building.
3. **Before/after imagery restricted** -- Ads suggesting dramatic physical results face increased scrutiny.
4. **Health claim sensitivity** -- Meta automatically classifies advertisers based on website content. Condition-specific pages trigger additional restrictions.
5. **Retargeting limitations** -- Custom audiences and retargeting capabilities reduced for health advertisers.

**Impact on NMN brands:**
- Brands making disease-specific claims (heart health, brain health, DNA repair) face higher classification risk
- Before/after biological age claims may trigger review
- Energy/performance claims must be carefully worded
- The shift to engagement-based optimization actually FAVORS educational, UGC, and storytelling content over direct-response clinical claims

### FDA/BBB Regulatory Landscape
- NMN confirmed as lawful dietary supplement (September 2025) -- removes previous regulatory uncertainty
- BBB/NAD increasingly scrutinizing supplement advertising claims
- Affiliate/influencer disclosure requirements tightening
- Direct disease-prevention claims remain off-limits

## Strategic Recommendations

### Creative Differentiation Opportunities

**1. Own the "How It Feels" Angle**
No NMN brand on Meta is effectively communicating the subjective, felt experience of taking NMN. UGC testimonials showing real people describing their experience (more afternoon energy, better sleep, clearer thinking, faster recovery) would be highly differentiated.

**2. Women-First Creative**
With 75-80% of the supplement audience being women 40-65, and almost NO NMN brand running women-specific creative, this is the single largest audience gap. Creative should address perimenopause energy crashes, skin health, hormonal aging, and women-specific longevity concerns.

**3. Stealth UGC / "Doesn't Look Like an Ad" Creative**
The highest-performing Meta creative in 2026 is content that takes "a second to realize it's sponsored." NMN brands are running obvious product ads. Lo-fi, authentic UGC that feels like a friend's recommendation would stop the scroll more effectively than another clinical infographic.

**4. Quiz Funnel as Lead Magnet**
With only 1.4% of supplement brands using quiz funnels and ZERO NMN brands doing so, a "What's Your Cellular Age?" or "Find Your NAD+ Plan" quiz would be a powerful cold-traffic engagement tool, especially given Meta's shift to engagement-based optimization for health brands.

**5. Comparison Content**
Position against the field with transparent comparison creative. "We tested 5 NMN supplements. Here's what we found." This addresses the comparison-shopper mindset and builds trust.

**6. Storytelling Arcs (Month 1/3/6)**
Show the NMN journey over time through serialized content. No brand is doing longitudinal storytelling about the supplement experience.

### Hook Framework Recommendation

Lead with hooks competitors are NOT using:

1. **Story hooks:** "Three months ago, I was crashing every afternoon at 2pm..."
2. **Curiosity hooks:** "There's a molecule your body makes less of every year after 30..."
3. **Identity hooks:** "For the woman who refuses to accept that 'tired' is just part of getting older"
4. **Contrarian hooks:** "Stop spending money on antioxidants. Scientists say aging is actually an energy crisis."
5. **Question hooks:** "What would you do with 20% more energy every single day?"
6. **Comparison hooks:** "I tried 4 different NAD+ supplements. Only one actually worked."
