## POST 14 (Thursday) — EDUCATION
**Single image:** Quick science fact about CoQ10

### Caption A
CoQ10. You've seen it everywhere. Here's what it actually does: it lives in your mitochondria and helps convert food into ATP — actual cellular energy. Your body makes it naturally, but production drops with age. It's also a fat-soluble antioxidant, meaning it protects the very membranes your cells depend on. Not glamorous. Just essential biochemistry.

### Caption B
Practitioner hat on. Let's talk CoQ10. Your heart, liver, and kidneys have the highest concentrations — because those organs burn the most energy. CoQ10 is part of the electron transport chain. Without it, your mitochondria can't finish the job of making ATP. Production declines after your 30s. It's one of the most well-researched supplements out there. Worth understanding.

### Caption C
Quick science fact because I think you deserve to know what you're putting in your body. CoQ10 works inside your mitochondria to help produce ATP — your cells' energy currency.
