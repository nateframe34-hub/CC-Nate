### Unit Economics

### Why This Works

**For the customer:** Under $50 impulse threshold. Compare-at $69.95 crossed out creates a permanent 28.6% savings perception without running a promo. $0.83/day is less than a coffee. 60-day supply guarantees they feel NMN working before running out (NMN takes 2-4 weeks to build NAD+ levels — competitors selling 30-day supplies lose customers who never feel results).

**For us:** 73.2% margin with $24.98 CPA ceiling gives real room to test and optimize on Meta (typical supplement CPAs run $20-40). Even if only 20-30% of buyers choose a bundle, blended AOV rises to ~$60-65 with no extra ad spend.

**Why all 3 tiers, not just the single:** The bundles don't compete with the Starter — they make it feel safe. When the Reluctant Decliner sees $49.95 next to $89.90 and $119.85, the internal question shifts from "is this worth $50?" to "which one do I pick?" Each avatar self-selects naturally:

- Reluctant Decliner / Burned Buyer → Starter (low risk, try it first)
- Proactive Optimizer / Weekend Warrior → Protocol or Transformation (committed, done their research)
- Exhausted Parent → could go either way, Subscribe & Save catches them at checkout regardless

## Offer #2: Subscribe & Save @ $42.95 Every 60 Days

**The retention engine. On-site upsell presented at checkout as a toggle/checkbox.**

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Price per shipment | $42.95 |
| Margin per shipment | $29.80 (69.4%) |
| Effective monthly cost | $21.48 |
| 5-cycle LTV | $214.75 |
| 5-cycle total margin | $149.00 |
| BE ROAS | 1.44 |

### Why This Works

**For the customer:** $21.48/month effective. The framing that creates the "I NEED this" feeling: *"Your daily coffee habit costs $150/month and gives you a crash. This costs $21/month and fixes the crash permanently."* That reframe works because our customer research showed "caffeine gives you a spike, this raises your baseline" is one of our strongest hooks. At $21.48/month, the comparison is undeniable.

**For us:** $29.80/shipment looks lower than the one-time $36.57, but the LTV math changes everything. A subscriber acquired at $25 CPA who stays 5 cycles generates $149 in margin from a $25 investment — 6x return. Auto-ship also eliminates "forgot to reorder" churn.

**The urgency mechanism:** "Save $7 every shipment" isn't the real hook — it's locking in the $49.95 launch price. Once we raise to $59.95 post-launch, existing subscribers at $42.95 are getting an even better deal. Message this as: *"Lock in launch pricing before it goes up."*

### Target Avatars

- **Proactive Optimizer** (30-45, tracks health metrics) → wants consistency for tracking results
- **Exhausted Parent** → wants "set it and forget it," doesn't have bandwidth to remember reorders

## Offer #3: $10 Off Transformation Bundle — 3x @ $109.85

**The warm traffic closer. For email sequences, retargeting, and exit intent.**

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Price | $109.85 |
| Margin | $80.17 (73.0%) |
| CPA ceiling (2x) | $54.93 |
| Perceived savings | $40 total ($30 bundle + $10 first order) |
| Effective $/bottle | $36.62 |
| Effective $/day | $0.61 |
| Supply | 180 days |

### Why This Over the Protocol Bundle or Standalone Transformation

**The Protocol (2x @ $89.90)** only saves $10 on a $90 purchase. That's 10%. Nobody gets excited about 10%. It doesn't make you feel like you'd be stupid to pass it up.

**The standalone Transformation ($119.85)** saves $30, which is better, but $120 is over the psychological threshold where people start justifying instead of impulse buying.

**$109.85 hits the sweet spot:**

- **$40 total perceived savings** — $30 bundle discount + $10 first-order discount. $40 is a number people feel in their gut. Law of Large Numbers: lead with the dollar amount, not the percentage.
- **Under $110** — just barely under the next psychological barrier. $109.85 feels fundamentally different from $119.85.
- **180-day supply** — reframes the purchase from "buying supplements" to "committing to a 6-month protocol." For The Reluctant Decliner, this matches the hook: *"You can feel the trajectory. This changes it."* A 6-month supply is the physical commitment to changing that trajectory.
- **$0.61/day** — cheapest per-day cost of any offer on the sheet. For someone already considering NMN, "$0.61/day for 6 months" vs "$0.83/day for 2 months" makes the bigger purchase feel like the smarter play.
- **Margin is nearly identical to the single bottle** (73.0% vs 73.2%) but AOV is 2.2x higher. Every conversion does double the work.

### Where to Use It

This is NOT the cold traffic offer. The Burned Supplement Buyer isn't spending $110 on day one. Use for:

- **Email welcome sequence** — after they've seen the landing page but didn't buy
- **Retargeting ads** — people who visited, added to cart, or browsed but didn't convert
- **Exit intent popup** — "$10 off if you grab the 6-month supply today"
- **Post-purchase upsell** — after a Starter purchase, offer the Transformation for their next order

The "$10 off your first order" feels like a personal, limited incentive stacked on already-good pricing. Creates a "this specific deal won't be here later" trigger.

## What We're NOT Running at Launch

| Offer | Why Not |
|-------|---------|
| B1G1 Free | 58.9% margin and 1.70 BE ROAS. Too thin for sustained spend. Reserve for launch week blitz to seed reviews, then kill it. |
| B2G1 Free | Strong offer but per Origins: BXGX offers are addictive. Reserve for 2-3 major events/year (Black Friday, anniversary). |
| % Off Sitewide | Trains customers to wait for sales. Never run this as a regular offer. |
| $10 Off Single Bottle ($39.95) | CPA ceiling of only $19.98 — below typical Meta supplement CPAs. Tests the $39.95 price point without having the ad budget room to actually scale it. |

Once we hit 50+ reviews and have proven CPAs, graduate from launch pricing:

| Metric | Launch | Post-Launch |
|--------|--------|-------------|
| Single bottle | $49.95 | $59.95 |
| 2-pack bundle | $89.90 | $109.90 |
| 3-pack bundle | $119.85 | $159.85 |
| Margin % (single) | 73.2% | 77.1% |
| CPA ceiling (single, 2x) | $24.98 | $29.98 |

Existing subscribers stay locked at launch pricing — this becomes an additional retention lever.
