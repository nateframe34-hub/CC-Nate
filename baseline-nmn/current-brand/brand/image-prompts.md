## Usage Notes

**Aspect ratios:**
- 4:5 — Standard for Shopify product images and Instagram feed
- 16:9 — Infographics, banner placements, desktop hero backgrounds
- 1:1 — Social media, square crops, email images

**Generation tips:**
- Run each prompt 3-4 times and pick the best output — AI generation has variance
- **Critical for the product bottle:** AI models cannot reliably render specific label text. Generate the scene/lighting/composition, then composite your real bottle photo or real label artwork in post-production. This is the single most important post-production step.
- For infographics (5A, 5B): Generate the visual style reference, then build the actual charts and diagrams manually in Figma or Illustrator for data accuracy
- Lifestyle portraits may need face refinement — upscale with an AI enhancer if needed
- The capsule shots should be the easiest to generate accurately since white capsules are simple geometry

**Brand color reference (Baseline identity):**
- Deep Teal: #0C3B3F (headers, nav, primary buttons)
- Teal Mid: #165C5A (section backgrounds)
- Teal Light: #1E7A77 (links, hover states)
- Copper: #C07840 (CTAs, accents, highlights)
- Cream: #F5F0E8 (page background)
- Near Black: #1A1A1A (body text)
- Product label accent: Purple/violet (as shown on actual bottle)

**Note on brand vs. product colors:** The Baseline brand identity uses teal + copper. The actual product label uses purple/violet + gold. Both are correct — the brand and the product label are intentionally different. Website and ads use the teal/copper brand palette. Product shots show the actual purple/gold label. Don't try to color-correct the bottle to match the website — they're designed to complement each other.
