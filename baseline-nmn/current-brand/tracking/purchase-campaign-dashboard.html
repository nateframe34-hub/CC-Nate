Decision Rules — What the Numbers Mean

  • 1
    CBO spend allocation is your primary signal. If one ad set has 60%+ of total spend by day 5-7, that's Meta telling you which sub-avatar converts most efficiently. Trust it.
  • 2
    CTR below 1% after 500+ impressions = rework. The creative isn't stopping the scroll. Try a different image or hook before killing the angle entirely.
  • 3
    ALL creatives below 1% CTR = creative problem, not targeting. Re-examine the images and opening lines. The algorithm can't sell what people don't stop to look at.
  • 4
    Zero add-to-carts after 7 days across all ads = offer or landing page issue. People are clicking but not converting. Check the product page, price, or trust signals.
  • 5
    Ignore CPA until day 10+ with at least 5 purchases. Before that, CPA is noise. Focus on engagement signals and spend allocation.
  • 6
    Read the comments. "This is me" and friend tags = strong resonance. Questions about the product = purchase intent. Angry reactions = misaligned targeting or creative.
  • 7
    Currency reminder: Meta reports spend in CAD. Revenue is USD. A $40 CAD CPA = ~$29 USD CPA. Your true ROAS is ~35% better than Meta shows.
