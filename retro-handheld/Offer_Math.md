# PocketArcade: Unit Economics & Offer Math

**Written 2026-10-16** from founder-supplied costs. 128GB version only (most games).
Assumes free shipping to the customer and payment fees of ~2.9% + $0.30. Returns, chargebacks and app costs are NOT included, so the real breakeven is slightly higher.

## Breakeven ROAS by offer

Breakeven ROAS = price ÷ (price − COGS − fees).

| Offer | Price | COGS | Fees | Margin | **Breakeven ROAS** |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 handheld | $75.99 | $28.23 | $2.50 | $45.26 | **1.68×** |
| 1 handheld | $79.99 | $28.23 | $2.62 | $49.14 | **1.63×** |
| 1 handheld + free case | $75.99 | $33.83 | $2.50 | $39.66 | **1.92×** |
| 2-pack | $129.99 | $53.25 | $4.07 | $72.67 | **1.79×** |

(Case costs are worst case, shipped separately. Shipping in the same package will lower them.)

## What this tells us

1. **COGS is ~37% of price. That is above our ≤30% checklist rule.** Breakeven is ~1.7×, close to the register fan's 1.76×. The difference: this product has proven demand, a gift season, and last year's data. Last year at ~$75 it reached about breakeven. **Same price and same offer means about the same result.** The offer has to lift profit per order.
2. **Profit per order comes from AOV.** The case is the cheapest lever: worth $14.99 to the customer, it costs us $5.60 or less. Each order that adds a case at $14.99 adds about $8.66 of margin.
3. **Free cases on the single cost too much.** It pushes breakeven to 1.92×. Save it for BFCM, when conversion rates are highest.
4. **The 2-pack is the hero offer for gifts** ("one for each kid", "one for you and one for him"). At $139.99 it brings in ~$82 per order vs ~$45 for a single.

## Recommended launch offer

| Tier | Price | What's included | Breakeven |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1 PocketArcade** | $79.99 (anchor $99.99) | Handheld | 1.63× |
| **2-pack ⭐ most popular** | $139.99 (anchor $199.98) | 2 handhelds + **free case** | 1.82× |
| **Cart add-on** | $14.99 | Silicone case (and joystick covers once costed) | — |

- Test $79.99 against $75.99 in week 1. Gift buyers check price less than self-buyers, and $4 is pure margin.
- **BFCM (Nov 27 – Dec 1):** free case on the single as well. Accept ~1.9× breakeven for the volume.
- Every free item must answer an objection. The case answers "will it get scratched in a backpack or stocking" and "is it a proper gift".
