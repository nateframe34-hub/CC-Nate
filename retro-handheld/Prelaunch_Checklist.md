# PocketArcade: Pre-Launch Checklist (2026-10-16)

## 🔴 Could sink the season
- [ ] **US duties / tariffs.** The US ended duty-free de minimis for low-value parcels in 2025. Confirm the $7.83 shipping is **DDP (duties paid)**. If not, customers get a duty bill at the door, or margins drop.
- [ ] **Shipping time + origin → Christmas cutoff date.** Late gifts mean refunds, chargebacks and processor flags.
- [ ] **Stock depth + reorder lead time.** A winner in late November can sell out. How many units can the supplier hold for us, and how fast can they restock?
- [ ] **Payment processor holds.** A new store with a sudden spike in sales often gets payouts held or a reserve put on it. Have a backup processor ready (PayPal + Shopify Payments), and cash to cover ad spend while payouts are held.
- [ ] **Ad account health.** We lost a BM to a bot flag before. Check daily spend limits, have a backup ad account, and warm it up before scaling.
- [ ] **Ad policy and preloaded games.** Never show recognisable games or name them in ads, the PDP or on the box shots. Keep the "thousands of games" claim generic.

## 🟡 Store
- [ ] **Remove the placeholder reviews from the reused PDP.** Fake reviews are an FTC risk. Use only real reviews (last year's customers, if they can be imported honestly), or none.
- [ ] Pixel + Conversions API. Place a test purchase and check that the value includes shipping and the case upsell.
- [ ] Cart progress bar: "Add a case for free shipping".
- [ ] Arrives-by date and order-by date on the PDP.
- [ ] Gift note / gift receipt option.
- [ ] Post-purchase upsell: "Add a 2nd one for $X" (one click, no checkout).
- [ ] Mobile speed and checkout test on a real phone.

## 🟡 Product & support
- [ ] **Order samples and test them:** battery, buttons, screen, and the **SD card**. Stock SD cards are this device's known weak point and fail often. Consider a better card, or a "spare card" add-on.
- [ ] What's in the box: is there a charging cable? A wall plug? State it plainly.
- [ ] **Quick-start card** (how to start a game, save, and exit). Fewer "it's broken" returns.
- [ ] Support inbox, FAQ, reply templates. Plan for December's "where is my order" volume.
- [ ] Order-tracking page.

## 🟢 Marketing
- [ ] Film b-roll with a sample (needed for all Batch 1 iterations).
- [ ] Original winning video file + last year's metrics.
- [ ] Comment-reply plan live from day 1 (see `ads/batch-1/B1_Winner_Iterations.md`).
- [ ] Email/SMS: abandoned cart + a BFCM list (pop-up offer for gift reminders).
- [ ] **Submit BFCM creative early.** Meta review slows down that week.
- [ ] Budget and cash-flow plan, since Q4 CPMs rise from mid-November.
