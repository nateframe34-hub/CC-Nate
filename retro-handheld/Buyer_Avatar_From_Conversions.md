Built with the Origins avatar method, using **only what the 6 sales show**. It checks the gift-first map in `SubAvatars_and_Angles.md` (v3) against real buyers. Anything not shown by the data is marked **(inference)**.

## Product & sophistication gate
- **What it does for her:** solves "what do I get them?" with one box that is ready to play.
- **How:** thousands of classic games already loaded. No setup, no downloads, no accounts.
- **Stage:** 3–4 (every R36S store says "thousands of games"). **Response: New Identity.** She becomes the one who found *the* gift. AD 7 already does this: "let me share my find."

## Core avatar
**"I need to get Christmas gifts sorted, and I want ones they'll actually love."**
**Why:** It's what AD 7 says in its first line, and it's the only message that has sold.

## Sub-avatars (ranked by what the orders show)

### SA-A · The List-Finisher Buying in Multiples ⭐ STRONGEST (4 of 6 orders)
- **Desire:** cross several people off the list with one purchase.
- **Experience:** she has 2–3 people to buy for (kids, or a partner and a kid). Gift-shopping in November.
- **Emotion:** relief at being done (primary: joy). The pressure under it is fear of the deadline and of getting it wrong.
- **Behavior:** buys the bundle and picks a different color for each person.
- **Demographic:** female buyer (5/6). Used only as a creative cue (a woman's voice and hands), **never in targeting**.
- **Verbatim language:** "If you're still looking for that Christmas gift" · "let me share my find" · "a hit with kids, a total nostalgia trip for adults" · "three different colors"
- **Evidence:** order mix (strong), 2-color duo (medium), who the gifts are for (inference).

**Angle 1: One box, everyone on your list is done**
Reason to buy: one order covers 2–3 people, and each gets their own color.
Hooks:
1. "Still need gifts for the kids? This covers all of them."
2. "One order. Three gifts. Done."
3. "I bought one for each of my kids and one for my husband."

**Angle 2: Kids love it, adults steal it**
Reason to buy: one gift that works for any age on her list.
Hooks:
1. "A hit with the kids. A nostalgia trip for the adults."
2. "I bought it for my son. My husband hasn't given it back."
3. "The only gift that works for a 9-year-old and a 39-year-old."

**Angle 3: Free cases, ready to wrap**
Reason to buy: the bundle comes gift-ready, with no extras to buy.
Hooks:
1. "2 for $139.99 and the cases are free."
2. "Wrapped, cased, done."
3. "Every kid gets their own color and their own case."

### SA-B · The Partner Who Found *His* Gift (2 single orders + comp review "got this for my girlfriend")
- **Desire:** a gift *he* will get excited about.
- **Experience:** he's hard to buy for; he grew up gaming; she may not game. (from the v3 map, **not yet confirmed by our orders**)
- **Emotion:** pride in finding it (joy).
- **Behavior:** buys a single, sometimes adds a case.
- **Evidence:** weak (2 single orders, recipient unknown).

**Angle: The gift he didn't put down**
Hooks: "He's impossible to buy for. Not this year." · "Every game he played as a kid, in one gift." · "My husband hasn't put it down since he opened it."

## Testing priority
1. **SA-A, Angle 1** with AD 7's body (the same footage and voice): it matches 4 of 6 real orders and pushes the bundle (higher profit).
2. **SA-A, Angle 2** next. You **don't** need a separate ad per angle: one ad can open with Angle 1's hook and carry Angle 2 in the voiceover, as AD 7 already does.
3. SA-B only after we know who the singles were for.

## What this changes
- **Ads:** woman's voice and hands, Christmas set, "still looking for a gift" energy. Not gamer call-outs.
- **PDP hero:** lead with the gift ("Christmas gifts for everyone on your list, sorted"); move "Replay your childhood" lower. Add "Order by Dec 11 for Christmas."
- **Bundle cards:** "One for each kid", "One for him, one for you".
