# Feedback Loop: Test AD 8 "Gamers, RUN don't WALK to Target" (killed 2026-11-08)

**Ad:** last year's winner (AD1 / T07), re-run unchanged. 11.8 s, music only, no voiceover. Caption: "Gamers, RUN don't WALK to Target for this! 🎁". Target storefront at night → walk in → box on the shelf → teal unit unboxed → film peel → game menu ("15,833 games") → gameplay.
**Campaign:** Testing CBO, $50/day, 10 ads in one ad set. Julie Brennan page.
**Status:** **Killed** at ~$147 spend with 0 purchases (our ~$150 no-sale line, ~3× the $49 target cost per sale).

## Verdict
**Great soft metrics, no conversion.** The ad wins attention and loses the sale.

## Why: the main learnings
1. **The hook works.** A store-run opening that doesn't look like an ad + a "Gamers, RUN don't WALK" call-out gave a 45–50% hook rate and the cheapest CPM in the account. Meta rewards attention with cheap delivery. **Keep this hook and reuse it.**
2. **No body: nothing sells after the hook.** Music only, no voiceover, no reasons to buy. After the store intro it shows the product but never says *why* it's worth $75 or *who* it's for. Viewers get curious, click, look, and leave.
3. **No CTA or offer in the ad.** Nothing tells the viewer what to do or why now (no Early Black Friday, no "free cases on 2+", no "order by Dec 11"). The click is curiosity, not intent.
4. **The audience it pulls is curious, not buying.** "Gamers" + a cool reveal pulls the people who enjoy watching the gadget (and correcting the Target claim), not the person shopping for a gift. Clicks were cheap *because* they were low-intent.
5. **The CBO amplifies cheap attention.** With almost no purchase data on a new pixel, Meta optimized for what it could see (cheap views and clicks) and gave this ad most of the budget, starving the ad that actually sold (AD 7). **Watch spend share against purchases, not CPM, in the first week.**

## Supporting evidence: AD 7 (T06) is the mirror image
Weak hook (33%), expensive CPM ($140+), but a **body that sells**: a warm female voiceover ("If you're still looking for that Christmas gift, let me share my find…"), the gift framing, "a hit with kids, a nostalgia trip for adults", a Christmas set and an unboxing → **the only full funnel, 1 duo sale on ~$51**.

## Rules going forward
- **Hook ≠ ad.** Judge hooks on hook rate (≥45%) and CPM; judge the ad on purchases. A strong hook with no body is a top-of-funnel asset, not a converter.
- **Every ad needs three parts:** hook (stop) → body (why it's worth it, who it's for, in a voice) → CTA/offer (what to do, why now).
- **Kill rule held up:** spend past ~3× target cost per sale with zero purchases = off, even with great soft metrics.
- **Don't let one cheap-attention ad eat the CBO** in week 1. If an ad takes >50% of spend for 3+ days with no sale, cut it or move it out.
