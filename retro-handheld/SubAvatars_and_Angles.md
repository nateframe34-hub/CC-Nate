# PocketArcade: Sub-Avatars & Angles (v3) · GIFT-FIRST

**Rebuilt 2026-10-15 (founder correction).** The research pack is written for the **self-buyer** (the gamer fed up with modern gaming, the nostalgic adult). That's the year-round market. **In Q4 we're mainly selling a gift.** The person who sees the ad and pays is usually **not** the person who plays.

So this version is built around **the giver**. The research isn't thrown away: it becomes **ammunition the giver uses to feel sure** ("he'll love it because…"). The self-buyer map is kept for after Christmas: `SubAvatars_and_Angles_v2_selfbuyer.md`.

> **Standing creative rule:** no Nintendo, Game Boy, Pokémon or real game titles or screenshots in ads.

---

## The core shift

| | Self-buyer (research pack) | **Gift-giver (Q4)** |
|---|---|---|
| Who sees the ad and pays | The gamer | **A partner, parent, grandparent, sibling, friend** |
| Their desire | Fun without ads and paywalls; to feel young again | **To give a gift that gets a real reaction**, and to not get it wrong |
| Their emotion | Anger at modern gaming → relief | **Gift anxiety → "I nailed it"** |
| What they need to believe | Modern gaming is broken… | **He'll love it · it's easy · it'll arrive in time · it's not junk** |
| Awareness | Problem → solution aware | **Solution-aware: they already know they need *a* gift.** Direct ads work |

## Core avatar

**"I want to give a gift that makes their face light up, and I don't want to get it wrong."**

The research gives us *why* the recipient will love it (nostalgia, no ads, real buttons, the classics). The giver's job is just to believe that.

## Sub-avatars (by who's giving to whom)

### SA1 · The Partner Buying For Him ⭐ biggest Q4 segment (validated: the "clueless wife")
| | |
|---|---|
| **Desire** | A gift he'll actually be excited about, not another gift card or sweater |
| **Experience** | Husband/boyfriend grew up gaming. She (often) isn't a gamer. Doesn't know what to buy or what's "good" |
| **Emotion** | Gift anxiety, "he's impossible to buy for," wanting to see a real reaction |
| **Behaviour** | Searches "gifts for him", "gifts for gamers"; asks friends/Reddit; buys Nov to early Dec |
| **Fears** | Buying the wrong tech · it needs setup she can't do · it's a cheap gadget · it won't arrive in time |

- **A1 · The gift he'll actually use:** *"He's impossible to buy for. This is the one he didn't put down all of Christmas."*
- **A2 · It takes him back to being 10:** the research's nostalgia, told from her side (*"he lit up like a kid"*)
- **A3 · You don't need to know anything about games:** thousands of games already on it, turn it on and play. Nothing to set up, nothing else to buy
- **A4 · He's done with phone games that nag for money:** the research's anti-mobile anger as *his* reason, said by her (*"no ads, no buying coins, just the games he grew up on"*)

### SA2 · The Parent Buying For Their Kid ⭐ validated by customer quotes
| | |
|---|---|
| **Desire** | A gift the kid loves that the parent also feels good about |

| **Emotion** | Wanting to be the "cool parent" + relief at no surprise charges |

- **A3 · Built for car rides and flights:** no Wi-Fi, no handing over your phone (founder angle)
- **A4 · One for each kid** (and one for you) → 2-pack / 3-pack

## Necessary Beliefs: the GIVER's ladder

The research's ladder (modern gaming is broken…) is the *recipient's* reason. The giver needs a shorter, different one:

| # | The giver must believe | Installed by |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **They'll love it.** It's the games they grew up on / a gift kids get excited about | Reaction footage, nostalgia cues, "he didn't put it down" |
| 2 | **I can't get it wrong.** No setup, games included, nothing else to buy, works out of the box | Unboxing in 10 seconds, "turn it on and play" |
| 3 | **It's not a cheap gadget.** It's solid and will last past January | Close-up hands-on b-roll, the case, reviews, warranty |
| 4 | **It will arrive before Christmas** | Shipping cutoff on ads and PDP from late November |
| 5 | **It's good value for my gift budget.** $75.99 for a gift that feels like more | Price anchor vs a new console or game; bundles |
| 6 | **This store is honest and safe** | Real reviews, clear shipping, easy returns, no fake 90%-off |
| 7 *(parents)* | **There's nothing bad on it:** no ads, no internet, no purchases | "Offline. Nothing to buy. Nothing to worry about." |

## What this means for the PDP and offer

- **Above the fold:** "The gift they'll actually use" + **arrives-by date** + the free case on the 2-pack.
- **Gift wrap / gift note** option (cheap, high perceived value for givers).
- **"What's in the box"** shown plainly: removes "will I need to buy anything else?"
- **Gift receipt and an easy return** line: givers fear the recipient won't like it.
- **Reviews from givers**, not just players (*"bought it for my husband, he hasn't put it down"*).
