# PocketArcade Batch 1: Iterations of Last Year's Winner

**Control (last year's winner):** hook *"Gamers, walk don't run to Target for this"* + product b-roll.
**Written 2026-10-16 from the hook alone. Re-check every iteration once we have the original video file** (voiceover or text, length, b-roll order, end card).

**Rule:** change ONE variable per iteration, so we know what moved. Keep the b-roll the same unless that is the variable being tested.
**Standing rules:** no Nintendo, Game Boy or real game names or screenshots. 5th-grade reading level. No em dashes.

---

## Why it worked (best read, before seeing the video)

1. **Call-out:** "Gamers" picks the viewer in one word.
2. **Urgency with humour:** "walk don't run" is a familiar phrase turned around. It reads like a friend's tip, not an ad.
3. **A real store name:** "Target" makes it feel like a find you can actually get, and suggests it's a mainstream product, not junk.
4. **Product b-roll:** the product is on screen from second 1, so clicks are pre-qualified (the opposite of the register fan's curiosity clicks).

What we keep in every iteration: the **"[Who], walk don't run…"** structure + the product on screen from frame 1.

## Launch plan

- **Week 1 (Oct 20+):** Round 1 (B1C1–C5) + B1C13 static, all in one CBO. They're cheap to make: one b-roll edit with five openings.
- **Scale rule:** ≥$150 spend and among the top spenders before scaling. No ad-level pauses before day 7. Cut ads at ~$25–40 of spend with no add-to-carts.
- **Week 2:** take the best call-out into Round 2 (visuals). Then Round 3 endings on the winner.
- Round 3 C11 (Christmas cutoff) waits until after Nov 20.

## Production notes

- On-screen hook text: large, top third, white with a black outline, on screen for the first 3 seconds.
- Use the same voice for every round so the hook is the only thing that changes.
- Length: match the original. If unknown, aim for 15–20 seconds.
- Real props and real branding stay in frame (gift wrap, a Target bag if used).
