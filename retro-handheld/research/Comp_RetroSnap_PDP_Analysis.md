## Their angle
**Core angle: "Replay your childhood": nostalgia for the games you grew up with, sold to the player.**
The page talks to "you" as the person who will play. Gift language ("perfect retro gift", Christmas hero) is the **wrapper**, not the core. Every specific line (reviews, feature block, FAQ) is about *you* reliving *your* childhood.

**Supporting angles, in order of weight:**
1. **Deal / Christmas sale:** "up to 70% off", buy more save more. The offer is the second headline.
2. **Social proof at scale:** 20,000+ customers, 70,000+ customers, 4.8 stars. Answers "is this a scam?"
3. **Play together:** duo as couples or partners ("we play together after work").
4. **Scarcity:** sold-out delivery windows (pressure to buy now).

**Awareness level:** product-aware. The page assumes traffic already saw the product in a TikTok or UGC video ("Creations!", "Become a Partner"). It skips the problem and goes straight to offer, proof and specs.
**Sophistication:** Stage 3–4. It competes on **offer + proof + scarcity**, not on a unique mechanism. Every R36S store makes the same "thousands of games" claim.

## What it means for us
1. **We are gift-giver first; they were player first.** Both are valid in Q4. (Correction, 2026-11-04: our leader "Test AD 8" is the **"Gamers, RUN don't WALK to Target"** ad, a gamer call-out, i.e. their SA1 player, not a giver ad.)
2. **Test a "Replay your childhood" PDP line** for the player SA: e.g. the hero subline or the first band, so traffic from self-buyer ads (T01, T02, T04, T08) lands on matching words. Keep the gift framing for giver ads (T03, T06, T09, T10).
3. **Use the couple angle for the duo:** "One for you, one for them. Play together." (Our duo copy already says "Play together".)
4. **Reviews from the player's voice:** when real reviews come in, put nostalgia ones ("took me right back to being a kid") next to giver ones.
5. **Don't copy:** the $224 fake anchor, the 70% off claims or the fake sold-out dates. Our honest edges: free shipping on everything, free cases on bundles, 128GB, a lower case price.
