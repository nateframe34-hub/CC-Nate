## 🚩 Key Challenges & Pain Points
### Pain Point 1: Modern Gaming Feels Predatory & Manipulative
Games are designed to extract money, not provide fun
“Free” games are loaded with ads, paywalls, and psychological tricks
Feels tricked, scammed, or drained after playing
### Pain Point 2: Gaming No Longer Feels Relaxing
Games feel like chores, not escapes
Daily logins, grind mechanics, and FOMO cause stress
Phone gaming brings notifications, distractions, battery anxiety
### Pain Point 3: Loss of Authentic Fun
Misses simplicity and fairness of older games
Frustrated that modern games feel hollow or soulless
Doesn’t trust modern platforms to preserve progress or ownership
## 🌟 Goals & Aspirations
### Short-Term Goals

### Long-Term Aspirations
