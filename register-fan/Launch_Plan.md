## 2. The auction — what to actually expect ⚠️ corrected

**Meta's auction is not segmented by product category.** "No register fan brands on Meta" does not, by itself, make our auction cheaper — we bid against every advertiser competing for the same person's attention, not against category peers.

**Two real reasons to expect better delivery than tallow anyway:**

1. **Audience vertical.** Skincare/beauty is among the most heavily-bid verticals on the platform. Home and home-improvement historically is not. Tallow was paying beauty-auction prices.
2. **Post-Andromeda the creative sorts the audience.** A hot-or-cold-bedroom ad classes us into the home pool rather than the beauty pool. This is the actual mechanism — not the absence of competitors.

**So: expect better than tallow's $3.17 CPC. Do not assume it. No CPC target is set** — we have no data, and an invented threshold makes a guess look like a rule.

### 🕐 The Q4 clock — the strongest argument for moving now

**Meta CPMs ramp from mid-October through December** as retail and holiday budgets enter the auction. Today is 18 September.

- $600 cap ÷ $50/day = **12 days**. Starting now finishes ~30 September, **before the ramp**.
- Starting in three weeks means paying holiday prices to learn what could have been learned at September prices.
- Seasonality also runs *with* us right now: heating season is arriving, and the desire renews the first genuinely cold night.

**This is a real deadline, not urgency theatre. Every day of prep costs auction quality.**

## 3. First angle — SA1 Angle 1, COLD-FRAMED

> ### Your furnace isn't broken. The warm air just isn't reaching that room.

**Sub-avatar:** SA1 The Room at the End of the Run
**Angle:** Angle 1 — *it isn't you, and it isn't broken*
**Awareness target:** Unaware → Problem Aware
**Sophistication response:** New Information + New Identity (avatar innovation)

**Why this one over SA2's sharper line:**

1. **It is the most Unaware-friendly thing we have.** Part 4's operating rule is to land Unaware or Problem Aware; this angle requires the reader to know nothing except that one room is wrong.
2. **It carries the permission shift**, which is the strategic core. The entire avatar-innovation play is speaking to the person who quietly believes the problem is their fault — and nobody else is speaking to them. Sourced verbatim: *"I'm on my 5th house/apartment where this has been the case. I feel like it has to be something I'm doing."*
3. **Strongest evidence** of any angle in the roster.

**It must be cold-framed.** Every quote we hold is about heat and it is heating season. The argument is identical; the scene is not. Hot-side copy goes back in the drawer until spring.

**Cold-side hook variants:**
- *Your furnace isn't broken. The warm air just isn't reaching that room.*
- *One room colder than the rest of the house? It isn't you.*
- *Put your hand on that vent. Air is coming out — it's just barely moving.*

**SA2 Angle 1** (*"Fine at bedtime. Wrong by 2am"* → cold version: *"You're running a space heater in a heated house"*) is batch 2, after a format wins.
