## B4C2: Simplified Us vs Them

**Why this template:** two images, a few words, no argument. The swipe "leverages something common that everyone understands." Everyone knows what a space heater is. Putting it next to the vent and naming what each one *is* does the whole reframe.
**Copy the swipe's parts:** plain white background · two objects side by side, same size · one short line under each · a small line beneath both · a small wordmark at the bottom.

### In-image text
> *(left, under the heater)* This is a second heater.
> *(right, under the vent)* This is the heat you already pay for.
> *(small, centred)* Getting your furnace's heat into the room it never reached.
> *(wordmark)* evenroom

### Primary text

That room has been cold every winter. So every October, the space heater comes out.

It works. But your furnace is already heating that room. You pay for that heat every month. It just isn't getting there.

That room is at the end of the duct run. The warm air reaches the rooms close to the furnace first. By the time it gets to the last room, it's a trickle. Put your hand on the vent in there while the heat's on. You'll feel it.

This replaces the vent cover in that room. Two small fans pull the warm air your furnace is already making the rest of the way in. It has its own thermostat, so you set the temperature for that room and it holds it.

No more plugging something in every night. No more waiting for the room to warm up before bed.

Two screws and a plug. Fits 4x10 and 6x10 floor vents.

$89.99 for our Fall Sale, with 60 days to try it.

## B4C3: Minimal Us vs Them · ⚠️ the A6 test

**Why this template:** the swipe reverses the usual us-vs-them. It only lists the **other** thing's pain points, then answers with "None of that." That's the cleanest way to test the cost and hassle framing (A6) in one ad.
**Copy the swipe's parts:** solid coloured background · a thin vertical divider · "Them" on the left with a familiar product and white callout bubbles · our product on the right with one bubble: **None of that.** · small wordmark bottom right.

### In-image text
> **Space heater** · bubbles: *Plug it in every night* · *A second bill* · *Only warms where it points* · *Another thing to remember*
> **Evenroom** · bubble: **None of that.**

A space heater does the job. It also comes with a routine.

So you're heating one room twice.

This replaces the vent cover in that room. Two small fans pull the warm air your furnace is already making into the room. Its own thermostat holds the temperature you set. It turns itself on and off.

Nothing to plug in at night. Nothing to remember.

Two screws and a plug. $89.99 for our Fall Sale, with 60 days to try it.
