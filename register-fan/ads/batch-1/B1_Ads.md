**Rebuilt 2026-09-26** against `Brand_Mastery.md` Part 6 (Origins-correct avatars and angles).

## The sub-avatar

| | |
|---|---|
| **Core desire** | This one room the same temperature as the rest of the house |
| **Behaviour** | Runs a box fan, tower fan or ceiling fan in that room — often permanently |
| **Emotion** | Resignation. They've concluded nothing works on this room |
| **THE GAP** | **A fan moves air. It cannot change the room's temperature.** They're treating a delivery problem with a circulation tool |

**Awareness:** Solution Aware ⭐ our prime segment — they've tried something and it failed.

## The two angles in this batch

| | Angle *(the reason to buy)* |
|---|---|
| **A1** ⭐ | **Warms the room instead of just moving the air around it** |
| **A2** | **Gives that room its own temperature, so you stop managing it by hand** |

---

## B1C1 — Native long-form · Julie Brennan page

**Method 5, Identity/Relief · carries A1 → A2 · TOF · right column excluded**

**Earns the read:** first line is the reader's own history — the fan that didn't work.

**Beats:** the room · the fan bought for it, and the second one · why it never changed anything *(A1 — a fan moves the air already in the room, and that air is cold)* · the duct run explained · putting something in the vent instead · the room holding a temperature without being managed *(A2)* · honest, partial outcome.

**Image:** authorless POV, a box fan sitting in the corner of an ordinary bedroom, unplugged, late afternoon winter light. Real phone-camera quality, mild grain. No product, no text.

---

## B1C2 — Static · **Angle A1**

**Method 2, Indirect Implication · TOF · all placements**
**Earns the read:** tells them something they did was pointless. Unscrollable if you did it.

> **ZONE 1** — *earn the read*
> ## You already tried a fan in there.
>
> **ZONE 2** — *mechanism*
> A fan moves the air that's already in the room. In winter that air is cold — moving it around makes it worse, not better. **This isn't a fan for the room. It's a fan for the vent.**
>
> **ZONE 3** — *channel*
> It pulls in the warm air your furnace already made.
> *$89, before it's properly cold.*

**Why it should work:** the only ad where the read, the mechanism and the desire are one idea — their failed fan is hook, explanation and proof at once. Method 2 is the swipe file's highest-rated channel because the reader reaches *"so that's why it didn't work"* themselves, and a conclusion you reach yourself is one you defend.

**Design:** 4:5. Left two-thirds, a real photo of a plain white wall register in warm domestic light. Right third, terracotta panel, three zones stacked, "vent" emphasised. Price inline at body weight — no band, no badge.

---

## B1C3 — Static · **Angle A2**

**Method 1, Direct Naming · TOF · all placements**
**Earns the read:** names a nightly behaviour they don't think of as a behaviour.

> **ZONE 1**
> ## You turn that fan on and off all day.
>
> **ZONE 2**
> The room isn't cold because it needs more air moved. It's last on the duct run — it gets whatever's left after every other room takes its share.
>
> **ZONE 3**
> Set a temperature for that room and leave it.
> *$89, before it's properly cold.*

**Design:** 4:5, warm off-white. Zone 1 large Archivo bold. Zone 2 small Inter with a minimal four-vent arrow motif thinning left to right. Zone 3 in terracotta. Small wordmark.
