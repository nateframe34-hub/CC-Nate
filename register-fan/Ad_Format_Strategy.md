# Ad Format Strategy — Register Booster Fan

**Created:** 2026-08-12
**Premise (founder call):** native camouflage is currently the most-run static style on Meta. We can run it, but we cannot rely on it alone.

---

## 1. What is actually being taxed

Meta does not price by style — there is no native surcharge in the auction. The path is downstream of the user: **a format someone has seen a thousand times stops fewer of them → engagement rate falls → quality/engagement ranking falls → you pay more for the same delivery.**

The distinction matters because it names the lever. **What gets taxed is familiarity, not the native format.** A native that looks like every other native pays it. A native that looks like something the user has not seen this week does not. So the answer is not "run fewer natives" — it is "never let the whole account sit in one visual register."

### The second argument, which is not about CPM at all

**Facebook's right column cannot render primary text.** A native's entire argument lives in the primary text, so in that placement it delivers an image with no case attached — the delivery fault we already paid to find on tallow.

Any format whose message is carried **in the image** works in every placement. That is a coverage problem natives cannot solve at any budget, and it is an independent reason to hold a mixed portfolio.

---

## 2. What this product makes possible that tallow did not

**The mechanism is physically demonstrable.** This is the single biggest creative asset the product has and it should drive the format mix.

> **The tallow precedent, learned expensively.** B32C3 put an *optical* claim on a whiteboard and died at $36.53 with 0 purchases. There was no physical process to draw, so the board carried no information and became decoration.

Here there is a process, and it is visible, measurable and legible at a glance:

- Two thermometers in two rooms, several degrees apart
- A hand held to a vent where air is barely moving
- A cross-section of a duct run losing pressure at every branch
- A number on a thermostat display changing

Tallow could not run demonstration formats. We can. And the incumbent is not — its ads are weak and product-shot-led, which leaves the entire demonstrative register open.

---

## 3. The format portfolio

Each format gets a **role**. A portfolio is not variety for its own sake; each one is there to do a job the others can't.

| # | Format | Role | Placement coverage | Cost to make |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | **Two-thermometer demonstration** (static) | Proof. The mechanism in one frame, no reading required | **All** — message is in the image | Low. No product needed |
| **B** | **Duct-run diagram** (static) | New Information — the Stage 2 requirement, delivered at the lowest comprehension cost available | **All** | Low |
| **C** | **Forum-post screenshot** | Attribution. The mechanism stated by someone with nothing to sell | Partial — some text in image | Low |
| **D** | **Native long-form** | Angle discovery. Only format that can carry a full argument | Feed only | Low |
| **E** | **Install POV video** | Kills the "is this a project?" fear; different inventory pool from static | **All**, incl. Reels/Stories | **Gated — needs a unit in hand** |
| **F** | **Hand-at-the-vent POV photo** | Authorless object-discovery — the B17C1 register | All | Low |

### A — Two-thermometer demonstration ⭐

Two identical cheap thermometers, one in a hallway, one in the problem bedroom, both readable, several degrees apart. Shot like someone documenting a grievance, not like an ad.

**Why it's the strongest format available to us:** it makes the argument without a single word, it is true, it is checkable by the viewer in their own house, and it is the exact frame nobody in the category is running. It also carries in every placement.

No product in frame. No offer terms in the image (standing rule).

### B — Duct-run diagram

A simple cross-section: the air handler, the run, the branches, the last room getting a trickle. Hand-drawn register, not corporate infographic.

**Why:** the market is **Stage 2 with low awareness** and the required response is **New Information** — the clearest explanation wins. A diagram is the cheapest possible explanation to consume, and unlike tallow's whiteboard the thing being drawn is a real spatial process.

### C — Forum-post screenshot

The account's own evidence: the **attribution lever** — the same mechanism sentence produced 12 purchases quoted from a named person with nothing to gain and 0 across ~1,002 impressions asserted by the advertiser. Reddit-style formats also outperformed text-message formats on tallow (text-message cells were killed on CTR and CPC).

Fits this product unusually well, because the research came from exactly these threads and the language is already ours.

### D — Native long-form

Still in the portfolio — it is the only format that can carry the permission shift, the mechanism, the fan pre-empt and the no-contractor close in sequence. That is its job: **discovering which angle argument lands.** It is not the delivery-cost play, and it should not be the majority of the account.

**Two cells maximum in any batch.**

### E — Install POV video

Twenty seconds, first person: unscrew the old cover, drop this one in, plug it in. No voiceover needed.

**Why it matters:** it competes in a different inventory pool from static natives, so it is the least familiarity-taxed thing we can make. It also answers "is this a project?" better than any static can.

**Blocked until a unit is in hand.** That is the strongest single argument for buying one at retail: it unlocks the format with the best long-term delivery economics, plus real photography for the PDP and the install card.

### F — Hand-at-the-vent POV

The B17C1 register: an authorless object in a domestic scene, shot like a real phone photo. Hand held flat against a vent, room slightly out of focus.

## 4. Batch 1 test design

**Hold the angle constant. Vary the format.**

This is the opposite of instinct — natives are the natural angle-discovery vehicle — but delivery cost is the account's known killer, and the account has already established that **creative is the biggest CPM lever**. Angle discovery is worthless if we cannot afford the clicks to run it. So batch 1 answers *what can we afford to deliver*, and batch 2 answers *which argument converts* inside the format that won.

**Fixed variables:** SA1 Angle 1 — *"It isn't you, and it isn't broken — the air is being made, it just isn't arriving."* Same offer, same LP, ABO, $12.50/day per cell.

| Cell | Format | Hook |
|---|---|---|
| B1C1 | **A** — two-thermometer demonstration | Same house. Same afternoon. |
| B1C2 | **B** — duct-run diagram | Why the last room on the run never cools |
| B1C3 | **C** — forum-post screenshot | Someone else's answer, not ours |
| B1C4 | **D** — native long-form | Your AC isn't broken. The cold air just isn't reaching that room. |

Four cells, $50/day, inside the $600 cap with room for a batch 2.

**What each result means:**
- If A or B wins on CPM *and* converts → the demonstrative register is the account's home and natives become the minority format.
- If D wins outright → the argument needs length, and the image-carried formats need stronger hooks rather than abandonment.
- If C wins → attribution is the lever again, as it was on tallow, and that should shape everything.
- If nothing clears the CAC read at ~$150 → stop, per the kill rules. Do not extend.

**Batch 2:** winning format held constant, angle varied — SA1 Angle 2 and 3, plus SA2 Angle 1 (*"Fine at bedtime. Wrong by 2am."*).

---

## 5. Standing rules for every cell

1. **No offer terms in any image.** No price, guarantee, shipping, discount or CTA — not as overlay, not as diegetic text. Carried from tallow, where a guarantee line on the creative made an ad read as an ad immediately.
2. **Real branding stays.** Never strip labels or brand marks from real props.
3. **Never generate the product.** Nano Banana does scenes; product imagery is real photography only (`Offer_Structure.md` §4b).
4. **Claim discipline.** Temperature, degrees, airflow, comfort, sleep. Never health, safety, risk, air quality, allergens or filtration.
5. **Every cell states its sub-avatar, angle, awareness level and format role** in its file.

---

## 6. Seasonality — read before scheduling

The hot-room version of this desire is going into its off-season. The unit has a **winter mode**, and "one room freezing in winter" is the same avatar in the other half of the year.

**Close the cold-side research gap before batch 1 ships if the calendar has turned.** The formats above are season-agnostic — two thermometers make the same argument in January, the duct diagram is unchanged — but the *hooks* and the scene imagery are not, and running summer hooks into a fall audience would be a self-inflicted wound of exactly the kind the tallow post-mortem warns about.
