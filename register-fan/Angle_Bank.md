## SUB-AVATAR #1: The Fan Runner ⭐ STRONGEST

*Runs a fan in that room. The fan moves air but never changes the temperature.*

### Angle 1: Warms The Room Instead Of Moving Cold Air Around It ⭐ **TEST THIS FIRST**
**Reason to Buy:** The fan you already run can only move the air that's in the room — and that air is cold. This pulls warm air in from the duct instead.
**Hook:** *"This isn't a fan for the room. It's a fan for the vent."*
*Inspired by: "This is a meal. Not a protein shake" — product redefinition*

### Angle 2: Makes The Fan Unnecessary
**Reason to Buy:** Once warm air actually reaches the room, you stop needing to push air around to make it bearable.
**Hook:** *"The fan comes out. The room stays warm."*
*Inspired by: "This is bread. This is breakfast" — this does X, this does Y*

### Angle 3: Gives The Room Its Own Temperature
**Reason to Buy:** Instead of switching a fan on and off all day, you set a number for that room and it holds it.
**Hook:** *"Set it once. Stop fiddling with that room."*
*Inspired by: "5 minutes to install. No more snapping" — install speed + behaviour elimination*

## SUB-AVATAR #2: Checking Someone Else's Room ⭐ SECOND STRONGEST

*Checks the room at night. Buys blankets and warmer sleepwear instead of warming the room. Guilt.*
**⭐ The elevation play — Comfort → Relationships is the only urgency upgrade available to us.**

### Angle 1: Stops You Checking That Room At Night ⭐
**Reason to Buy:** The room holds a set temperature on its own, so the trip down the hall before bed stops being necessary.
**Hook:** *"Set the room. Stop checking it."*
*Inspired by: "5 minutes to install. No more snapping" — behaviour elimination*

### Angle 2: Changes The Room Instead Of Adding Another Layer
**Reason to Buy:** A thicker duvet, a warmer sleepsuit and a second blanket all manage a cold room. None of them warms it.
**Hook:** *"A thicker duvet doesn't make the room warmer."*
*Inspired by: "This is bread. This is breakfast" — this does X, this does Y*

### Angle 3: Ends The Coldest-Room-In-The-House Problem
**Reason to Buy:** Their room is the one you worry about because it's last on the duct run. Fix the delivery and it stops being the exception.
**Hook:** *"The coldest room in the house is the one they sleep in."*
*Inspired by: "Moms of 15 year olds" — direct avatar call-out*

> 🚨 **Strictest claim rules apply to this whole sub-avatar.** Temperature, the monitor, waking, not having to check. **Never** SIDS, safe sleep, risk, danger, hazard, or any health framing.

## SUB-AVATAR #3: The Nightly Heater ⭐ THIRD

*Plugs in a space heater or oil radiator every night, October to April.*

### Angle 1: Stops You Heating One Room Twice ⭐
**Reason to Buy:** The furnace is already paying to heat that room — the air just isn't arriving. Fix the delivery and the second appliance isn't needed.
**Hook:** *"You're heating that room twice."*
*Inspired by: "This is bread. This is breakfast"*

### Angle 2: Puts The Space Heater Away
**Reason to Buy:** Warm air actually reaching the room means you stop dragging a heater out every October.
**Hook:** *"The heater goes back in the cupboard."*
*Inspired by: "14+ Hours. No Swelling" — specific outcome elimination*

### Angle 3: Stops The Second Heating Bill ⚠️ *inferred, not quoted*
**Reason to Buy:** A heater run nightly through winter is a real line on the bill, on top of the heating you already pay for.
**Hook:** *"Two bills to heat one room."*
*Inspired by: "This is bread. This is breakfast"*

## SUB-AVATAR #4: The Door Closer

*Keeps the door shut and treats the room as lost. Now worries the sealed cold room is damaging the house.*

### Angle 1: Makes The Room You Shut Off Usable Again ⭐
**Reason to Buy:** You closed that door because the room was cold, not because you didn't need the room.
**Hook:** *"It's a spare room because it's cold. Not the other way round."*
*Inspired by: "This is bread. This is breakfast"*

### Angle 2: Stops You Losing A Room Every Winter
**Reason to Buy:** Every winter that room comes off the map for five months. Warm air reaching it puts it back.
**Hook:** *"You lose that room five months a year."*
*Inspired by: "Drains double chin in 7 days" — timeline framing*

### Angle 3: Lets The Door Stay Open
**Reason to Buy:** Shutting the door keeps the cold in the room. Warm air arriving means it doesn't have to be shut.
**Hook:** *"The door you keep shut."*
*Inspired by: "Moms of 15 year olds" — direct call-out of a behaviour*

## SUB-AVATAR #5: The Renter

*Asked the landlord. Got deflected. Bought a heater instead.*

### Angle 1: Control Of Your Room Without Touching The Building's System ⭐
**Reason to Buy:** The thermostat isn't yours and the landlord isn't calling back — this changes one room without changing anything that belongs to them.
**Hook:** *"The thermostat isn't yours. This room can be."*
*Inspired by: "This is bread. This is breakfast"*

### Angle 2: Comes Out Exactly How It Went In
**Reason to Buy:** Two screws and an outlet, and the original cover goes back on when you leave.
**Hook:** *"Goes back exactly how you found it."*
*Inspired by: "5 minutes to install. No more snapping"*

### Angle 3: Stops You Waiting On The Landlord
**Reason to Buy:** You asked. Nothing happened. This doesn't need them.
**Hook:** *"You asked in October. It's December."*
*Inspired by: "Drains double chin in 7 days" — timeline*

**⚠️ Gate:** confirm the unit is reversible and needs only a standard outlet before spending here.

## SUB-AVATAR #6: The Quoted ⚠️ MOF / PDP ONLY — NOT A TOF LEAD

*Called a contractor, got a quote, didn't proceed.*

### Angle 1: Fixes The Room Without The Ductwork Job
**Reason to Buy:** The quote was for rebuilding the duct run. This changes what comes out of one vent.
**Hook:** *"Not $3,400."*
*Inspired by: "Don't change your life, just change lunch" — minimal change, maximum impact*

**Why it isn't TOF:** the defining behaviour — *having got a quote* — is rare, so the number is a stranger's, and the angle channels cost-avoidance rather than the comfort desire. Correct at MOF, on the PDP alternatives section, and as a beat inside a native.

# TESTING PRIORITY

**Start with Sub-Avatar #1, Angle 1** — *"This isn't a fan for the room. It's a fan for the vent."* Best evidence, aimed squarely at Solution Aware, and the reader reaches the conclusion themselves.

If it hits, run variations. If not, move to Sub-Avatar #2, Angle 1.

**Remember: one concept can carry several angles.** Lead with the strongest hook and layer the rest in body copy rather than building a separate ad for each.
