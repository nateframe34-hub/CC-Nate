# Marketing Angles & Hooks — Evenroom Vent Thermostat

**Created 2026-09-26 · Expanded 2026-09-26** to the `Origins - Angle Identifier` output spec.
**Core Avatar:** *"I want this one room to be the same temperature as the rest of the house."*

> **PRIORITY NOTE: you do NOT have to test each angle separately.** Test the #1 hook from Sub-Avatar #1 first. Supporting angles layer into body copy inside the same concept.

**30 angles across 6 sub-avatars.** The number per avatar is set by how many *distinct reasons to buy* its behaviour actually produces — not by a quota. Each is a different reason, not a rephrasing.

**Hook patterns used** (from the Angle Identifier's winning-ad set): *"14+ Hours. No Swelling"* — timeline / outcome elimination · *"This is a meal. Not a protein shake"* — product redefinition · *"This is bread. This is breakfast"* — this does X, this does Y · *"Don't change your life, just change lunch"* — minimal change, maximum impact · *"5 minutes to install. No more snapping"* — install speed + behaviour elimination · *"Drains double chin in 7 days"* — dual timeline · *"Moms of 15 year olds"* — direct avatar call-out.

## The four rules that apply here — and only one of them is compliance

**Founder review 2026-09-26: this category is not compliance-sensitive.** Meta's restricted categories cover health, finance, housing, employment and social issues. A vent cover is in none of them, and everyday behaviours ("your spare room is cold") are not protected attributes. **Tallow was a genuinely restricted category; this is not, and the posture should not be carried over.**

I had bundled four different kinds of rule under one heading. Separating them, because three of them still stand for reasons that have nothing to do with policy:

| Rule | What it actually is | Still applies? |
|---|---|---|
| **Outcomes stay partial** | **Truth, and the returns that follow from overclaiming.** The honest result is "much more comfortable," not "fixed" | ✅ **Yes** |
| **No noise claims** | **Founder call, returns-driven.** The incumbent's loudest complaint is *"marketed as quiet but it is too loud"*, and one buyer unhooked a working fan over an unfamiliar sound | ✅ **Yes** |
| **No competitor or category name** | **Funnel strategy** (§9a-i) — never make them Product Aware and hand them a search term | ✅ **Yes, at TOF** |
| ~~Health/safety framing~~ | Was treated as compliance. Mostly it isn't | ⚠️ **Relaxed — see below** |

**On the nursery material specifically:** the reason not to write *"keep your baby safe"* is not that Meta would reject it. It's that **a vent fan does not make anyone safe, so it isn't true**, and a claim like that sets an expectation the product cannot meet. Temperature, the monitor reading, waking, not having to check — all fine, and now on ordinary judgement rather than a policy ban.

## SUB-AVATAR #1: The Fan Runner ⭐ STRONGEST — 5 angles

*Runs a fan in that room. It moves air; it never changes the temperature.*

### Angle 1: Warms The Room Instead Of Moving Cold Air Around It ⭐ **TEST FIRST**
**Reason to Buy:** The fan you already run can only move the air that's in the room — and that air is cold. This pulls warm air in from the duct instead.
**Hook:** *"This isn't a fan for the room. It's a fan for the vent."*
*— product redefinition*

### Angle 2: Makes The Fan Unnecessary
**Reason to Buy:** Once warm air actually reaches the room, you stop needing to push air around to make it bearable.
**Hook:** *"The fan comes out. The room stays warm."*
*— this does X, this does Y*

### Angle 3: Gives The Room Its Own Temperature
**Reason to Buy:** Instead of switching a fan on and off all day, you set a number for that room and it holds it.
**Hook:** *"Set it once. Stop fiddling with that room."*
*— install speed + behaviour elimination*

### Angle 4: Does Something A Fan Can't Do In Winter ⭐
**Reason to Buy:** A fan is useless in a cold room — there's no cool air to move and nothing to evaporate. This is the one that works in the half of the year a fan can't help with at all.
**Hook:** *"A fan is no use to you in January."*
*— this does X, this does Y*

### Angle 5: Warms The Room, Not Just The Spot In Front Of It
**Reason to Buy:** A fan only helps while it's pointed at you. This changes the temperature of the room, so it's still comfortable when you move.
**Hook:** *"You have to sit in front of a fan. You don't have to sit in front of this."*
*— this does X, this does Y*

## SUB-AVATAR #2: Checking Someone Else's Room ⭐ SECOND — 5 angles

*Checks the room at night. Buys blankets and warmer sleepwear instead of warming the room. Guilt.*
**⭐ The elevation play — Comfort → Relationships is the only urgency upgrade open to us.**

### Angle 1: Stops You Checking That Room At Night ⭐
**Reason to Buy:** The room holds a set temperature on its own, so the trip down the hall before bed stops being necessary.
**Hook:** *"Set the room. Stop checking it."*
*— behaviour elimination*

### Angle 2: Changes The Room Instead Of Adding Another Layer
**Reason to Buy:** A thicker duvet, a warmer sleepsuit and a second blanket all manage a cold room. None of them warms it.
**Hook:** *"A thicker duvet doesn't make the room warmer."*
*— this does X, this does Y*

### Angle 3: Ends The Coldest-Room-In-The-House Problem
**Reason to Buy:** Their room is the one you worry about because it's last on the duct run. Fix the delivery and it stops being the exception.
**Hook:** *"The coldest room in the house is the one they sleep in."*
*— direct avatar call-out*

### Angle 4: Lets Them Sleep In Their Own Room ⭐
**Reason to Buy:** If that room's temperature is the reason someone's sleeping elsewhere, changing the room gives it back.
**Hook:** *"They have a room. It's just too cold to use."*
*— this does X, this does Y*

### Angle 5: The Monitor Stops Reading Low
**Reason to Buy:** You already know the number in that room, because you check it. This is what changes the number.
**Hook:** *"You know exactly what that room reads at 2am."*
*— direct avatar call-out*

> 🚨 **Strictest claim rules in the project apply to this entire sub-avatar.** Temperature, the monitor, waking, sleeping through, not having to check. **Never** SIDS, safe sleep, risk, danger, hazard, or any health framing. We sell the not-worrying, never the thing they're worried about.

## SUB-AVATAR #6: The Quoted ⚠️ MOF / PDP ONLY — 3 angles

*Called a contractor, got a quote, didn't proceed.*

### Angle 1: Fixes The Room Without The Ductwork Job
**Reason to Buy:** The quote was for rebuilding the duct run. This changes what comes out of one vent.
**Hook:** *"Not $3,400."*
*— minimal change, maximum impact*

### Angle 2: Try The Cheap Thing Before The Expensive One ⭐
**Reason to Buy:** If the cheap fix closes the gap, the quote was never necessary. If it doesn't, you've lost very little finding out.
**Hook:** *"Try the $89 thing first."*
*— minimal change, maximum impact*

### Angle 3: Tells You Whether It's A Delivery Problem At All
**Reason to Buy:** If boosting the vent changes the room, the problem was delivery. If it doesn't, you know the quote is worth taking seriously.
**Hook:** *"Find out what it actually is, for $89."*
*— this does X, this does Y*

**Why none of these is a TOF lead:** the defining behaviour — *having got a quote* — is rare, so the number is a stranger's, and the angle channels cost-avoidance rather than the comfort desire. Correct at MOF, on the PDP alternatives section, and as a beat inside a native.
